# adni-design-operating-characteristics

## Why

The second case study, ADNI serum metabolomics (`adni_sexdiff`, report `reports/motco-adni-2026-11-02.md`), ran its primary `motco analyze` at n = 1,501 (sex × stage cells F 237 / 310 / 125, M 223 / 448 / 158), two blocks (Biocrates p180, 133 metabolites; Nightingale NMR, 165), five covariates in six design columns (`age`, `education`, `apoe4[1]`, `apoe4[2]`, `phase[ADNI2GO]`, `statin[1]`), the rank floor of 2 and net displacement, at a held-out CV stage AUROC of 0.600 and a CV-selected rank of 8. Its covariate-blind basis follows the p180 run offset: the first latent component correlates 0.54 with `phase`, and the block's own first principal component 0.73. The primary rejects nothing (`delta` p 0.112, `angle` 0.688, `shape` 0.711), and across its CV-selected sensitivity analyses `delta` sits at 0.045–0.19. Its binding reading rule 3 says, verbatim: "`angle` and `shape` non-rejections are uninformative: MOTCO's operating characteristics were measured at n = 80 on a structural analogue (InterSIM blocks, 498 features), and no power statement exists at this design (n = 1501, 298 metabolites, six design columns)." That is true. Every size and power measurement MOTCO has made sits at n ≤ 80 with a weak stage signal (the SEA-AD configuration), or at n ≤ 1,000 with a saturated one (the design-guidance profiles, AUROC 1.000), or at n = 200 with a weak one and no covariates (the design-guidance δ arm, where no statistic had power). None covers a large n with a weak stage signal, categorical covariate columns and a batch-like nuisance the basis follows. Roadmap Next changes item 9 names this check, and item 10, the Phase 6 exit review, reads its result; so it is the last measurement before Phase 6 can close.

## What Changes

- **A check at the ADNI design.** A new script, `scripts/adni_design_check.py`, composes the case-study construction (`scripts/case_study_type_i_check.py` and the D10 construction of `scripts/covariate_type_i_check.py`, imported not copied) at ADNI's cell table `[[237, 310, 125], [223, 448, 158]]` (n = 1,501), with two blocks (methylation + expression, the Phase 6 layers), a weakened per-omic stage effect δ, and six covariate design columns whose kinds match ADNI's:
  - `z`, the D10 numeric covariate confounded with group × stage and injected into the features at `s_z` (ADNI's `age`-like column);
  - the D10 three-level categorical covariate, drawn from the same linear predictor, **declared** as a factor (two drop-first columns, ADNI's `apoe4[1]`/`apoe4[2]`) and not injected (`s_c = 0`);
  - **`phase`**, a new binary covariate drawn per sex × stage cell at ADNI's recorded ADNI2GO proportions (F 0.603 / 0.623 / 0.424, M 0.561 / 0.507 / 0.481) and injected as a dense per-feature offset into the **expression block only** (131 features, standing for the 133 run-affected p180 metabolites) at a strength `s_p`, so the covariate-blind basis follows it as ADNI's does;
  - two pure-noise numeric covariates (`education`/`statin`-like).
- **Calibration before paper grade, recorded.** A `calibrate` subcommand evaluates anchor units in two arms (`adjusted`: the six columns in the trajectory model, the primary as issued; `unadjusted`: descriptive) and marks three criteria: the `adjusted` arm's mean held-out CV AUROC inside a declared band around ADNI's (0.55–0.65; ADNI reads 0.580–0.616 across its CV-selected analyses), its median first-component |Spearman| with `phase` at or above a threshold (0.4; ADNI reads 0.54), and the `unadjusted` arm rejecting above α on at least one statistic (the construction confounds). The knobs are δ, `s_z` and `s_p`; every iteration is appended to `calibration.json`. The selected-rank distribution (ADNI 8), the expression block's PC1 |corr| with `phase` (ADNI's p180 0.73), the realized median |d| of the offset (ADNI 1.12), the eigengap (0.74) and the `angle` null q95 (35°) are recorded beside the criteria, descriptively.
- **One paper-grade profile: Type I and power at the calibrated strength, in one run.** Cells are the zero-effect anchor, the `translation` negative control at e = 1.0, magnitude at e ∈ {0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 1.0}, and orientation and shape at e ∈ {0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0}: 14 cells × 500 replicates × 999 permutations, each unit one `adjusted` analysis through `motco.analysis.analyze_trajectories` (7,000 analyses). Seeds derive from a fresh base seed (2032) and the replicate index only, so every cell of a replicate shares its baseline draw, covariates and permutation draws. Each record carries the p-values, statistics, selected rank, CV AUROC, the first component's correlation with `phase` and `z`, the null summary and the eigengap.
- **A predeclared decision.** A pure function reads the merged records and writes `adni_design_decision.json`:
  - *size*: `delta`, `angle` and `shape` at the anchor and the translation control against the one-sided bound α + 2·√(α(1 − α)/R) (six readings, the case-study check's rule);
  - *power*: for each mode's target statistic (magnitude/`delta`, orientation/`angle`, shape/`shape`) the rate at every effect with its binomial SE and the selected-rank distribution; `informative_from` is the smallest effect at which the rate reaches the 0.80 floor of the Phase 4–6 gates, or null; off-target rates are reported descriptively;
  - *verdict*: `power_statement` when size holds and at least one statistic has an informative effect; `non_rejections_uninformative` when size holds and none does; `diagnose_design_excess` when a size reading exceeds, naming it, in which case no power statement is issued;
  - a per-statistic measured statement that `adni_sexdiff` can adopt in place of rule 3, and the consequence.
- **Run, report and consequence.** A SLURM array template, local smoke and calibration with a timing at 999 permutations, an approval gate on the calibrated settings and the shard plan, the resumable sharded run on `ing` from a clean worktree, a dated report under `docs/reports/` and the results under `results/`. `docs/roadmap.md` marks item 9 done and records the verdict in the Phase 6 section (unblocking item 10); `docs/api/analysis.md` and `AGENTS.md` gain the check beside the SEA-AD ones. `adni_sexdiff` replaces or confirms rule 3 in its own change.
- **Out of scope.** No change under `src/motco/`: every record field the check reads exists in 0.13.0, and without the opt-ins the analysis is byte-identical to the 0.10.0 the case study pinned, so there is no release. No `latent_stage` arm (ADNI's sensitivity arm; it would double the cost and the SEA-AD diagnosis left it without a MOTCO change), no residual-correction arm (κ = √(1501/1491) ≈ 1.003 is immaterial, and the primary is uncorrected), no forced-rank, export-variant or CN / LMCI / AD (n = 1,130) arms, whose characteristics stay inferred. The blocks are InterSIM omic analogues, not metabolomics, so the structural-analogue limitation is wider than for SEA-AD and the report carries it. The Phase 6 exit review (item 10) is its own change.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `adni-design-validation`: the size and power check of the real-data analysis at the ADNI case study's design — n = 1,501 in ADNI's cells, two blocks, six covariate design columns of ADNI's kinds including a phase-like binary nuisance injected into one block, a weak stage signal and the rank floor — with its construction and calibration criteria, the single-arm units on matched seeds, the size rule over both controls, the power floor rule per statistic, the decision artifact and the measured statement it carries for the case study.

### Modified Capabilities

None. `case-study-configuration-validation`, `covariate-adjustment-type-i` and `real-data-trajectory-analysis` keep their requirements; the new capability composes the first two's constructions and states its own.

## Impact

- **Code:** new `scripts/adni_design_check.py` (imports `case_study_type_i_check` and `covariate_type_i_check` for the construction, settings hash, sharding, resume and the anchor bound) and `scripts/adni_design_array.sbatch` (modelled on `scripts/corrected_permutation_array.sbatch`). No change under `src/motco/`.
- **Tests:** new `tests/test_adni_design_check.py` (the ADNI table and its six declared columns; the phase draw's cell proportions and its expression-only injection; reproducibility and shared seeds across cells; resume, merge and the refused resume; the calibration criteria; the pure decision's three verdicts, the informative-effect rule and the indicative flag), on a small registered test table so the run-path tests stay fast, modelled on `tests/test_corrected_permutation_check.py`.
- **Results and docs:** `results/adni-design-<date>/` (settings, calibration, PROVENANCE, rates, replicates and decision; `records/` and any table over about 1 MB stay on `ing`), `docs/reports/adni-design-<date>.md`, `docs/roadmap.md` (item 9, the Phase 6 section), `docs/api/analysis.md` (a validation paragraph beside the case-study ones), `AGENTS.md` (the `analysis.py` paragraph).
- **Downstream:** `adni_sexdiff` reads the decision. `power_statement` replaces its reading rule 3 with the measured statement; `non_rejections_uninformative` confirms it with a measurement; `diagnose_design_excess` names what to diagnose. Each is its own change there. Item 10 reads the verdict.
- **Compute:** 7,000 analyses at n = 1,501 and 498 features. The ADNI primary itself took 26 s at 9,999 permutations and rank 8 on the workstation (298 features), so a unit at 999 permutations is expected at 30–45 s there and 80–120 s on an `ing` core: about 150–230 core-hours, re-timed at smoke before the approval gate, with a reduced replicate count as the gate's option. Calibration runs locally (anchor units, R = 20, two arms per iteration).
