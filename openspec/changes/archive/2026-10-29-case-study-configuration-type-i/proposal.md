# case-study-configuration-type-i

## Why

The SEA-AD case study's primary analysis (`sead_sexdiff`, re-issued under MOTCO 0.10.0 on 2026-10-26) runs `motco analyze` at the rank floor of 2 with six numeric donor covariates in the trajectory model (`age_at_death`, `pmi`, `rin`, `years_education`, `log10_n_rna`, `log10_n_atac`; `missing_covariates="error"`) on the covariate-blind basis, at a held-out stage AUROC of 0.64–0.70 where that basis spends its first component on a donor library-quality axis (|Spearman| 0.84). No simulation has measured size at that configuration. The covariate Type I check (0.8.0, `covariates_validated`) ran the unfloored rank rule at a saturated stage signal (AUROC 1.000) with three design columns and a nuisance the basis did not follow (first-component |ρ| 0.64), and its report says in so many words that more columns at n = 80 and a nuisance larger than the stage signal are outside it. The rank-floor check (0.10.0, `adopt_floor`) ran without covariates. The latent-adjustment Type I check (0.9.0) measured, descriptively, the one arm that is the case study's analysis type — model adjustment alone on a basis whose first component is the nuisance — and it rejected `shape` at 0.078 against the 0.0695 bound it would have faced, at floor 1 and eight covariates. The case study's primary therefore rests on three checks none of which covers it, with a documented warning sign on `shape`. The design-guidance slice report (2026-10-29) names this check as follow-up (c); it is the last operating characteristic the paper's case-study section cites without a measurement.

## What Changes

- **A check at the case study's configuration.** A new script, `scripts/case_study_type_i_check.py`, composes the two existing constructions it sits between: the covariate Type I construction (D10, `scripts/covariate_type_i_check.py`, imported not copied: a numeric covariate `z` confounded with group, stage and their interaction and injected into the features along fixed directions) at the weakened stage signal of the rank-floor check (the per-omic `delta_*` as a setting, `p_dmp` at the Phase 6 value 0.1), with five pure-noise numeric covariates declared beside `z` so the trajectory model has six numeric design columns, as the case study's does. No categorical covariate is declared (the case study enters none; the D10 categorical injection strength is 0). Cells are the zero-effect anchor and the `translation` negative control at e = 1.0, the controls of the rank-floor check. Seeds depend on the replicate index only, so the two cells share the baseline draw, the covariates and the permutation draws.
- **Three arms per unit on the same data, CV `random_state` and RRPP seed,** all at the rank floor of 2 with the net-displacement estimator: `adjusted` (the six covariates in the trajectory model only — the case study's primary analysis; judged), `unadjusted` (no covariates; descriptive evidence that the construction confounds) and `latent_stage` (the six covariates with `covariate_latent_adjustment="stage"` — the case study's sensitivity arm and the fallback if the primary fails). Each arm records its p-values, selected rank, mean held-out CV stage AUROC, the Spearman correlation of its first component with `z`, its null summary and any analysis error (recorded as unavailable).
- **Calibration before paper grade, recorded.** A smoke run places the construction in the case study's regime before any paper-grade run: the `adjusted` arm's mean held-out CV AUROC falls inside a declared band around the SEA-AD cohorts' (0.60–0.75; the cohorts read 0.636–0.697), the `unadjusted` arm's median first-component |ρ| with `z` is at least 0.8 (the real basis reads 0.84), and the `unadjusted` arm rejects above α on at least one statistic (the construction confounds, so the gate is not vacuous). The per-omic δ and the injection strength `s_z` are tuned to meet the three criteria and every iteration is appended to `calibration.json`, as in the rank-floor check.
- **A predeclared decision.** A pure function reads the merged records and applies the one-sided anchor bound `α + k·√(α(1 − α)/R)` at k = 2, R the available replicates, to `delta`, `angle` and `shape` at both controls: `primary_validated` when the `adjusted` arm holds every statistic at both cells; otherwise `adopt_latent_stage` when the `latent_stage` arm holds every statistic at both cells; otherwise `diagnose_configuration_excess` naming the exceeding arm, cell and statistic. The `unadjusted` arm never enters. If the paper-grade run uses more than one stage-signal strength, the decision is the conjunction over the runs, as the rank-floor check's was.
- **Run, report and consequence.** A SLURM array template, a resumable sharded paper-grade run at 500 replicates × 999 permutations on `ing` after the user approves the calibrated settings, a dated report under `docs/reports/` and the results directory under `results/`. The report states the consequence for the case study: `primary_validated` keeps its primary as issued; `adopt_latent_stage` means the primary must move to the `stage` latent adjustment (a change in `sead_sexdiff`, not here); `diagnose_configuration_excess` names what to diagnose. `docs/roadmap.md` records the decision under Next changes item 1 (the case study) and marks item 7(c) done; `docs/api/analysis.md` and `AGENTS.md` gain the check beside the three it composes.
- **Out of scope.** No change to `src/motco/`: the analysis entry point, the floor, the covariate and latent-adjustment machinery and every record field the check reads exist in 0.12.0, so there is no release (a patch, 0.12.1, only if implementation finds a record field missing). No power claim at this configuration (the design-guidance pilot's δ arm measured none at δ ≤ 0.1). No change to the case-study repository. The microglia cohort's cell sizes (n = 76) are not run; the check uses the astrocyte `cohort_30` sizes `[[11, 10, 28], [9, 10, 12]]` that every Phase 6 check uses, and the report carries that as a limitation.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `case-study-configuration-validation`: the size check of the real-data analysis at the case study's configuration — rank floor 2, six numeric covariate design columns, a weak stage signal and a nuisance covariate the covariate-blind basis follows — with its construction and calibration criteria, the three matched arms, the anchor-bound rule over both controls, the decision artifact and the consequence it carries for the case study.

### Modified Capabilities

None. `covariate-adjustment-type-i`, `rank-floor-validation` and `latent-adjustment-validation` keep their requirements; the new capability composes their constructions and states its own.

## Impact

- **Code:** new `scripts/case_study_type_i_check.py` (imports `covariate_type_i_check` for the construction, settings hash, sharding and resume helpers) and `scripts/case_study_type_i_array.sbatch` (modelled on `scripts/rank_floor_array.sbatch`). No change under `src/motco/`.
- **Tests:** new `tests/test_case_study_type_i_check.py` (reproducibility, three arms on shared data with the `adjusted`/`unadjusted` latent spaces identical and `latent_stage`'s different, six declared design columns, resume, merge, calibration criteria, the pure decision rule's three outcomes and the conjunction), modelled on `tests/test_rank_floor_check.py`.
- **Results and docs:** `results/case-study-type-i-<date>/` (settings, calibration, PROVENANCE, rates, replicates and decision; the merged JSONL stays on `ing`), `docs/reports/case-study-type-i-<date>.md`, `docs/roadmap.md` (Next changes items 1 and 7), `docs/api/analysis.md` (a validation paragraph beside the covariate and latent-adjustment ones), `AGENTS.md` (the `analysis.py` paragraph).
- **Downstream:** `sead_sexdiff` reads the decision. `primary_validated` changes nothing there; `adopt_latent_stage` requires its own re-issue change there.
- **Compute:** about 3,000 analyses per stage-signal strength (2 cells × 3 arms × 500 replicates) at 18–30 s each on an `ing` EPYC core, so 15–25 core-hours per strength.
