# minimum-latent-rank

## Why

The production latent space is a stage-supervised PLS-DA basis whose rank is chosen by double cross-validation over the candidates `1 … max_components − 1`. Nothing stops the rule from choosing rank 1, and in a one-dimensional space the trajectory statistics degenerate: two net-displacement (or PC1) vectors on a line are parallel or antiparallel, so `angle` is 0° or 180° by construction and its RRPP null is a two-point distribution; the Procrustes `shape` distance of collinear stage means carries no bend. `delta` alone survives. Both SEA-AD cohorts in [Omnia-Lab-UC/sead_sexdiff](https://github.com/Omnia-Lab-UC/sead_sexdiff) selected rank 1 (`docs/roadmap.md`, Next changes, item 4), so their orientation and shape results were uninterpretable, while the diagnostic `forced_components` override already refuses anything below 2. The 0.9.0 latent-adjustment power comparison recorded the selected rank per unit so this change would have its evidence: no Phase 6 unit selected rank 1, so the rank-1 selection is a small-n, weak-signal, real-data phenomenon and the floor must be validated on a construction that reproduces it.

## What Changes

- **A rank floor of 2 in the production latent space.** `fit_pls_latent_space` (the core shared by the simulation harness and `motco analyze`) restricts the cross-validation candidates to `min_components … max_components − 1` with `min_components = 2` by default: the inner argmax, the per-fold selections, the modal rank and the final pooled fit all live at rank ≥ 2. `max_components` must exceed the floor; it is clamped to `[min_components + 1, min(n_features, n_samples)]` and an infeasible sample is an error naming the counts. The forced-rank path keeps its own `[2, max]` range and still skips cross-validation.
- **The primitive gains the knob with the old default.** `plsda_doubleCV` gains keyword-only `min_components: int = 1` (candidates `min_components … max_components − 1`; `1 ≤ min_components < max_components` or a `ValueError`). The default reproduces the committed `plsda_doublecv_identity.json` byte for byte. `motco plsr`, the generic PLS-DA cross-validation subcommand, exposes `--min-components` with the primitive's default of 1: its outputs do not change. The floor of 2 is the trajectory measurement's rule, not the generic tool's.
- **A hash-omitted spelling of the old floor, as for the orientation estimator.** `SimulationEvaluationParams.min_components: int | None = 2`; `1` normalizes to `None`, the spelling omitted from the parameter signature, the cell id and `runtime_metadata` (`grid._SIGNATURE_OMIT_WHEN_NONE`), so floor-1 cell ids, signatures, matched seeds and records equal those produced before the field existed. The default `2` is hashed and recorded. The latent metadata's `integration_params` gains `min_components` only when the floor is above 1. `evaluation.min_components` is a study-config field (`null` names the evaluator's default, `1` the old floor) and a contracted design axis, so a study can measure both rules on the same data.
- **Every committed study config pins the old floor.** Each config under `examples/trajectory_power_study/` names `"min_components": 1`, as it names `"pc1"`, so `tests/test_committed_config_identity.py` and the dated reports keep reproducing. A pin test enforces that every committed config names the floor deliberately. The historical diagnostics that pin `"pc1"` to reproduce a dated report (`scripts/latent_rank_probe.py`, `scripts/orientation_measurement_probe.py`, `realized_geometry_study`, `specificity`, the byte-identity fixture captures) pin `min_components=1` for the same reason.
- **`motco analyze` adopts the floor.** `TrajectoryAnalysisParams.min_components: int | None = 2` (CLI `--min-components`, default 2), validated, with `1` normalized to `None` and omitted from `to_dict()` while `None`, so a floor-1 analysis reproduces every 0.9.0 record and CSV byte for byte (the existing identity fixtures, now run with `min_components=1`). The record's `latent` block states the floor beside `selected_lv`; the printed summary names it.
- **Validation on a construction that selects rank 1.** A new script, `scripts/rank_floor_check.py`, reuses the Phase 6 small-n construction with the stage signal weakened until the unfloored rule selects rank 1 in at least half of the null replicates (calibrated on a recorded smoke run before any paper-grade run). Two arms per replicate on the same data and RRPP seed: floor 1 (the 0.9.0 rule) and floor 2. The zero-effect anchor and the `translation` control must hold the one-sided anchor bound on `delta`, `angle` and `shape` in the floor-2 arm; magnitude, orientation and shape effects at the Phase 6 levels are measured in both arms, with the rank distribution per arm and cell. The decision file, `rank_floor_decision.json`, returns `adopt_floor` or `diagnose_floor_excess`; the floor-1 arm is measured, not judged, and its `angle` null is reported as the degenerate two-point distribution it is. Acceptance rules are written before any run, and nothing is submitted to the cluster without the user's approval.
- **Documented limitation.** The floor guarantees a two-dimensional measurement space, not a second axis with signal: when the stage signal supports one component, the second is the best of the remaining candidates and `angle`'s null widens accordingly. The record's `config_spectrum` eigengap and the null summary say how resolvable orientation is in each analysis.
- **Out of scope.** No change to the orientation and shape estimators, to RRPP, to the diagnostic `pca` and `pls_within_group` methods (fixed ranks by construction), or to the generic `motco plsr` default. No re-run of any committed study: their pins make the floor invisible to them.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `rank-floor-validation`: the size and power check of the rank-2 floor against the unfloored rule on a construction calibrated to select rank 1: the construction and its calibration, the two matched arms, the anchor-bound rule, the rank distributions and the decision artifact.

### Modified Capabilities

- `plsda-nested-cv`: the inner-loop selection requirement gains the candidate floor (`min_components`, keyword-only, default 1); the public-signature requirement admits it among the keyword-only arguments whose defaults reproduce the previous output.
- `plsr-latent-space`: `motco plsr` accepts `--min-components` (default 1) and passes it to the cross-validation.
- `simulation-evaluation-harness`: the PLS integration scenario selects the rank from candidates at or above the floor, with 2 as the default; a new requirement states the floor's spelling, hash omission and recording, mirroring the net-displacement requirement; the forced-component requirement notes that the override keeps its own range.
- `real-data-trajectory-analysis`: the measurement requirement states the floor and its default; the parameters, the record and `motco analyze` gain the setting; the equivalence and identity scenarios hold at the floor of 1 and at the floor of 2 respectively.
- `trajectory-power-study`: every committed configuration names `evaluation.min_components`; the field is a contracted design axis beside the estimator and the integration method.

- **Code:** `src/motco/stats/pls.py` (`min_components` in `plsda_doubleCV`, candidate indexing by value); `src/motco/simulations/evaluation.py` (the params field with its normalization and resolved value, `fit_pls_latent_space(..., min_components=)`, the clamp of `max_components`, metadata and `runtime_metadata`); `src/motco/simulations/grid.py` (`_SIGNATURE_OMIT_WHEN_NONE`); `src/motco/simulations/study/config.py` (the field, its normalizer, the axis contract); `src/motco/analysis.py` (the params field, `to_dict()` omission, the record); `src/motco/cli.py` (`--min-components` on `plsr` and `analyze`); the historical diagnostics and scripts that pin `"pc1"`; new `scripts/rank_floor_check.py` and its sbatch array.
- **Configs and fixtures:** every `examples/trajectory_power_study/*.json` gains `"min_components": 1`; `tests/data/committed_config_identity.json`, `orientation_default_identity.json`, `plsda_doublecv_identity.json`, `pls_latent_space_identity.json`, `phase6_prechange_fixture.json`, `orientation_probe_prechange_fixture.json`, `analysis_no_covariate_identity.json` and `analysis_no_latent_adjustment_identity.json` stay as committed and keep passing under the floor-1 spelling.

- **Tests:** `tests/test_pls.py` (candidate floor, validation, default identity), `tests/test_pls_integration.py` and `tests/test_latent_adjustment.py` (rank ≥ 2 by default, the committed core fixture under floor 1, the same input selecting ≥ 2 under the floor), `tests/test_simulation_evaluation.py` (normalization, hashing, metadata), `tests/test_analysis.py`, `tests/test_analysis_cli.py` and `tests/test_analysis_identity.py`, a floor pin test beside `tests/test_orientation_default_pins.py`, study-config tests for the field and axis, and a fast test of the check script.
- **Docs:** `docs/api/analysis.md` and `docs/api/cli.md` (the floor and its limitation), `docs/api/pls.md`, `docs/roadmap.md` (Next changes item 4 resolved; the decision), the `AGENTS.md` paragraphs for `pls.py`, `evaluation.py`, `analysis.py` and `cli.py`, and a dated report under `docs/reports/`.
- **Release:** a minor version (0.10.0), as a default flip. Rollback for any caller is `min_components=1`.
