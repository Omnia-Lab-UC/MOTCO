# covariate-aware-latent-construction

## Why

`covariate-adjusted-trajectories` (0.8.0) put the declared covariates into the trajectory regression and left the PLS-DA basis covariate-blind (design D1), recording the covariate-blind basis as a risk: a covariate effect that is large and aligned with stage can make PLS spend a component on it. The SEA-AD case study in [Omnia-Lab-UC/sead_sexdiff](https://github.com/Omnia-Lab-UC/sead_sexdiff) has met exactly that risk. In both cohorts CV selects rank 1, so `angle` is 0° by construction, and the single component is a donor-level library-quality axis: Spearman −0.84 with the astrocyte nuclei's ribosomal-protein UMI fraction, +0.75 with their mitochondrial fraction, −0.77 with the ATAC TSS enrichment and fraction of fragments in peaks, −0.73 with the RNA TMM factor, against +0.49 with stage (`reports/motco-astrocyte-technical-axis-2026-10-19.md`). The six analysis covariates leave those correlations in place (partial −0.82 with the ribosomal fraction), because the adjustment, although exact within the basis, never touches which basis is chosen. The axis is PC1 of the exported RNA block, and at forced rank 2 it carries 98 % of the squared sex-difference geometry.

The case study tried the upstream fix: label-blind residualization of the blocks on library sizes and the ribosomal and mitochondrial fractions before feature selection (`reports/motco-astrocyte-feature-selection-2026-10-19.md`). It removed the axis (Spearman with the ribosomal fraction ≈ −0.05) but dropped the held-out CV AUROC from 0.63 / 0.66 to 0.41 / 0.48. The reading is Frisch–Waugh–Lovell: the features were residualized on the technical variables alone while the PLS response (stage) was not, so the stage signal collinear with those variables was removed with them. Doing it properly needs the labels, which under the scope boundary of `docs/roadmap.md` (Next changes, item 2) puts it in MOTCO: the step touches the model and the basis the null is measured in.

## What Changes

- **Opt-in covariate-aware latent construction.** `TrajectoryAnalysisParams` gains `covariate_latent_adjustment: None | "stage" | "group_stage"` (default `None`) and `motco analyze` gains `--latent-adjustment {stage,group_stage}`. When set, the pooled-standardized joint features are residualized on the declared covariates before the PLS-DA basis is fitted, with the covariate coefficients estimated conditional on the chosen design: stage indicators (`stage`) or the full group × stage indicators (`group_stage`). The residualization covariates are exactly `dataset.covariate_cols`; the setting is an error without covariates.
- **Only the basis changes.** The latent scores handed to the trajectory model and to RRPP are the raw standardized features projected onto the new basis (`X W'`). The trajectory model, the LS-means, the RRPP reduced model and the permutation scheme are those of 0.8.0 with the same covariates. This is exact: for any fixed basis the covariate-adjusted stage means in the latent space are the projected feature-space adjusted means, and, with the covariates in both RRPP models, projecting the raw or the residualized features gives identical statistics and identical permutation draws. Residualization therefore changes which basis, and which CV rank, is selected, and nothing else.
- **Leak-free cross-validation.** Inside the double CV, the residualization coefficients are estimated on each training set (at both levels) and applied to the held-out samples through their covariates alone; held-out labels are never read. The final basis uses the full-sample coefficients.
- **The pooled scale is kept.** The residualized features are not re-standardized: the residualized path fits PLS with `scale=False` on the pooled-standardized residuals, so a feature whose variance was mostly covariate is down-weighted rather than inflated. Without the setting the folds keep `scale=True`, as today.
- **Guards and record.** Every residualization fit (full sample and every CV training set) must have full column rank, or the analysis fails naming the covariates or the fold. `result.json` gains a `latent_adjustment` block, emitted only when the setting is on: the conditioning, the design columns, the residual degrees of freedom of the full fit, the smallest training-set degrees of freedom seen in CV, the feature scale, and a per-feature R² summary. The latent block names the conditioning the basis was fitted under.
- **Byte identity when absent.** With the setting absent, every output equals 0.8.0, including `record["params"]` (the new field is omitted while `None`). A fixture is captured from the 0.8.0 tree before any code change, in the manner of D8.
- **Attribution stays rejected with covariates.** Nothing changes in the attribution guard.
- **Type I check at the Phase 6 design.** A new script, `scripts/latent_adjustment_type_i_check.py`, extends the D10 construction with a nuisance effect calibrated so that the 0.8.0 covariate-blind basis spends its first component on it. Three arms per replicate: 0.8.0 (covariates in the model only), `stage`, `group_stage`. Each residualized arm must hold the one-sided anchor bound on `delta`, `angle` and `shape`; the baseline arm is reported with the calibration evidence. The decision file names the admissible conditionings and adopts `stage` unless only `group_stage` holds.
- **Power comparison against the 0.8.0 basis.** A second script, `scripts/latent_adjustment_power_comparison.py`, runs magnitude, orientation and shape effects at the Phase 6 effect levels under the same nuisance, and a second column with the covariates present but the nuisance effect at zero. The rule follows the study engine's `column_decision` convention: a gain at the top effect when the nuisance axis exists, no loss when it does not, every control within the bound, protected statistics within the pooled-SE loss tolerance. The CV-selected rank is recorded per arm and replicate. Acceptance rules are written before any run, and nothing is submitted to the cluster without the user's approval.
- **Documented limitation.** Residualizing a variable that mediates the stage effect removes real signal, and no simulation can tell a confounder from a mediator. The case study must choose and justify its covariate set in its own proposal; nothing SEA-AD-specific enters MOTCO.
- **Out of scope.** The rank-1 `angle` degeneracy (CV may select rank 1, where `angle` is 0° or 180° by construction, while `forced_components` requires ≥ 2) is a separate change; this change's power comparison records the selected rank so that change has its evidence. Covariates in the study engine, covariate × design interactions, and covariate-aware attribution stay out.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `covariate-residualized-latent-space`: the opt-in covariate-aware basis construction: residualization on the declared covariates conditional on a chosen design, leak-free cross-validation, the kept pooled scale, the raw-feature projection, the guards and the `latent_adjustment` record block.
- `latent-adjustment-validation`: the Type I check and the power comparison of the residualized basis against the 0.8.0 covariate-blind basis at the Phase 6 design, including the calibrated nuisance axis, the three-arm construction, the acceptance rules and the decision artifacts.

### Modified Capabilities

- `real-data-trajectory-analysis`: the covariate models requirement no longer states that the latent space is always built without the covariates; it is built without them unless latent adjustment is requested. The parameters gain the setting, `motco analyze` gains the flag, and the record gains the optional block. The no-setting output stays identical to 0.8.0.
- `plsda-nested-cv`: `plsda_doubleCV` gains optional keyword-only `nuisance` and `scale` arguments. With `nuisance` given, every training set residualizes its predictors on the nuisance covariates conditional on the nuisance design before fitting, and held-out predictors are residualized with the training coefficients. With `scale=False` the PLS fits do not re-standardize. The defaults preserve the existing behaviour and the existing positional contract.

## Impact

- **Code:** `src/motco/stats/pls.py` (per-fold residualizer and the `scale` knob in `plsda_doubleCV` and `_plsda_auroc`); `src/motco/simulations/evaluation.py` (`fit_pls_latent_space(..., nuisance=)`; projection of the raw features when a nuisance is given); `src/motco/stats/design.py` (a conditioning-design helper beside `build_covariate_matrix`); `src/motco/analysis.py` (the params field, its omission from `to_dict()` while `None`, the record block); `src/motco/cli.py` (`--latent-adjustment`); new `scripts/latent_adjustment_type_i_check.py`, `scripts/latent_adjustment_power_comparison.py` and their sbatch arrays.
- **Unchanged:** the stats primitives (`estimate_difference`, `RRPP`), `build_simulation_trajectory_design`, `SimulationEvaluationParams`, the study engine, every committed study config and byte-identity fixture, the `covariates` record block, the attribution guard, and `motco de`.
- **Tests:** `tests/test_analysis.py`, `tests/test_analysis_cli.py` and `tests/test_pls.py` gain residualization cases; a new identity fixture pins the no-setting path; the existing `test_matches_harness_*`, `test_analysis_identity.py` and the config identity tests must pass unchanged.
- **Docs:** `docs/api/analysis.md` (a latent-adjustment section replacing the "covariate-blind basis" limitation with the opt-in and the mediator limitation), the CLI reference, `docs/roadmap.md` (Phase 6 and a new Next changes item), the `analysis.py` paragraph in `AGENTS.md`, and a dated report per validation run under `docs/reports/`.
- **Release:** a minor version (0.9.0). sead_sexdiff pins it when its own proposal adopts a covariate set.
