# MOTCO Scientific Roadmap

## Objective

Develop MOTCO into a scientifically defensible workflow that can:

1. detect whether groups follow different multivariate molecular trajectories;
2. distinguish progression magnitude and orientation reliably;
3. treat shape with an explicitly validated interpretation; and
4. identify stable feature and pathway drivers after a global trajectory difference is detected.

The roadmap prioritizes interpretability and validation before larger simulations or additional integration methods. The evidence for each statement below is in its dated report under [`reports/`](reports/); the [measurement visibility](measurement-visibility.md) page explains what the production measurement can and cannot see.

## Current position

### Established

- Methylation is converted from B values to M values before standardization and PLS integration.
- **PLS is the production measurement space.** One pooled, stage-supervised PLS-DA fit, its rank chosen by double cross-validation with a floor of 2 (item 4), and `delta` / `angle` / `shape` measured in its score coordinates. Block weighting and sparse stage-PLS (item 17, `keep_production`) and orthonormal span coordinates (item 15, `power_loss`) were measured against it and not adopted.
- **Magnitude is specific under the `joint` construction** at paper grade: magnitude/`delta` 1.000 at the top effect, magnitude/`angle` 0.024 and magnitude/`shape` 0.000 ([addendum](reports/phase5-magnitude-remeasurement-2026-10-08.md)).
- **Shape invariance is audited and corrected.** Production `shape` removes translation, uniform scale and any orthogonal transformation; reflections are aligned away at every ambient dimension (`unify-shape-reflection-policy`, resolving audit finding F3).
- **Orientation is measured by net displacement**, the harness and `motco analyze` default since 2026-10-15. It holds size at paper grade and raises orientation/`angle` power from 0.300 to 0.648 at the Phase 6 design and from 0.840 to 0.924 at Phase 5, removing the shape → `angle` leak ([report](reports/net-displacement-power-2026-10-14.md)).
- **Given a difference inside the stage-supervised span, the tests are sensitive**: a 45° rotation and a half-step bend are rejected in 0.96–1.00 of replicates at five designs at 500 × 999 (item 13 (a), `sensitivity_established`).
- **Covariates in the trajectory model hold size** at the Phase 6 design (item 2). At the case study's configuration (six covariate columns at n = 80) the Freedman–Lane null needs the opt-in `df` residual correction, which is validated (item 8, `adopt_df`).
- **Two tests cover what the span cannot see**, each validated for size in simulation and on SEA-AD's real blocks: the split interaction test for differences outside the span (item 16, `split_validated`) and the per-feature interaction scan for sparse ones (`scan_validated`; by block, `by_block_validated`).
- **Covariate-adjusted orientation attribution** is validated on semi-synthetic data at the case study's configuration (item 11, `adjusted_attribution_validated`).
- A controlled inverse-PLS study showed that latent interventions reconstruct reproducibly into feature space, with round-trip errors near machine precision; a 45° latent orientation change reconstructed as about 42° in feature space with negligible magnitude change. Latent orientation differences are different relative combinations of stage-associated molecular patterns, usually dense loading-aligned feature changes.
- SNF is not intrinsically unsuitable for integration, but its graph-spectral geometry is not naturally aligned with MOTCO's Euclidean path-length and angle statistics.

### Not yet established

- **The feature-set surgeries are not geometrically pure, and the measurement barely sees them.** Orientation's `shape` response (0.998 at paper grade) is construction-present cross-talk of a per-omic feature permutation, present *within* each omic block; its predeclared "projection-associated" label is **refuted** ([diagnostic](reports/magnitude-construction-diagnostic-2026-09-10.md), §1 and §4), and the paper must not repeat that framing. The surgeries' orientation and shape plants keep only the random-direction share of their energy in the stage-supervised span (0.2–1.1 %), so their low power measures visibility, not sensitivity ([stage-span pilot](reports/stage-span-pilot-2026-11-04.md)).
- **The Phase 5 orientation claim is PC1's, and it is conditional.** Under PC1 at n = 1,200, ρ = 0, orientation `angle` power is 0.840 ± 0.016 at paper grade ([report](reports/phase5-paper-grade-2026-09-10.md), §5); it straddles the 0.80 floor by eigengap tercile (0.754 / 0.886 / 0.880), and it saturates at 0.84–0.88 from e = 0.25, so the strictly monotone form of the power claim is unsupported. Under PC1 the `angle` null tracks its own observed statistic (slope 0.811) and is load-bearing rather than corrigible ([report](reports/angle-null-pivotality-2026-09-01.md)). Under net displacement the eigengap no longer gates orientation power at the Phase 6 design; the CV-selected rank does, and it replaces the eigengap as the covariate reported beside any `angle` result (case studies below).
- **Cross-replicate driver stability** has no design that holds the driver set fixed, so it is not claimable.
- **Specificity of the other magnitude constructions.** The former default `all` (methylation δ alone) moves `angle` (0.276) and `shape` (0.742) far past the control bound at paper grade. Its off-target response is the geometry of concatenating one block that grew with two that did not ([diagnostic](reports/magnitude-construction-diagnostic-2026-09-10.md)); `joint` is the remedy and the default since 2026-10-09, and the historical configs pin `all`. Specificity stays unestablished for `all`, `extremes` and `shape_kind='magnitude'`, so a magnitude result must name its construction. The acceptance-target reader's default two-sided rule records `joint`'s conservative controls as not met; the opt-in `bound: "upper"` agrees with the gate.
- **Power at the Phase 6 design** (n = 80, three stages, two blocks). Only `delta` has useful power (0.542 at a 10 % joint size change, 0.956 at 25 %), and no statistic is mode-specific for orientation or shape ([small-n report](reports/phase6-small-n-2026-10-09.md)). Under net displacement orientation/`angle` reaches 0.648 at the construction maximum (0.280 when CV keeps three or fewer components, 0.996 above) and 0.084–0.178 at e ≤ 0.75, so a non-rejection of `angle` at n = 80 stays uninformative.
- **Limits of the span measurement.** The statistics are measured in sheared PLS score coordinates, which leave a path-length residual for the planted bend at e = 0.5 (−0.48 % of A's path on the SEA-AD table, −0.59 % at four stages); orthonormal coordinates remove it but lose target power (item 15), so the scores stay. The rotation's off-target `shape` rises to the per-cell bound at 45° (0.070 at n = 200, 0.086 at four stages). How much of a *real* difference the span sees is not established (item 13 (b)).
- **No real-data trajectory difference has been detected.** Neither case study rejects any statistic (below). At ADNI's design the non-rejections are measured to be uninformative (item 9); at SEA-AD's they are bracketed to be uninformative. Orientation-driver attribution has therefore not run on a real-data claim, and on SEA-AD's real blocks a planted orientation on 20 of 10,000 features is not detected at up to 3 within-stage SDs, because it lies almost entirely outside the stage-PLS span (item 12). Neither the split test nor the interaction scan rejects on SEA-AD; at its stage signal no part of the split test has power for any plant, so those non-rejections are uninformative too.

## Phases

The original sequence ran from closing the baseline studies through the shape and construction audits, attribution, a medium pilot and the paper-grade study to a real-data case study, with SNF-native methods optional at the end.

### Phase 0 — Close and preserve the baseline

**Done 2026-08-05.** The latent-space metric-compatibility and inverse-PLS studies are archived and their capability specifications are canonical. The production contract is recorded: pooled PLS-DA, M-value methylation, a fixed shared coordinate system and per-statistic interpretation boundaries. The July [M-value/PLS pilot](reports/mvalue-pls-pilot-2026-07-30.md) predates the corrected shape estimator and the realized-geometry diagnostics; it is kept unchanged as historical evidence.

### Phase 1 — Shape invariance

**Resolved 2026-08-04** ([audit](reports/procrustes-shape-invariance-audit-2026-08-04.md)). Translation, uniform scaling and proper rotation have documented expected outputs enforced by tests. The reflection policy was revisited on 2026-09-03 (`unify-shape-reflection-policy`): reflections are aligned away at every ambient dimension, and mirror pairs score zero at full rank and above.

### Phase 2 — Orientation and shape constructions before integration

**Resolved 2026-08-05** ([findings](reports/realized-generator-geometry-2026-08-05.md)). Every study mode has a documented realized geometry before PLS, and evaluations record analytic population, standardized population, observed standardized and PLS-latent checkpoints. Orientation is pure in native methylation but mixed after the biological cascade; both shape constructions are free or mixed bends, not shape-specific interventions.

### Phase 3 — Orientation-driver attribution

**Implemented 2026-08-27** (`add-orientation-driver-attribution`; extended 2026-09-04 by `align-orientation-attribution`; [API](api/attribution.md)). One pooled preprocessing and PLS model stays fixed for both groups. Attribution reports the observed normalized feature contrast `q_observed = d_B/‖d_B‖ − d_A/‖d_A‖`, its PLS-reconstructed counterpart `q_PLS` and their residual, for adjacent transitions and for the signed principal orientations that decompose the principal-axis `angle` (with a relative-eigengap degeneracy flag). It adds bootstrap sign, rank and top-k stability within design strata, correlated-feature module and caller-supplied pathway summaries, and standardized and original-unit effects. Pooled VIP is context only. The exit gate, a reproducible driver report for a significant orientation result, is exercised in simulation from Phase 4 on and has never been exercised on a real-data claim (Phase 6).

### Phase 4 — Medium pilot

**HOLD 2026-08-27** ([report](reports/phase4-medium-pls-pilot-2026-08-27.md); 1,900 units at 100 × 199, n = 300, four stages). All 18 Type I controls passed, magnitude `delta` was 1.00 and specific, and shape `shape` was 0.94–1.00 and monotone. Orientation `angle` reached 0.65 against the 0.80 floor with a flat curve. A sign-anchor defect was found and fixed but did not explain the shortfall ([report](reports/orientation-sign-anchor-2026-08-28.md)). The [pivotality study](reports/angle-null-pivotality-2026-09-01.md) explained it: each replicate's `angle` critical value tracks its own observed angle, a load-bearing property rather than a defect. The [configuration spectrum](reports/latent-config-spectrum-2026-09-02.md) records, on every replicate, the eigengap that makes it resolvable, and the [geometry audit](reports/geometry-audit-2026-09-01.md) set the preconditions for Phase 5. Two findings carry forward: driver reports must use the observed contrast, because the PLS reconstruction keeps about 6 % of it (captured-component top-20 precision 0.15 against 1.00 observed); and a space supervised on stage is sized for stage separation, not for the group orientation contrast.

### Phase 5 — Paper-grade operating-characteristic study

**Closed 2026-09-10 as a conditional pass** ([exit review](reports/phase5-exit-review-2026-09-10.md)). The design point is ρ = 0, n = 1,200, four stages, `p_dmp` 0.1 ([design-point pilot](reports/phase5-design-point-pilot-2026-09-08.md)), and the rank rule is stage-supervised double CV ([latent-rank ladder](reports/latent-rank-ladder-2026-09-08.md), `keep_cv`). The paper-grade run (9,500 units at 500 × 999, 0 failures; [report](reports/phase5-paper-grade-2026-09-10.md)) met all three power diagonals and all 21 Type I checks and held the gate at HOLD on the two magnitude controls. The [magnitude-construction diagnostic](reports/magnitude-construction-diagnostic-2026-09-10.md) traced that to the methylation-only `all` surgery, and the `joint` re-measurement lifted the magnitude claim on 2026-10-08 ([addendum](reports/phase5-magnitude-remeasurement-2026-10-08.md); 4,500 units, gate PROCEED). Sample size and block count at three stages are measured at paper grade by the design-guidance slice (item 7).

### Phase 6 — Real-data case study

**Closed 2026-11-02 on a size-controlled null, with the attribution leg reported failed** ([exit review](reports/phase6-exit-review-2026-11-02.md)). The exit gate (collaborators trace each global trajectory claim to stable drivers and reproduce it from documented inputs) is recorded as not met as written. Reproduction is met; driver tracing is unexercised because neither case study has a claim, and the vacuous pass is refused. Covariate-adjusted attribution was then validated (item 11), but the plasmode on SEA-AD's real blocks did not detect a 20-feature planted orientation (item 12, `attribution_leg_failed`). Phase 6 is not reopened. The case studies are described below.

### Phase 7 — SNF-native trajectory methodology (optional)

**Deferred; the question is closed rather than pending** ([Phase 5 exit review](reports/phase5-exit-review-2026-09-10.md), §5). It awaits a decision to define graph-native path-length and angle statistics, which is independent of the PLS workflow. Candidate work: group-stage states on one pooled fused graph; diffusion or resistance path length as a magnitude analogue; transition profiles or diffusion flows as an orientation analogue; normalized stage-by-stage diffusion-distance matrices as a configuration analogue; a permutation scheme that rebuilds or conditions on the graph without breaking exchangeability; and validation on controlled nonlinear manifolds. **Exit gate:** an SNF-specific statistic has a graph-native estimand, calibrated Type I error, controlled power and no claim of preserving feature-space Euclidean geometry.

## Case studies

**SEA-AD** middle temporal gyrus astrocytes, with microglia as a positive-control cell type, in [Omnia-Lab-UC/sead_sexdiff](https://github.com/Omnia-Lab-UC/sead_sexdiff). Donor-level RNA + ATAC pseudobulks (TMM log2-CPM, top 5,000 variable features per block, chrX/Y/M removed); sex is the group and ADNC is merged into three stages (Not AD + Low / Intermediate / High). The primary cohort has ≥ 30 nuclei (astrocytes n = 80: F 11/10/28, M 9/10/12); the ≥ 50-nuclei cohort is a sensitivity analysis. Six donor covariate columns, rank floor 2, `--residual-correction df`.

**ADNI** serum metabolomics in [Omnia-Lab-UC/adni_sexdiff](https://github.com/Omnia-Lab-UC/adni_sexdiff): sex × stage (CN / MCI / AD) on Biocrates p180 (133 features) and Nightingale NMR (165), n = 1,501 (F 237/310/125, M 223/448/158), five covariates (six design columns), 9,999 permutations.

**How a case study runs MOTCO.** Through `motco analyze` or `motco.analysis.analyze_trajectories` ([API](api/analysis.md)), pinned to the release that contains every option it uses. That entry point is the harness's production measurement on caller-supplied blocks (a regression test checks it against the harness on identical data); it defaults to net displacement, requires an explicit stage order and records the selected rank, null summary and spectrum. Do not assemble the analysis from `motco plsr` + `motco de` or from the stats primitives: they are a different pipeline, and the primitives and `motco de` default to PC1. Carry the structural-analogue limitation (InterSIM blocks, 498 measured features).

**Binding interpretability statement** at the SEA-AD design ([small-n report](reports/phase6-small-n-2026-10-09.md); `angle` revised 2026-10-15 from the [power comparison](reports/net-displacement-power-2026-10-14.md) §7):

- **`delta`.** A rejection is evidence that the trajectories differ; it is consistent with a size difference but does not exclude a direction or shape difference. A non-rejection argues only against size differences of about 25 % or more.
- **`shape`.** A rejection is evidence that the trajectories differ, but not of which kind. A non-rejection is uninformative at n = 80.
- **`angle`.** The angle between the groups' net displacement vectors (last stage mean − first stage mean) in the latent space, so it reads only the endpoint stages. A rejection is evidence that the groups' net directions of change differ: Type I is controlled (anchor 0.050), and the size, shape and location constructions stay within the bound (0.024–0.068). Translation sits at 0.058–0.068, near the bound, so a rejection alongside a location difference warrants care. A non-rejection is uninformative at n = 80.
- **Selected rank.** The cohort's CV-selected PLS rank is the covariate reported beside any `angle` result.

**Results to date.**

- **SEA-AD.** No statistic is rejected in any of the 44 analyses: the four primaries, twelve forced ranks, sixteen preprocessing variants, eight latent-adjustment arms and four donor exclusions (re-issued under `df`, MOTCO 0.13.0, 2026-10-31). The primaries' `delta` / `angle` / `shape` p-values: astrocyte `cohort_30` (CV rank 5, AUROC 0.636) 0.501 / 0.449 / 0.331; astrocyte `cohort_50` (rank 2) 0.628 / 0.242 / 0.514; microglia `cohort_30` (rank 2) 0.705 / 0.456 / 0.991; microglia `cohort_50` (rank 4) 0.834 / 0.291 / 0.644. The split test's complement, its one confirmatory part, is rejected in none of the 40 analyses that carry it (0.18.0, 2026-11-06; `cohort_30` astrocyte p = 0.886, microglia 0.795). The interaction scan's global p-value, read at α = 0.05 under a multiplicity policy fixed before any real-label scan, rejects in neither `cohort_30` primary (0.20.0, 2026-11-10; astrocyte 0.962, microglia 0.956), and no feature is a hit in any primary. The positive control fails on the primary basis in `cohort_30` and holds on the `latent_adjusted` basis. The `stage` latent adjustment is inadmissible at this configuration (`residualized_basis`), so its `angle` and `shape` p-values are reported but not read.
- **ADNI.** The primary selects CV rank 8 at a held-out stage AUROC of 0.600 and rejects nothing (`delta` p 0.112, `angle` 0.688, `shape` 0.711). Across the sensitivities only `delta` rejects, at forced rank 6 (0.047) and under the `nmr_all_columns` export (0.045); `adni_sexdiff` reads both as dependence on that arm's setting. At ADNI's design no statistic reaches the 0.80 power floor (item 9), so its non-rejections are uninformative.

## Cross-cutting engineering work

- Preserve fitted preprocessing, PLS model, component count, feature order and loadings as a versioned analysis artifact. `motco analyze --out-dir` writes `result.json`, latent scores, PLS loadings and latent stage means, records units, the feature-order signature and software versions, and pickles no estimator.
- Record seeds, configurations, parameter signatures, software versions and failed replicates in every study.
- Keep fast deterministic invariance tests separate from slow operating-characteristic studies, and keep CI coverage for core geometry invariants and study-config validation.
- Prefer structured CSV/JSON outputs plus concise Markdown reports over findings that exist only in notebooks.
- Keep public documentation synchronized with methodological decisions and archived OpenSpec findings.
- **Preprocessing scope boundary** (decided 2026-10-16): a step belongs in MOTCO only if it touches the model or the permutation null. Normalization stays upstream: it is omic-specific, and when it is blind to the labels it changes what is measured, not the validity of the test. Label-blind feature filtering stays upstream; supervised filtering (by group or stage) would bias the null unless repeated inside every permutation, so MOTCO documents it as forbidden. Imputation stays upstream; the finite-values contract stays, and missing covariates are handled only by the explicit `missing_covariates` policy. Block weighting is the one preprocessing step that belongs in MOTCO (item 5).

## Explicitly deferred

- Expanding SNF use before graph-native metrics are defined
- Reading the pooled orientation power figure as applicable at any eigengap
- Claiming `angle` or `shape` specificity against the methylation-only `all` magnitude construction (its controls fail; the claim holds for `joint` only — [addendum](reports/phase5-magnitude-remeasurement-2026-10-08.md))
- Interpreting pooled VIP scores as orientation drivers
- Fitting separate, unaligned PLS spaces per group
- Claiming that a latent-space reconstruction is a unique or causal molecular inverse
- Optimizing performance before the statistic and construction contracts are settled
- Reading a non-rejection at an unmeasured design or stage signal as evidence that trajectories agree

## Next changes

### Open

Each is its own change; none is owed by Phase 6 ([exit review](reports/phase6-exit-review-2026-11-02.md) §8).

1. **A group-aware space refitted per permutation** (thread 2 of item 16; sketched 2026-11-09, unblocked by item 17's `keep_production`, so it pairs against `pls_cv`). The question is a group-stratified stage space: stage-PLS fitted per group and merged into one basis, so each group's own stage program is in the span and a group-specific direction (the φ = 90 rotation every pooled space is blind to) becomes measurable, not only detectable.
    - **Why it must refit.** The harness has the construction as the diagnostic `pls_within_group` (K − 1 components per group, stacked rotations orthonormalized by pivoted QR, pooled-centred projection). The orientation probe (2026-10-12) found it keeps the planted rotation (90.3° against 90°) but, fitted once on the real labels, inflates anchor `angle` to 0.48–0.88: the union span is chosen where each group's noise aligns with stage, and RRPP's draws never see the fitting step. Any basis fitted with group labels has this problem, so a valid version refits the basis inside every draw: Freedman–Lane on the reduced model's feature residuals with RRPP's serial draws (the split test's scheme), two stage-PLS fits per draw at a rank chosen label-blind from the pooled CV and shared by both groups, projection, then `delta` / `angle` / `shape`. At fixed rank a fit takes milliseconds, so 999 refits cost seconds.
    - **The pilot, when proposed:** four arms on the latent-space comparison's designs and cells (its φ ∈ {0, 30, 60, 90} cells were built for this): `pls_cv`, `within_group_fixed` (to show the size failure on these cells), `within_group_refit` (the candidate) and `cell_pls_refit` (one PLS on the group × stage cells refitted per draw; one basis, no QR tolerance). Read for size at anchor and translation, out-of-span power against the complement's 1.00 / 0.68, and in-span cost on magnitude `delta` (two added near-empty dimensions cost 0.74 → 0.37 at n = 200 in the comparison; the QR tolerance is a knob the null depends on).
    - **The bar:** match the complement at held size with no in-span loss beyond the comparison's 2-pooled-SE rule, else the complement stays the answer. Its payoff is measurement (a measured angle, group-specific attribution), not detection, which the complement already provides; at SEA-AD the pooled span keeps 0.10 of the stage program on 80 donors, so per-group bases on 40 are expected to add little on real data.
