    Univariate interaction baseline — report (2026-11-23)

    Change: univariate-interaction-baseline (MOTCO 0.25.0, no package change). Reads: results/univariate-baseline-2026-11-23/ (univariate_baseline_decision.json; per profile settings.json, rate_table.csv, paired_table.csv, cell_table.csv, accounting.json; parent/ and surgery/reproduction.json; PROVENANCE.txt, sacct.txt). Parents: the interaction-scan validation (its simulation settings and comparator table, bound by sha256) and the design-guidance slice (its two core configs and operating tables). Concept page: measurement visibility.

    The first comparison a reader makes is against the default analysis with no integration: a group × stage interaction test on each feature. The package has that test as the interaction scan, which was validated as a complement for sparse differences and never judged as a baseline (roadmap open item 13). This change judges it as one. On each unit, one production fit feeds the trajectory test, which enters as one omnibus test (smallest of delta, angle and shape at 0.05 / 3). Its standardized features feed the per-feature interaction F, combined three ways: the scan's max-T maximum (scan), Benjamini–Hochberg on the F p-values (fdr), and the split test's total part (total). One decision, fixed before any unit ran, labels each of 71 signal cells by paired exact tests.

    In one paragraph. The verdict is complementary.

    • Each side leads somewhere, and where follows how the difference spreads. Production leads in 16 cells: every one is a small stretch of the stage program or a small bend inside its span. For example, a stretch of e = 0.05 at n = 200 reads 0.815 against fdr's 0.235 and total's 0.045. The baseline leads in 21 cells: the feature-set surgeries' orientations and shapes at n = 80 and 200 (fdr 1.000 against production's 0.26–0.86) and one shape at n = 500, the out-of-span rotations, and in-span rotations at n = 80 and at the weaker stage effect. The other 34 cells are unresolved, most because both sides saturate.
    • The default analysis holds size in two of its three forms. fdr and total hold in both families. The max-T scan is inadmissible: on the three-block design at n = 80 it rejects 38 and 35 of 500 null units, above the per-cell bound of 0.0695, where its rule allows one such cell. Production holds everywhere (at most 26 of 500).
    • The grade is detects_only. Production detects 26 of the 36 in-span stage-span cells at 0.80 or more. Its off-target statistics fail as a family on one cell: a 45° rotation at the weaker stage effect, where shape reads 0.665 and delta 0.155. A rejection there names a difference, not its kind.
    • Both reproductions pass exactly (196 and 99 rows), and 33,700 of 33,700 units completed.
    • The consequence fixed with the rule. The documentation states the map of which side leads by plant family and design. A case study reports both analyses under a multiplicity policy fixed in advance. The reading holds at 658 simulated features with Gaussian noise; no real-noise reading exists (roadmap open item 2). No verdict changes what motco analyze runs.

The anchor and a translation at 500 replicates.     • Profile surgery: the slice's three-block units. The design-guidance slice's columns at n = 80, 200 and 500 with three blocks, from its committed configs: the anchor, translation at e = 1.0, magnitude at e = 0.02, 0.05 and 0.1, and orientation and shape at e = 0.25, 0.5 and 1, every cell at 500. 16,500 units, 27 signal cells.     • Units. 4,400, 12,800 and 16,500 complete: no failed unit, no duplicate, no failed planted fixed point.
    • parent reproduction: passed. All 196 rows of the scan validation's comparator table are equal: the scan, the split test's parts and delta / angle / shape, per design and cell.

    3. The decision

    The order is gate, size, detection. Size is judged per arm in two families: stage_span (m = 8) and surgery (m = 6). Each family uses the count-plus-ceiling rule (judge_rates), with a per-cell bound of 0.0695 at 500 replicates. Then each signal cell is labelled by paired exact tests of production against every admissible baseline arm:

    • production_leads. The gain p-value is at most 0.025 / 71 = 3.5 × 10⁻⁴ against every admissible arm, and the rate exceeds each arm's by at least 0.10.
    • baseline_leads. The loss p-value is at most 0.025 / (71 · A) against at least one arm, and that arm's rate exceeds production's by at least 0.10. A is the number of admissible arms; with A = 2 the level is 1.76 × 10⁻⁴.
    • unresolved. Otherwise.

    The verdict is by existence: complementary when both labels occur. The out-of-span cells stay in the family. This makes complementary and baseline_leads the live outcomes (design D6).

    criterion reading
    gate both reproductions passed; 0 failed units
    size production, fdr and total admissible; scan inadmissible (stage_span)
    labels 16 production_leads, 21 baseline_leads, 34 unresolved of 71
    verdict complementary
    grade detects_only

    • scan is inadmissible. 38 / 500 = 0.076 and 35 / 500 = 0.070 both exceed the bound of 0.0695 in the stage_span family, which allows one exceedance. Its surgery family holds.
    • The two exceedances are nearly one observation. In stage_span, the anchor and the translation of a replicate are the same null dataset; the translation adds a group offset, which the interaction F does not see. Only the permutation seeds differ by cell. fdr, which draws no permutation, reads 31 and 31 there. The rule counts cells as declared, and the arm leaves the comparison; the report does not waive it.

The parametric fdr on the same F values holds (at most 0.062), as does total (at most 0.060). Production's largest null rate is 0.052.

    5.3 Unresolved (34 cells)

    Thirty of the 34 have both production and fdr at 0.88 or more, the saturated end of each ladder. The other four sit near the level on both sides: at δ = 0.5 a stretch at e = 0.05 (production 0.075, fdr 0.045), a rotation at e = 0.1 (0.090, 0.095) and a bend at e = 0.1 (0.075, 0.035), and the surgery's magnitude at e = 0.02 at n = 80 (0.070, 0.034). Every rate is in the rate table and the decision's cell_rates.

    6. The characterization grade: detects_only

    The grade reads production alone, on the 36 in-span stage_span cells, where off target has an exact truth under the planted scale. Production detects 26 of them at 0.80 or more. Their 52 off-target statistics (two per cell) form one family under judge_rates: a per-cell bound of 0.081 at 200 replicates, a ceiling of 0.105, and three exceedances allowed.

    design cell statistic rate reading
    n = 200 bend e = 0.1 delta 0.085 above the bound
    n = 200, δ = 0.5 rotation e = 0.5, φ = 0 delta 0.155 above the ceiling
    n = 200, δ = 0.5 rotation e = 0.5, φ = 0 shape 0.665 above the ceiling

    All three failing readings come from two cells, and the two above the ceiling come from one. That cell is the 45° rotation at the weaker stage effect, where the CV rank inflates (median 4, 10–90 % range 3–5, against 2 at δ = 2.0). This is the rank confound the slice found below δ = 2.0, now as off-target leakage. The other 49 readings hold. Under detects_only, a rejection by production names a group × stage difference; which statistic rejects does not name its kind at every design. The baseline cannot make that second claim at all (design D7).

    The surgeries' off-target rates are reported in the decision (off_target_reported, 35 readings) and not graded, because those plants are not geometrically pure.

    7. Descriptive readings

    7.1 The recorded arms

    • production_minp. Its rate is never below the omnibus's and at most 0.055 above (n = 80, stretch e = 0.05: 0.400 against 0.345), in all 85 cells. The Bonferroni omnibus costs little here.
    • pca_matched. The trajectory statistics on PCA at production's rank rarely reach production's power: in one of 71 signal cells only (the surgery's orientation at e = 1 at n = 80, 0.840 against 0.482). Unsupervised components at the same dimension do not hold the stage program that production's fit holds.
    • span and complement. span tracks production on the in-span cells. complement reads at its level on the stretches and bends (0.035–0.075) and on rotations up to e = 0.25 (0.035–0.110), and rises to 0.16–0.31 on the rotation at e = 0.5. It reads 1.000 on every surgery orientation and shape but one (0.774), and 0.885–1.000 on the out-of-span rotations except the 20-feature form at n = 80 (0.245). That is the split test's statement: these differences lie outside the fitted span.

    7.2 Discoveries

    On the null cells the share of units with any fdr discovery is the arm's rejection rate (0.028–0.062). On the signal cells, the fdr arm's realized false discovery proportion on population-null features averages 0.005–0.045 per cell, below the 0.05 it controls. The scan arm controls the family-wise rate instead, and finds fewer features: for example, a median of 192.5 hits against 223 discoveries on the surgery's orientation at e = 0.5 at n = 80.

    7.3 The fit

    Production's CV rank has median 2 at every δ = 2.0 design (3 in a few n = 80 cells) and 3–4 at δ = 0.5. Its median held-out AUROC is at least 0.918 in every cell. The in-span stage-span plants keep 0.88–0.99 of the group difference in the fitted span (medians), the out-of-span rotations 0.003–0.010 (0.03–0.17 for the 20-feature form).

    7.4 The parent profile

    The scan validation's own units, now with fdr and production's omnibus beside the committed tests (decision, descriptive.parent). They repeat the question's pattern. At n = 200, a stretch reads production 0.66 against scan 0.09 and fdr 0.06, and a bend at e = 0.25 reads 0.99 against 0.85 and 0.74. An in-span rotation at e = 0.25 reads 0.73 against 1.00 and 1.00, and the out-of-span rotation at e = 0.25 reads 0.01 against 0.76 and 0.70. On this profile the omnibus's null rates are 0.024–0.036.

    8. What follows

    • The documentation states the map (design, migration plan). Measurement visibility now says what each side tests and where each leads. The trajectory test leads on diffuse stretches and small bends inside the stage program. The per-feature test leads on concentrated differences: feature-set surgeries, out-of-span rotations, and in-span rotations at small n or a weak stage effect.
    • A case study reports both analyses under a multiplicity policy fixed before any real-label reading. The admissible per-feature combinations at these designs are fdr and total. The max-T scan ran above its level at n = 80 on three blocks.
    • What only the trajectory test claims. Detection of a diffuse difference that no single feature carries. Under detects_only, the statistic that rejects is not a reliable name for the kind of difference at every design: the weak-stage-effect rotation leaks into shape.
    • What does not change. motco analyze runs what it ran; no default moves.
    • Follow-ups the change names. A separate change may add the false-discovery reading to the scan's output and to motco analyze, since fdr was admissible and leads where the scan cannot be read. The real-noise reading of the same arms belongs to the first multi-omic ROSMAP or ADNI export (roadmap open item 2). A moderated per-feature statistic (roadmap item 6) would be a fourth baseline arm.

    9. Limitations

    • Simulated features with Gaussian noise. Every claim holds at 658 simulated features with one variance per feature. Per-feature tests are exact there, and a moderated statistic has nothing to add. Real methylation, expression and proteomics have heavy tails, mean–variance dependence and correlated features. No real-noise reading exists (roadmap open item 2).
    • The map depends on the cells chosen. The verdict is by existence for that reason; the map, not a count, carries the content. Stretches and bends of the stage program favour production, and permutation surgeries and out-of-span rotations favour the baseline. How real differences divide between these forms is not known.
    • concat was not run (approval gate, cost). Its comparison against production is the latent-space comparison's, on a related construction and other units.
    • The scan's inadmissibility rests on two correlated readings of one design. The rule was declared that way and is applied as declared. A scan at n = 80 on more blocks, or with a corrected residual, was not read.
    • The surgery profile reuses the slice's permutation seed (3 for every unit). That reproduces the published counts exactly. It also means the profile's units differ only in their generator seeds.
