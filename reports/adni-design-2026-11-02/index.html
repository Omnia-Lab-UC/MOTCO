    Operating characteristics at the ADNI design — 2026-11-02

    This report measures size and power of the real-data analysis at the ADNI case study's design (openspec change adni-design-operating-characteristics, spec adni-design-validation; roadmap Next changes item 9). The case study (adni_sexdiff) reads its angle and shape non-rejections as uninformative under its reading rule 3, because no power statement existed at its design. This check supplies one.

    Every number below is read from results/adni-design-2026-11-02/: - run/adni_design_decision.json, run/size_readings.csv, run/adni_design_rates.csv, run/adni_design_rank_strata.csv, run/merge.json, run/decide.json, run/settings.json, run/PROVENANCE.txt; - calibration/calibration.json (every calibration iteration); - the smoke smoke/ (R = 20 × 99 permutations, indicative).

    run/records/ and the 1.66 MB run/adni_design_replicates.csv stay on ing; run/PROVENANCE.txt names them with their sha256. Rates are rejection rates at p ≤ 0.05 over R = 500 replicates at 999 permutations.

Its git tree 3ec28936… equals the workstation's, and the change's tests passed there. - All 175 tasks ended COMPLETED 0:0 between 15:23Z and 16:48Z on 2026-11-02. 7,000 of 7,000 units, 0 failures. - 174.6 elapsed core-hours (planned about 200, range 105–265); the units' own timers sum to 152.7 h. - The afterany job 932123 ran merge and decide.     Verdict: non_rejections_uninformative, read verbatim from adni_design_decision.json ("indicative": false). - "size": "holds": true, "exceeding": []. All six readings sit at or below the 0.0695 bound. - "informative_from" is null for delta, angle and shape: no target statistic reaches the 0.80 floor at any effect.

    In one paragraph. - The design. ADNI's sex × stage table (n = 1,501) was rebuilt with two blocks and six covariate columns of ADNI's kinds: a confounded numeric, a factor in two drop-first columns, a phase-like binary nuisance injected into one block, and two noise columns. The stage signal was calibrated to ADNI's held-out AUROC, and the run used a seed family no committed check uses. Every unit ran the case study's primary as issued: analyze_trajectories with the six covariates, the rank floor of 2 and net displacement. - Size. It holds at both controls. The six readings are 0.034–0.068 against 0.0695. - Power. None of the three statistics has useful power. Magnitude/delta reaches 0.530 at a doubled stage effect in one group (e = 1.0). Orientation/angle and shape/shape stay at the null rate (0.034–0.076) at every effect. - Rank. The CV-selected rank sits at the cap, 19, in 95–100 % of units at every cell, against ADNI's 8. - Consequence. adni_sexdiff keeps its rule 3, now citing these measured rates, in its own change. The Phase 6 exit review (roadmap item 10) reads ADNI as a design without power for any of the three statistics.

    1. Construction

    scripts/adni_design_check.py composes the case-study construction by import (case_study_type_i_check, built on the D10 construction of covariate_type_i_check):

    • Generator. The semi-synthetic generator at ADNI's table [[237, 310, 125], [223, 448, 158]], three stages, methylation (367 CpGs) and expression (131 genes), joint magnitude, p_dmp 0.1, and one weakened per-omic stage effect δ.
    • Covariates, in model order (six design columns, as covariates.design_columns records them):
    • z: D10's numeric covariate, confounded with group × stage and injected along a fixed direction over 20 % of the joint features at s_z (ADNI's age).
    • category[c1], category[c2]: D10's three-level covariate on the same linear predictor, declared as a factor and not injected (ADNI's apoe4[1], apoe4[2]).
    • phase: binary, drawn per sex × stage cell at ADNI's ADNI2GO shares (F 0.603 / 0.623 / 0.424, M 0.561 / 0.507 / 0.481; adni_sexdiff results/motco_input/). It is injected into the expression block only as a dense offset s_p · sd_f · d_f · phase, with d_f i.i.d. N(0, 1) drawn once from the base seed. It enters the model as a numeric 0/1 column (ADNI's phase[ADNI2GO]). The expression block's 131 features stand for p180's 133.
    • x1, x2: i.i.d. N(0, 1), never injected (ADNI's education, statin[1]).
    • Seeds. Every seed derives from base seed 2032 and the replicate index, so the 14 cells of a replicate share the baseline draw, the covariates, the phase draw and the permutation draws, and differ only in the surgery.
    • Analysis. One adjusted analysis per unit: the six covariates in the trajectory model, min_components = 2, net displacement, the harness CV knobs (3 × 4 folds, 5 repeats, max_components 20), 999 permutations and the replicate's RRPP seed. There is no latent adjustment and no residual correction, as in ADNI's primary (κ ≈ 1.003 at n = 1,501 would be immaterial).
    • Cells. The anchor; translation at e = 1.0; magnitude at e = 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 1.0; orientation and shape at e = 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0.

    Calibration

    Twelve iterations on anchor units (R = 20 × 99 permutations, two arms), all in calibration/calibration.json, met the three predeclared criteria at δ = 0.02, s_z = 0.1, s_p = 1.75. The iterations and why this one was chosen are tabulated in the change's design.md (Calibration). In short:

    • At D10's s_z = 3 the first component followed z (|ρ| 0.94) and the AUROC sat at 0.77–0.88.
    • At s_z = 0.3 the AUROC kept a floor near 0.645 that δ barely moved.
    • At s_z = 0.1 the floor fell, and the phase offset steered the basis without adding stage signal: raising s_p from 1.5 to 2.5 moved the AUROC from 0.602 to 0.604.

    The calibrated construction, as realized at the paper-grade run's 500 anchor units (calibration_realized in the decision), beside ADNI's primary:

    The construction matches ADNI on the calibrated quantities (AUROC, the basis following phase, the size of the offset) and differs on the latent geometry. The CV selects every component it is offered (rank 19 against 8). The stage means are less one-dimensional (eigengap 0.33 against 0.74), and the angle null is wider (51° against 35°). The offset is the injected block's whole first axis, where ADNI's p180 has other structure of its own.

    The paper-grade run has no unadjusted arm. At calibration the unadjusted arm rejected delta at 0.15 and shape at 0.45 at the anchor, so the construction confounds (criterion 3).

    2. Size

    size_readings.csv, bound α + 2·√(α(1 − α)/R) = 0.0695 at R = 500, so at most 34 rejections of 500 hold:

    control delta angle shape
    anchor (e = 0) 17 / 500 = 0.034 30 / 500 = 0.060 20 / 500 = 0.040
    translation (e = 1.0) 17 / 500 = 0.034 27 / 500 = 0.054 34 / 500 = 0.068
    • Every reading holds. Translation shape holds at the largest count the bound allows (34). Under the rule's design (D8), a valid test fails at most one of six readings about 17 % of the time at exact α, so one reading near the bound is expected.
    • delta is conservative (0.034 at both controls). Six covariate columns at n = 1,501 cost almost nothing in residual df, so the liberal delta the SEA-AD configuration showed at n = 80 (residual_df_shrinkage) does not arise here.
    • The size sentence, verbatim: "Size holds at the ADNI design: all six readings at or below the one-sided bound".

    • Magnitude/delta. It is the only statistic whose rate rises with the effect, but it stays below the floor. At e = 1.0 (group B's per-omic stage effect twice group A's) delta rejects 0.530. The magnitude surgery also raises the stage AUROC, from 0.614 at e = 0.1 to 0.683 at e = 1.0 (cv_mean_auroc_mean).
    • Orientation/angle and shape/shape. Both sit at the null rate at every effect: 0.056–0.076 and 0.034–0.062. These surgeries permute or relocate differential features whose stage effect is δ = 0.02. At an AUROC near 0.60 that moves the group's stage means by too little for a test whose null angle q95 is about 51°.
    • Rank strata (adni_design_rank_strata.csv; descriptive). The orientation surgery pushes more units to rank 2–3 as e grows (4 → 96 of 500), but those units do not reject more often. At e = 1.0: 2 / 96 = 0.021 at rank 2–3, 1 / 8 at 4–7, 26 / 396 = 0.066 at 8+. At e = 0.75: 3 / 53 = 0.057 at 2–3, 35 / 444 = 0.079 at 8+. The Phase 6 pattern, where power rises above rank 3, does not appear, because there is no power to stratify. Magnitude sits at rank 8+ in 499–500 units per effect. The strata's replicates sum to the pooled R in every cell.
    • Off-target rates (descriptive). They lie at 0.022–0.078: magnitude by angle 0.050–0.060 and by shape 0.028–0.042; orientation by delta 0.022–0.050 and by shape 0.030–0.058; shape by delta 0.030–0.044 and by angle 0.050–0.078. Only one of the 24 off-target readings lies above 0.0695: shape e = 0.75 read by angle (0.078), which does not enter the verdict.

    4. Verdict, statements and consequences

    Verdict: non_rejections_uninformative. Size holds at both controls and no target statistic has an informative effect. The decision file's statements, verbatim:

    • delta: "At the ADNI design delta reaches at most 0.530 (SE 0.022, R = 500) at e = 1 of the magnitude construction, at CV-selected rank 19 (median 19); a non-rejection is uninformative up to the construction maximum (e = 1; floor 0.80)."
    • angle: "At the ADNI design angle reaches at most 0.076 (SE 0.012, R = 500) at e = 0.75 of the orientation construction, at CV-selected rank 2–19 (median 19); a non-rejection is uninformative up to the construction maximum (e = 1; floor 0.80)."
    • shape: "At the ADNI design shape reaches at most 0.062 (SE 0.011, R = 500) at e = 0.25 of the shape construction, at CV-selected rank 2–19 (median 19); a non-rejection is uninformative up to the construction maximum (e = 1; floor 0.80)."

    Consequences.

    • adni_sexdiff. It keeps reading rule 3 in its own change, now as a measurement rather than an absence of one. Its angle and shape non-rejections are uninformative: at this design and stage signal neither statistic detects the generator's strongest orientation or shape construction above the null rate. Its delta non-rejection (p 0.112) is uninformative too, by the same rule: delta reaches 0.53 only at a doubled stage effect in one group.
    • Size holds at both controls, and delta is conservative there (0.034).
    • So the sensitivity arms' delta rejections near α (forced rank 6, p 0.047; nmr_all_columns, 0.045) are not explained by a liberal null at this design. They remain the arm-setting dependences the case study reads them as.
    • MOTCO. The documentation states the measured rates beside the SEA-AD statement. Nothing under src/motco/ changes, and the version stays 0.13.0.
    • Roadmap item 10 (Phase 6 exit review). It reads both case studies as size-controlled designs without power for orientation and shape. ADNI also has no useful power for size.

    5. Limitations and scope

    • Structural analogue. The blocks are InterSIM omics, not metabolomics: 367 methylation and 131 expression features (498) against p180's 133 and NMR's 165 (298). The block shares differ: methylation / expression 74 / 26 % against NMR / p180 55 / 45 %. The generator's latent geometry differs from ADNI's: the CV rank is at the cap (19 against 8), the eigengap is 0.33 against 0.74, and the phase offset is the injected block's whole first axis (0.99 against 0.73). No statement is made about metabolite-level behaviour.
    • One table. Only the intersection cohort's table (n = 1,501) is measured. The CN / LMCI / AD arm (n = 1,130) is inferred, not measured.
    • One strength. The run used one calibrated stage signal (δ = 0.02, s_z = 0.1, s_p = 1.75). The calibration matched AUROC, the basis following phase and the offset size. It did not match the rank, which the design records but does not tune to.
    • The covariate model. Covariates enter additively and linearly: one confounded numeric (z, weakly injected at s_z = 0.1), one confounded factor (category, declared, not injected), one injected binary nuisance and two noise columns.
    • The primary only. The latent-adjusted, residual-corrected and forced-rank arms, and ADNI's export variants, were not run, so their operating characteristics at this design stay inferred.
