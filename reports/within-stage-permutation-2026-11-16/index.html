    Within-stage permutation draws — pilot report (2026-11-16)

    Change: within-stage-permutation (MOTCO 0.25.0). Reads: results/within-stage-permutation-2026-11-16/ (within_stage_permutation_decision.json; per profile the rate, paired and cell tables, accounting.json, reproduction.json and settings.json; plasmode/preflight.json; PROVENANCE.txt, sacct.txt). Parents: the predictive-subspace pilot and the predictive size-excess diagnosis. Concept page: measurement visibility.

    RRPP permutes the reduced model's residuals across all samples, which assumes they are exchangeable across stages. The diagnosis (roadmap item 19, both_restore) found that restricting the draws within stage removes the predictive coordinates' size excess on the SEA-AD plasmode anchor, and that production holds size and gains plant power under the same draws. It opened this change: the within-stage draws as an opt-in option of RRPP, the harness and motco analyze, read for size, power and specificity before predictive_orthonormal is re-read.

    In one paragraph. The verdict is specificity_excess.

    • Size holds everywhere. The within-stage null holds size in all four families: the simulated controls, the weak design, 500 SEA-AD label draws no run had read, and Gaussian data with stage-unequal dispersion, where the unrestricted null over-rejects when the largest stage is the narrowest (up to 0.102) and is conservative when it is the widest (down to 0.024).
    • Protected power holds at its limit. It costs angle power on three orientation cells, exactly the three the rule allows.
    • What fails is specificity in the stage-effect band. On in-span rotations the within-stage draws add off-target delta and shape rejections above the bound, 7 readings against the 4 allowed, one of them below the family's ceiling (delta 0.08 → 0.21 at the SEA-AD table, δ = 0.5, rotation e = 0.5).
    • The consequence fixed with the rule. The null stays opt-in, documented with those readings, and predictive_orthonormal is not reopened.
    • The candidate, read anyway. Under the same draws predictive_orthonormal qualifies against production by the pilot's four criteria. That reading is recorded and does not bear on the verdict.

    1. Configuration and provenance

    • Arms. One production fit per fitted unit. The scores (pls_cv) and the three predictive coordinate systems of that fit (predictive, predictive_orthonormal, predictive_fitted), each read under the unrestricted draws and, with the suffix __within_stage, under the package's permutation_strata="stage", at the unit's RRPP seed. Every arm went through the package option, not a driver loop.
    • Profiles.

    weak_size 1,000 the pilot's weak-design anchor and translation, 500 × 999, p < 0.05 ing
    plasmode 1,000 the SEA-AD astrocyte anchor at the paper stream's replicates 0–499 (anchor) and 500–999 (anchor_fresh), 999 permutations, every arm at rank 5, six covariates and df, p ≤ 0.05 workstation

The workstation rebooted during the dispersion run; the 6,058 complete records were intact and the run resumed the rest.

    2. Accounting and reproduction

    • Units. 19,800 of 19,800 complete: no failed unit, no failed arm, no inconsistent unit and no duplicate.
    • Checks. Every applicable check held on every unit, same_statistics and construction_identity exactly.
    • Pre-flight: passed. On replicates 0 and 500 the cross-validated fit selected rank 5 at held-out AUROC 0.635556 and gave the forced call's statistics and p-values exactly.
    • Reproduction: passed.
    • Weak size: the four unrestricted arms' counts equal the pilot's committed table on 24 of 24 rows.
    • Plasmode anchor: the unrestricted arms equal the pilot's counts (12 of 12 rows, for example pls_cv 21 / 24 / 13 and predictive_orthonormal 67 / 9 / 38). The within-stage arms equal the diagnosis's (12 of 12, for example pls_cv__within_stage 19 / 27 / 19 and predictive_orthonormal__within_stage 29 / 17 / 32). This is the package option reproducing the diagnosis's independent loop on real data.

    3. The decision

    Rule version 1 has two layers (design.md D12).

    • Layer 1, the null (pls_cv__within_stage against pls_cv):
    • Size: the stage-span paper grade's count-plus-ceiling rule in four families.
    • Protected power: the pilot's paired loss rule on the 54 target cells at δ ≥ 0.25.
    • Specificity: the bound rule on the δ = 2.0 off-target readings. In the band, a reading counts only if it is above the size bound and in significant paired excess over production (p < 0.025), at most 4 of 72 and none below 0.05 / 72.
    • Layer 2, the candidate (predictive_orthonormal__within_stage against pls_cv__within_stage): the pilot's S1–S3 and G with the reference changed to production under the same draws. It bears on the verdict only when layer 1 holds.
    criterion family m reading holds
    size simulation controls 42 0 above the bound; rates 0.01–0.09 yes
    size weak size 6 0 above; 0.040–0.048 yes
    size plasmode anchor_fresh 3 0 above; 0.046–0.064 yes
    size dispersion 30 1 above (allowed 3), none above the ceiling; 0.034–0.066 yes
    protected power target cells, δ ≥ 0.25 54 3 one-sided loss p-values below 0.025 (allowed 3), none below 0.05 / 54 yes
    specificity δ = 2.0 off-target 36 0 above the bound yes
    specificity band off-target 72 7 counted (allowed 4), 1 below the ceiling no

    The verdict is specificity_excess. The gate passed. The consequence fixed with the rule: "The null holds size and reads more off-target rejections than the bound allows. It stays opt-in, documented with those readings; predictive_orthonormal is not reopened."

    4. Size

    • Simulated controls. Under the within-stage draws production's anchor and translation read 0.01–0.09 over 42 readings at 100 units each, none above the bound of 0.0936. Where the unrestricted null is conservative the within-stage one reads closer to α. For example, the SEA-AD table's anchor delta is 0.01 under unrestricted draws and 0.06 under within-stage ones, and the weak design's 0.03 and 0.08.
    • Weak design (500 × 999). pls_cv__within_stage reads 23 / 21 / 20 on the anchor and 24 / 20 / 22 on the translation, against 17 / 19 / 25 and 18 / 13 / 26 under unrestricted draws.
    • Fresh SEA-AD draws (replicates 500–999). pls_cv__within_stage reads 32 / 26 / 23, inside the bound of 34. Production under unrestricted draws reads 32 / 24 / 12 on the same draws. On the reproduced draws (0–499) the diagnosis read 19 / 27 / 19, so the label draws alone move production's delta from 21 to 32 of 500.
    • Dispersion. Rejections of 1,000 (delta / angle / shape), unrestricted | within stage:
    pattern d = 2 d = 5
    equal 66 / 59 / 55 | 66 / 57 / 53 62 / 61 / 59 | 51 / 58 / 58
    predictive 79 / 50 / 73 | 54 / 53 / 55 77 / 48 / 59 | 60 / 40 / 46
    predictive_doubled 91 / 41 / 76 | 49 / 39 / 50 102 / 56 / 93 | 52 / 58 / 48
    scores 46 / 39 / 33 | 50 / 43 / 34 38 / 38 / 42 | 41 / 44 / 46
    scores_doubled 34 / 28 / 24 | 57 / 38 / 38 38 / 31 / 33 | 52 / 50 / 52
    • The mechanism, confirmed at the planned size. When the largest stage is the narrowest (the predictive coordinates' pattern) the unrestricted draws over-reject delta and shape, up to 0.102 and 0.093 with the inequality doubled. When it is the widest (the scores' pattern) they are conservative, down to 0.024. The within-stage draws read 0.034–0.066 in every cell.
    • The one exceedance is equal, d = 2, delta: 66 of 1,000 under both draws. The two arms read the same matrices, so it is a property of those matrices, not of the restriction; one exceedance is allowed at m = 30.

    5. Protected power

    The three readings below 0.025 are all angle on orientation cells (rates pls_cv → pls_cv__within_stage; b counts units only production rejects, c units only the within-stage arm rejects):

    design cell angle b / c one-sided p (loss)
    seaad (δ = 2.0) orientation e = 0.5 0.96 → 0.86 12 / 2 0.0065
    n200_delta1 orientation e = 0.25 0.62 → 0.53 11 / 2 0.011
    n200_delta0.5 orientation e = 0.25 0.27 → 0.19 8 / 0 0.0039

    The rule allows 3 at m = 54, so the criterion holds at its limit. Beside the losses the within-stage draws gain: delta on magnitude and shape on shape plants, in 4 of the 18 δ = 2.0 target cells and 5 of the 36 band cells at one-sided p < 0.025 (for example the SEA-AD table's shape at e = 0.1, b / c = 0 / 14). The weak design gains nowhere.

    6. Specificity: the band failure

    The 7 counted readings are all on rotation plants:

    design cell off-target pls_cv → pls_cv__within_stage b / c status
    n200_delta1 orientation e = 0.25 delta 0.05 → 0.12 0 / 7 excess
    n200_delta1 orientation e = 0.5 delta 0.07 → 0.16 0 / 9 excess
    n200_delta0.5 orientation e = 0.1 shape 0.08 → 0.14 0 / 6 excess
    n200_delta0.5 orientation e = 0.5 delta 0.28 → 0.34 0 / 6 excess
    n200_delta0.25 orientation e = 0.25 delta 0.08 → 0.14 0 / 6 excess
    seaad_delta0.5 orientation e = 0.25 shape 0.15 → 0.23 0 / 8 excess
    seaad_delta0.5 orientation e = 0.5 delta 0.08 → 0.21 0 / 13 below the ceiling (p = 0.00012 < 0.05 / 72)
    • It amplifies a leak production already has. Under unrestricted draws, 12 of production's 72 band off-target readings are above the bound; under within-stage draws, 23 are. The predictive-subspace pilot had recorded production's band leak under the planted scale. The rule charges the null only for readings it pushes both above the bound and significantly above production, and 7 qualify.
    • The pattern (descriptive, not tested). On an in-span rotation the within-stage draws trade angle for delta: they lose angle power on the same orientation cells where they add off-target delta. The planted interaction is in the reduced model's residuals, because the reduced model omits it. Unrestricted draws carry each stage's share of it to other stages; within-stage draws keep it in its own stage and exchange it only between groups. The angle null's median 95th percentile is slightly wider under within-stage draws on those cells (for example 33.9° → 34.5° at the SEA-AD table's rotation e = 0.5, and 23.4° → 23.8° at n200_delta0.5, e = 0.25). The delta rejections rise on the same cells. A reading of why is beyond this run.
    • At δ = 2.0 nothing leaks. No off-target reading of the 36 is above the bound under within-stage draws.

    7. Layer 2: predictive_orthonormal under the same draws (recorded; does not bear on the verdict)

    predictive_orthonormal__within_stage against pls_cv__within_stage qualifies:

    • S1 size holds in all three families. Its one simulation exceedance is the SEA-AD table's translation angle (11 of 100). On the fresh SEA-AD draws it reads 36 / 15 / 26: delta is above the bound (34) and at the ceiling (36), the one exceedance m = 3 allows.
    • S2 protected power holds with no loss below 0.025.
    • S3 specificity holds in both families: no δ = 2.0 reading above the bound, and no band reading in significant excess over production under the same draws. Six of its off-target readings over both families are above the bound.
    • G gain holds in 10 of the 18 δ = 2.0 target cells at 0.05 / 18, mostly orientation angle. For example the SEA-AD table's rotation e = 0.25, b / c = 0 / 51, and n = 200's rotation e = 0.1, 0 / 24. Recorded beside it: 30 of 36 band cells gain at p < 0.025, and none of the weak design's 9.

    The other two coordinate systems under the same draws: predictive__within_stage reads specificity_excess (S3), and predictive_fitted__within_stage size_excess (its fresh-anchor delta is 37 of 500, above the ceiling). Under the unrestricted draws predictive_orthonormal against pls_cv again reads size_excess: its fresh-anchor delta is 69 of 500, as on the pilot's draws (67).

    9. What follows

    • The verdict's consequence. The within-stage null is an opt-in option, documented as holding size, including where stage dispersion is unequal and where the unrestricted null fails, and as reading more off-target delta and shape on in-span rotations in the stage-effect band than production does. The unrestricted draws stay the default of RRPP, the harness and motco analyze. No case study reports within-stage p-values as its test, and the predictive coordinates stay opt-in diagnostics.
    • The verdict does not say the unrestricted null is right. On Gaussian data with the scores' dispersion pattern the unrestricted null is conservative (0.024–0.046 at 1,000 units), and with the predictive coordinates' pattern it is liberal. On the fresh SEA-AD draws the predictive coordinates still exceed under it (delta 69 of 500). The size question the diagnosis opened is answered for the null; specificity on rotations is what keeps it opt-in.
    • Questions this run leaves.
    • Whether the band leak belongs to the null or to the planted scale's rotations in a small band of designs. Every counted reading is at δ ≤ 1.0 under the planted scale, where production itself leaks.
    • Whether a null that is exact within stage can avoid trading angle for delta on rotations.
    • Whether the candidate's gain under the same draws survives a null that passes specificity. Its layer-2 reading is the strongest the predictive coordinates have had, so it is the one to re-read if a null qualifies.
    • The rule is not amended after the fact. The readings that failed were declared before any unit ran.

    10. Limitations

    • The anchor rates are conditional on one fit. All 1,000 anchor draws share one fit, as in both parents.
    • The simulation profile is pilot grade (100 × 199). The band criterion counts readings at 100 units, where one unit is 0.01.
    • The dispersion profile is a toy. It is isotropic Gaussian noise with no fit; it validates the null under one mechanism and does not account for the anchor.
    • The fresh draws are the first 500 unread ones. Production's own delta moved from 21 to 32 of 500 between the two halves, about 1.6 standard errors of the difference: a single block of 500 draws carries a binomial SE of about 5 counts, which is the margin the marginal readings in this report sit within.
    • df keeps its definition under strata, which over-corrects by √(n / (n − K)): 1.9 % at n = 80 and three stages, in the conservative direction (design.md D4).
    • The mechanism of the band leak is described, not tested.
