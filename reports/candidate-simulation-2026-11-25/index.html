    Simulation-only candidate comparison — report (2026-11-25)

    Change: simulation-only-candidate-comparison (MOTCO 0.27.0: the opt-in generator setting stage_dispersion_scale; roadmap open item 1 and Done items 17, 18 and 22). Reads: results/candidate-simulation-2026-11-25/ (candidate_simulation_decision.json; per profile settings.json, rate_table.csv, paired_table.csv, cell_table.csv, rank_table.csv, accounting.json; {committed,surgery,adni_design}/reproduction.json; PROVENANCE.txt, sacct.txt). Parents: the univariate baseline's stage_span and surgery profiles, the stage-effect scaling run's saturated, band and calibration profiles, and the ADNI gain-and-specificity run's simulation profile (their settings and rate tables, bound by sha256). Concept page: measurement visibility.

    Three measurement candidates were kept out of production by readings on real noise: predictive_orthonormal by the SEA-AD plasmode's size and ADNI's protected power, and block weighting and sparse stage-PLS by a pilot whose gain rule counted only the weak design and the SEA-AD plant. Neither export is the data MOTCO was designed for. On 2026-11-25 the user decided to re-read the candidates in simulation alone, across more conditions, with the real-noise readings kept as recorded limits and not as vetoes. This run does that on 58,400 simulated datasets, each read by every arm on the same draws, under a decision fixed before any unit ran.

    • The arms. pls_cv is production: stage-PLS scores at the CV rank. predictive_orthonormal measures the K − 1 stage-predictive directions of the same fit. weighted is its own CV fit on blocks scaled to unit total variance. sparse_block is its own CV fit with a feature budget per block, on three designs only.
    • The families. The decision reads each arm against production in five design families (core, weak_stage, four_stages, unbalanced, adni_design), so a candidate that helps at some designs and harms at others is described as such.
    • Two riders. A stage-dispersion criterion on null designs, and the incidental routes (feature-set surgeries and out-of-span rotations), which are labelled and never decide.

    In one paragraph.

    • The verdict is scoped_candidates. predictive_orthonormal and weighted each qualify in three of the five families. No arm qualifies everywhere, so no default change is proposed, and every arm stays opt-in.
    • The gate passed. Production reproduced every committed count it was compared with: 228 rows on committed, 66 on surgery and 60 on adni_design (both arms there), none differing.
    • Production holds size in every family and under unequal noise by stage. Across the 10 stage-dispersion designs it reads 2 of 60 readings above the bound and none above the ceiling. It loses nothing to a candidate anywhere: no arm loses protected power in any family.
    • predictive_orthonormal qualifies in core, weak_stage and four_stages.
      • The gains follow the rank. It gains where the CV rank exceeds K − 1. Where it does not, it measures production's subspace in another metric and reads like production. For example, angle at n = 200, δ = 0.5 and a 9° rotation goes from 0.080 to 1.000, and delta on the band's stretch at e = 1 from 0.460 to 0.995.
      • It fails specificity in two families. In unbalanced, one off-target angle reading is above the ceiling (0.110 against 0.100). At the ADNI design, stretches are read as shape (0.060 → 0.295), the failure known before the run.
    • weighted qualifies in core, four_stages and unbalanced. It gains 4–7 points on average there. It exceeds size in weak_stage (angle 0.090 on n320_delta1's translation, against a ceiling of 0.078) and gains nothing at the ADNI design.
    • sparse_block reads keep_production. At n200 it gains nothing, and on the band it adds off-target shape (0.050 → 0.135).
    • Both candidates fail size under unequal noise by stage, where production holds. The failing readings sit where the largest stage has the narrowest noise on the SEA-AD cell table. Item 20 found that pattern over-rejects on Gaussian latent matrices: here predictive_orthonormal reads up to 0.090 and weighted up to 0.100, against a ceiling of 0.084.
    • What it holds for. Simulated Gaussian noise on 658 features (at most about 8 per sample), three blocks, three or four stages. No real-noise reading entered the decision. The SEA-AD and ADNI readings stand as limits (§11), and size at many features per sample is unread (open item 15). No verdict changes what the harness or motco analyze runs.

    3. The decision

    • The readings. delta, angle and shape, each at p < 0.05. On adni_design the rule is p ≤ 0.05, the parent's.
    • The criteria, within a family. These are the predictive pilot's, with judge_rates and the exact paired tests imported unchanged:
      • S1, size. The anchors and translations under the count-plus-ceiling rule (at most the allowed count above the per-cell bound, 35 or more of 500, and none above the family's Bonferroni ceiling).
      • S2, protected power. The in-span target cells (stretch → delta, rotation at φ = 0 → angle, bend → shape) by a one-sided exact paired loss against pls_cv, under the count-plus-ceiling rule.
      • S3, specificity. The target cells' two off-target statistics. At δ = 2.0 designs the arm's own rates against the bound (17 or more of 200). Below δ = 2.0, where production's own off-target rates exceed it, the arm's paired excess over pls_cv.
      • G, gain. At least one target cell with an exact paired gain p-value at or below 0.05 divided by the family's target cells.
    • The label is the first that applies, in the order size_excess, power_loss, specificity_excess, no_gain, qualifies. A family is not_run for an arm that has no design in it, and partial when the arm has some of its designs. partial never counts toward qualifying everywhere.
    • D, stage dispersion. Each arm's 60 readings on the ten dispersion designs, under the count-plus-ceiling rule. If production failed, every candidate would instead be judged by its paired excess over pls_cv. Production holds (§8), so the rule applies as written.
    • I, incidental routes. Never decisive. Each candidate is labelled against pls_cv per cell on the 18 surgery target cells and the 20 out-of-span rotation cells, by one-sided exact paired tests at 0.025 / 38 with a margin of 0.10.
    • Per arm. qualifies_everywhere means every family is run and qualifies, and D holds. scoped_candidate means at least one family qualifies. Otherwise the arm is keep_production.
    • Overall. propose_default_change (one arm qualifying everywhere) or scoped_candidates or keep_production.

    12. What follows

    • The arms stay opt-in (design, Migration Plan, scoped_candidates). The measurement-visibility page carries the map by family (measurement visibility). motco analyze and the harness run what they ran.
    • A rule that recommends an arm from a cohort's design is a separate change. For predictive_orthonormal the map says what such a rule would need to read: whether production's CV rank exceeds K − 1 (where the gains are), whether the residual dispersion differs by stage with the narrowest noise in the largest stage (where size fails), and the off-target leak under unbalance and at ADNI's design.
    • The roadmap's reopening condition for predictive_orthonormal is amended as the user's decision of 2026-11-25: the simulation-only reading replaces the requirement of a real-noise anchor, and the SEA-AD and ADNI readings are restated as limits.
    • Production's size at four stages, on the SEA-AD table with three blocks and under stage-unequal dispersion is added to the roadmap's simulated conditions.
    • The roadmap records this run as Done item 28. Unbalance at equal dispersion was diagnosed in parallel as Done item 27 (small_cells), and its 5-per-stage designs are outside this run's families.
    • Still open. Size at many features per sample (open item 15), real multi-omic noise (open item 2), and the dispersion excess that within-group draws leave (open item 17).

    13. Limitations

    • Simulated Gaussian noise. Every reading holds at 658 features on three blocks, at most about 8 features per sample, three or four stages and no covariates outside the ADNI design. Neither the SEA-AD regime (125 features per donor) nor the target ROSMAP and ADNI multi-omic cohorts are read.
    • Two labels turn on one reading each. predictive_orthonormal's unbalanced label rests on one off-target reading above the ceiling (22 of 200 against 20). weighted's weak_stage label rests on n320_delta1, where production is at the bound on the same units. The rule was fixed before any unit ran and is applied as written.
    • 17 units failed the planted fixed point, 16 of them at four stages. Their cells' rates rest on 190–199 converged units, and a unit that fails to converge may differ from one that converges.
    • sparse_block ran on three designs. Its verdict could not exceed scoped_candidate, and its dispersion reading is not run.
    • The dispersion profile reads size only. Power under unequal noise by stage is not read.
    • The within-stage draws are recorded, not judged, and they cost specificity in the band (item 20).
