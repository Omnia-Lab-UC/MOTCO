    The ADNI real-noise anchor — report (2026-11-17)

    Change: adni-real-noise-anchor (MOTCO 0.25.0, no package change). Reads: results/adni-real-noise-anchor-2026-11-17/ (adni_anchor_decision.json, rate_table.csv, paired_table.csv, point_table.csv, accounting.json, preflight.json, settings.json, PROVENANCE.txt). Parents: the within-stage permutation pilot (its SEA-AD anchor tables, bound by sha256), the predictive size-excess diagnosis and the predictive-subspace pilot. Concept page: measurement visibility.

    Every real-noise reading MOTCO had came from one export, SEA-AD astrocytes at 80 donors on 10,000 features. On its anchor predictive_orthonormal rejected delta in 67 of 500 replicates where production rejected 21, and that veto kept the predictive subspace an opt-in diagnostic (roadmap item 18). The diagnosis (item 19) traced the excess to a stage fit that is mostly in-sample, read under RRPP's unrestricted draws, and predicted that it would shrink as participants per feature grow and be absent on ADNI. This change reads that prediction, and production's size, on ADNI's own blocks, under a decision fixed before any unit ran.

    In one paragraph. The verdict is excess_absent.

    • Size holds for both operating arms everywhere. At 80, 200 and 500 participants subsampled within cells and at the full cohort of 1,501, neither production (pls_cv) nor predictive_orthonormal has a reading above the bound (34 of 500) under RRPP's unrestricted draws. Their largest counts are 32 and 29 of 500.
    • The consequence fixed with the rule. SEA-AD's excess does not transfer to ADNI. The size part of open item 1's second reopening condition is met for ADNI. predictive_orthonormal stays an opt-in diagnostic until the gain and specificity readings at ADNI's design, which are the next change.
    • The fixed-rank grade is reference_exceeds. Forced to rank 8 or 19, the scores themselves exceed at n = 200 (shape 41 and 38 of 500; 41 is above the family's ceiling of 40). So the predictive arms carry no grade, and this run does not say whether the hold is owed to ADNI's noise or to the low ranks its CV selects.
    • Recorded beside the verdict. Production under the within-stage draws has no size scope: at the full cohort it rejects delta 35 and angle 38 of 500, above that point's ceiling of 36. predictive_orthonormal under the within-stage draws holds everywhere.

    1. Configuration and provenance

    • Export. adni_sexdiff/results/motco_input, read in place through ADNI_CONTRACT and never copied: 1,501 participants, the p180 (133) and nmr (165) metabolite blocks, Female 237 / 310 / 125 and Male 223 / 448 / 158 at CN / MCI / AD. Its five file hashes are in settings.json and were unchanged after the run.
    • Design points (500 replicates × 999 permutations each, rejection at p ≤ 0.05):

    A subsampled unit draws its participants within cells and has its own fit. The cohort's 500 units share one fit and differ only in the labels, as SEA-AD's anchor does. - The null. Each unit permutes the sex labels within stage and leaves blocks and covariates on their rows. No analysis ran under the real labels. - Arms. Each arm is one analyze_trajectories call at the case study's configuration (five covariates, rank floor 2, net displacement, the harness CV defaults) with the df residual correction, all at the unit's RRPP seed. - Operating: pls_cv (the cross-validated fit; at the cohort, forced at rank 8) and predictive_orthonormal on the same fit, each also under the within-stage draws (__within_stage). - Fixed rank: the same four combinations forced at ranks 8 and 19. Rank 8 is not repeated at the cohort. - Control: pls_cv__uncorrected, production without the df correction.

    A subsampled unit holds 13 arms and one cross-validation; a cohort unit holds 9 and none. - Code and host. Revision 2099db4 on proposal/adni-real-noise-anchor, pushed before any unit ran, from a clean detached worktree. The workstation, 7 workers, Python 3.11.15, numpy 2.3.5, scipy 1.16.3, scikit-learn 1.8.0, pandas 2.3.3, BLAS pinned to one thread. - Compute. 8.12 recorded core-hours against an approved cap of 10. A service restart on the workstation killed the first launch at 658 units; their records were complete, and the resumed run evaluated the other 1,342 under the same settings and seeds (PROVENANCE.txt).

    2. Accounting and the pre-flight

    • Units. 2,000 of 2,000 complete: no failed unit, no failed arm, no inconsistent unit and no duplicate. No subsample needed a redraw for an absent covariate level, and no label draw reproduced the real labels.
    • Checks. On every unit each forced arm reported its rank, every arm fitted at one rank had the same model, each predictive arm measured two dimensions, each within-stage arm and the control observed exactly their counterpart's statistics, and the realized cell table was the point's.
    • Pre-flight: passed. On cohort replicates 0 and 1 the cross-validated fit selected rank 8 at a held-out stage AUROC of 0.599514, the values of adni_sexdiff's primary record (MOTCO 0.10.0), and the call forced at rank 8 gave identical statistics and p-values. The export, the row order and the environment reproduce the case study's fit.

    3. The decision

    Rule version 1 (design.md D8–D11).

    • Size scope. An arm is judged on the ladder's upper sets (all four points; n200 and above; n500 and above; the cohort alone) by the count-plus-ceiling rule over each set's delta, angle and shape readings as one family. Its scope is the first set that holds.
    • Verdict. Read on the operating arms under the unrestricted draws: gate_failed, then production_excess (production's scope does not start at n80), then excess_persists (the candidate has no scope), then excess_scoped, else excess_absent.
    arm all four points (m = 12) n200 and above (9) n500 and above (6) cohort (3) scope
    pls_cv 0 above the bound 0 0 0 n80
    predictive_orthonormal 0 above the bound 0 0 0 n80
    pls_cv__within_stage (recorded) 2 above (allowed 1) 2 2, one above the ceiling 2, one above the ceiling none
    predictive_orthonormal__within_stage (recorded) 0 above the bound 0 0 0 n80

    The verdict is excess_absent. The gate passed and the decision is at paper grade (not indicative). The consequence fixed with the rule: "predictive_orthonormal holds size on ADNI's blocks at every design point under RRPP's unrestricted draws: SEA-AD's excess does not transfer to ADNI. The size part of open item 1's second condition is met for ADNI. It stays an opt-in diagnostic until the gain and specificity readings at ADNI's design, the next change. The fixed-rank grade says whether the hold is owed to ADNI's noise or to the ranks its CV selects. In every verdict the SEA-AD anchor's reading stands for SEA-AD's design, and keep_production stands."

    5. The within-stage arms (recorded beside the verdict)

    • pls_cv__within_stage has no size scope. At the cohort it rejects delta 35 and angle 38 of 500, both above the bound and angle above the ceiling of 36 for a one-point family. Paired against the unrestricted draws on the same units, the within-stage draws reject more at the larger points: delta 0 units against 10 at the cohort (two-sided p = 0.002) and 0 against 8 at n500 (p = 0.008).
    • predictive_orthonormal__within_stage holds everywhere, its largest count 33 (n500 angle).
    • What the rule does with it. Nothing for the verdict, which reads the unrestricted draws (design D9). The documentation of the opt-in null records the failing readings (measurement visibility). This change explains no failure.

    8. What follows

    • The size part of open item 1's second reopening condition is met for ADNI. predictive_orthonormal stays an opt-in diagnostic. The next change reads its gain over production at ADNI's design in simulation and its specificity there, in simulation and on plants in a held-out reference span (open item 2's next slice), each under its own predeclared decision.
    • The veto stands for SEA-AD's design. By the scoping rule (S1–S5, written into the decision), a size reading speaks for its export and design points, and cohorts do not vouch for one another. keep_production stands.
    • Production has a second real-noise size reading. It holds on ADNI's blocks at every point, at the case study's configuration with df and, descriptively, without it.
    • Production's size at a forced rank. At the ranks its CV does not select on ADNI, the scores exceed at n200. The roadmap records this (the grade's statement). No case study forces a rank, and the cross-validated production arm holds.
    • The within-stage null fails at ADNI's cohort. Its failing readings join the opt-in null's documentation.
    • Item 19's mechanism is not tested on ADNI. The grade could not say whether an over-fitted fit on ADNI's noise reproduces SEA-AD's excess. Reading that needs a reference that holds at the forced ranks.

    9. Limitations

    • Subsamples of one cohort are not independent cohorts. Every rate is conditional on ADNI's 1,501 participants, and at n500 two subsamples share about a third of their participants. Independent real noise needs ROSMAP.
    • The cohort's 500 units share one fit. Its rates, the within-stage arm's failure included, are conditional on that fit.
    • At small n the CV mostly selects rank 2, where the candidate measures the scores' own subspace. The rates by rank class show the candidate holding above rank 2 as well, on fewer units.
    • The family rule has a false-alarm rate. A correctly sized arm fails the ladder family about 7 % of the time (design D8), and two arms decide. The verdict does not rest on a reading near the ceiling: both operating arms have no reading above the bound.
    • The fixed ranks are not what a case study runs. Their grade characterizes a mechanism and cannot change the verdict.
    • One omic type. ADNI's blocks are metabolites at 0.2 to 3.7 features per participant; SEA-AD's were RNA and ATAC at 125. Nothing here says what SEA-AD's blocks add.
