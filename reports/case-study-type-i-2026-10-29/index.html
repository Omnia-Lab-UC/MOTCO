    Case-study configuration Type I check — 2026-10-29

    Paper-grade size check of the real-data analysis (motco analyze, MOTCO 0.12.0) at the SEA-AD case study's configuration — the rank floor of 2, six numeric covariate design columns, a weak stage signal and a nuisance covariate the covariate-blind basis follows (openspec/changes/case-study-configuration-type-i/, spec case-study-configuration-validation). It is the measurement the case-study section cites for its primary analysis and for its stage latent-adjusted sensitivity arm. Every number below is read from results/case-study-type-i-2026-10-29/ (delta0.1/ and delta0.05/: case_study_type_i_decision.json, case_study_type_i_rates.csv, case_study_type_i_replicates.csv, case_study_type_i_pvalues.csv, merge.json, settings.json, PROVENANCE.txt; case_study_type_i_overall_decision.json; calibration/calibration.json; smoke-delta0.1-sz10/, smoke-delta0.05-sz10/; merged.jsonl and records/ stay on ing as each PROVENANCE.txt records). Rates are rejection rates at p ≤ 0.05 over the R = 500 available replicates at 999 permutations; the bound is the one-sided α + 2·√(α(1 − α)/R) = 0.0695, i.e. at most 34 rejections in 500.

    Run: two 50-shard SLURM arrays on ing (partition 512x1024, n11 excluded) from the clean worktree of feat/case-study-configuration-type-i at ffd6249 (tree hash ececcb83…, identical to the workstation): job 930122 for δ = 0.1 (50 / 50 tasks completed, 15.2 core-hours) and job 930172 for δ = 0.05, whose stalled and slow shards were moved twice (jobs 930224 and 930266; 35.4 recorded core-hours, about 15 of them productive). 1,000 units per strength (the zero-effect anchor and the translation control at e = 1.0 × 500 replicates), three analyses per unit, 6,000 analyses in all, 0 arm failures, every latent_stage arm available.

    Decision: diagnose_configuration_excess at δ = 0.1 and primary_validated at δ = 0.05, hence diagnose_configuration_excess under the predeclared conjunction (case_study_type_i_overall_decision.json). The adjusted arm — the case study's primary — holds the bound for every statistic at both controls at δ = 0.05 and for five of six at δ = 0.1, exceeding it on the translation control's delta by two rejections (36 / 500 = 0.072 against 0.0695). The latent_stage arm — the case study's sensitivity arm and the predeclared fallback — exceeds the bound at the anchor on angle (0.090 / 0.084) and shape (0.092 / 0.078) at both strengths, so it is not admissible either.

    In one paragraph. At the Phase 6 SEA-AD design with the per-omic stage effect weakened to δ = 0.1 and 0.05 and a numeric covariate z confounded with group, stage and their interaction injected at s_z = 10 — calibrated so that the covariate-adjusted analysis reads a held-out CV stage AUROC of 0.70 / 0.69 (the cohorts read 0.64–0.70) and the covariate-blind basis spends its first component on z (median |Spearman| 0.92; the real basis reads 0.84) — each of 1,000 units per strength was analysed three times on the same data, CV folds and permutation draws at the rank floor of 2 with the net-displacement estimator: with z and five pure-noise covariates in the trajectory model (adjusted), with no covariates (unadjusted) and with the six covariates under the stage latent adjustment (latent_stage). The unadjusted arm confirms that the construction confounds (anchor delta 0.672 / 0.686, shape 0.252 / 0.222). The adjusted model removes almost all of it: anchor delta 0.068 / 0.064, angle 0.048 / 0.048, shape 0.056 / 0.054, translation delta 0.072 / 0.060, angle 0.042 / 0.044, shape 0.068 / 0.068. Its delta sits at or just above the bound in every reading (0.066 pooled over the four control readings, 34–36 rejections where 34 are allowed) and its shape at the bound at translation; angle is comfortably under it but its RRPP null is wide (median q95 109–113°). The stage latent adjustment removes the nuisance axis from the basis (|ρ| 0.08) at the price of a heavier rank tail (up to 11), a lower AUROC (0.66 / 0.62) and anchor angle and shape rates of 0.078–0.092. Under the rule written before the run, the case study's primary is withheld pending diagnosis of a small delta inflation at six design columns and n = 80, and its stage sensitivity arm's angle and shape rejections are not interpretable at this configuration.

    1. Construction and calibration

    The check composes two validated constructions by import (scripts/case_study_type_i_check.py, design D1):

    • The D10 covariate construction (scripts/covariate_type_i_check.py, covariate Type I report): a null semi-synthetic dataset at the Phase 6 small-n design (three stages, group_stage_sizes = [[11, 10, 28], [9, 10, 12]], n = 80, methylation + expression, joint magnitude), a numeric covariate z = 0.5·g + 1·s + 2·g·s + ε and its effect injected into every feature along a fixed direction over 20 % of the joint features, X_f += s_z·sd_f·w_f·z, on the M-value scale. The three-level categorical covariate D10 draws stays in the RNG stream but is neither declared nor injected (s_c = 0): the case study enters six numerics and no factor, and without a factor no CV training set can lose a level (design D2).
    • The weakened stage signal of the rank-floor check (report): one --delta applied to delta_methyl, delta_expr and delta_protein, with p_dmp at the Phase 6 value 0.1 so the translation surgery keeps its pool headroom.

    Beside z the check declares five pure-noise covariates x1–x5 (i.i.d. N(0, 1) from their own seed, never injected), so the trajectory model carries six numeric design columns, as the case study's does. Cells are the zero-effect anchor and the translation negative control at e = 1.0 (design D4). Seeds depend on the replicate index only, so both cells and all three arms of a replicate share the baseline draw, the covariate values and the RRPP seed; the adjusted and unadjusted latent spaces are asserted identical per unit (they share the covariate-blind basis), so an adjusted excess is attributable to the trajectory model and an unadjusted excess to confounding. All three arms run at min_components=2, the harness CV knobs (3 × 4 folds, 5 repeats, max_components 20, random_state 1203), 999 permutations and the net-displacement estimator.

    Calibration

    Smoke profiles

    smoke-delta0.1-sz10/ and smoke-delta0.05-sz10/ (R = 20, 99 permutations, both cells, three arms): 40 / 40 units at each strength, 0 arm failures, the latent_stage arm available in every unit. Both smoke decisions were primary_validated; the latent_stage arm's anchor shape rate of 0.35 (7 / 20) at both strengths was recorded in the design as the warning sign to carry into the paper-grade run, and the paper-grade run confirmed an excess on shape (0.092 / 0.078) and on angle (0.090 / 0.084) at R = 500. One unit took 57 s locally at 999 permutations and 47–55 s on an ing EPYC core.

    3. What the arms show

    The construction confounds, and the trajectory model removes almost all of it. The unadjusted arm rejects delta in two thirds of the null units and shape in a quarter; the adjusted arm, on the identical latent scores (asserted per unit), brings delta to 0.060–0.072, shape to 0.054–0.068 and angle to 0.042–0.048. The covariate Type I check of 2026-10-16 measured the same model at three design columns (z plus the two indicator columns of a three-level factor), a saturated stage signal (AUROC 1.000), the unfloored rank rule and a nuisance the basis did not follow (|ρ| 0.64): adjusted delta 0.040, angle 0.062, shape 0.034. Here, at six columns, the case study's AUROC and a basis that follows the nuisance, delta reads 0.064–0.072 in every control reading, at or above the 34-rejection limit, and shape reads 0.068 at translation in both runs; angle is lower than in 2026-10-16. The excess that trips the rule — two rejections on one control at one strength — is one Monte Carlo SE wide, but the pooled delta of 0.066 over 2,000 units, every reading above α, is what the diagnosis should take as its starting point: a small delta inflation of the covariate-adjusted model at six design columns and n = 80, which the three-column check did not show.

    The adjusted arm's angle null is wide. The median q95 of the RRPP angle null is 109° (δ = 0.1) and 113° (δ = 0.05) in the adjusted arm against 36° in the unadjusted arm on the same basis and 57–59° in the latent_stage arm (case_study_type_i_replicates.csv, angle_null_q95). With six covariates in the reduced model the permuted residuals carry little stage structure, so the permuted net displacements point almost anywhere; angle is size-controlled at this configuration but, as the design-guidance δ arm found at δ ≤ 0.1, it has no power to spend.

    The stage latent adjustment is inadmissible at this configuration. It does what it is built to do — the first component's |ρ| with z falls from 0.92 to 0.08 — and its translation control holds (0.048–0.064), but at the anchor it rejects angle 0.090 / 0.084 and shape 0.092 / 0.078, about three Monte Carlo SE above α in each of the four readings. Beside it, the basis changes: the held-out AUROC falls to 0.659 / 0.622 (from 0.702 / 0.688), the CV rank's tail lengthens (rank ≥ 4 in 91 / 500 and 61 / 500 anchor units against 6 / 500 and 1 / 500 in the adjusted arm; maximum 11), and the smallest training-set residual df inside the CV is 31 (cv_min_training_df; full-sample residual_df 71: n − 1 − 2 stage indicators − 6 covariates). The 2026-10-20 latent-adjustment check measured the same conditioning at the unfloored rule, eight covariates including a factor and a saturated stage signal (δ = 2.0): stage held delta 0.031, angle 0.067, shape 0.058 against 0.0706, while its covariate-blind baseline arm rejected shape at 0.078. The excess here therefore belongs to the weak-signal, floor-2, six-numeric regime, not to the conditioning as such; the per-training-set residualization at 31 residual df and the rank tail are the two things to look at first.

    Rank and signal. The adjusted arm selects rank 2 in 366 / 500 (δ = 0.1) and 407 / 500 (δ = 0.05) anchor units and rank 3 in most of the rest; no unit selected rank 1 (the floor binds). The rank-floor check at the same strengths without covariates measured the floor-2 arm's anchor at delta 0.044 / 0.042, angle 0.060 / 0.054 and shape 0.038 / 0.036 (δ = 0.1 / 0.05), so the covariate-adjusted model, not the floor, carries the delta and shape readings here.

    4. Decision, consequence and what to diagnose

    Decision, read verbatim from results/case-study-type-i-2026-10-29/case_study_type_i_overall_decision.json: "decision": "diagnose_configuration_excess", "admissible": [], runs delta0.1 → diagnose_configuration_excess and delta0.05 → primary_validated. The rule, written before the run (proposal, design D5): primary_validated when the adjusted arm holds every statistic at both controls in every run; otherwise adopt_latent_stage when the latent_stage arm does; otherwise diagnose_configuration_excess. The adjusted arm fails the conjunction on one reading (translation delta at δ = 0.1, 36 / 500) and the latent_stage arm fails on four (anchor angle and shape at both strengths).

    Consequence for the case study. Under diagnose_configuration_excess the primary analysis of sead_sexdiff (covariates in the trajectory model, floor 2, net displacement, 999 permutations) is withheld pending diagnosis: it does not rest on a validated size at its configuration, and it cannot move to the stage latent adjustment, which is not admissible here either. Concretely for the readers of its results, until the diagnosis is done: the primary's delta p-values should be read against a realized size of about 0.066 rather than 0.05 (a p-value near 0.05 is not a rejection at the nominal level; a p-value well below it is unaffected in kind); its angle and shape p-values are size-controlled in this check (0.042–0.068), with angle's null so wide that a non-rejection is uninformative; and the stage sensitivity arm's angle and shape rejections are not interpretable at this configuration. Nothing in sead_sexdiff changes under this report; the diagnosis is its own change there and here.

    What to diagnose (named by the rule; none of it is done here):

    1. The adjusted arm's delta (0.060–0.072, pooled 0.066). Candidates, in the order they should be excluded: the degrees-of-freedom cost of six columns at n = 80 (the same seeds and construction at one, three and six noise columns, which isolates the column count from the confounding); the LS-mean reference row and the RRPP reduced model under covariates at these cell sizes (the design's reference_row is the pooled mean; the reduced model keeps the covariates); and the bound itself at R = 500 (a replication at R = 1,000 on the same seed family would halve the SE, at about 30 core-hours per strength).
    2. The latent_stage arm's anchor angle and shape (0.078–0.092). Candidates: the per-training-set nuisance coefficients at cv_min_training_df 31 (the residualized CV rank's tail reaches 11, and angle/shape at rank ≥ 4 should be tabulated against rank 2–3); and the interaction of the residualized basis with the rank floor, which the 2026-10-20 check never measured (it ran at floor 1).

    5. Limitations and scope

    Four limitations the spec requires the report to carry, then the scope:

    • Astrocyte primary cohort sizes only. The check uses the astrocyte cohort_30 cell sizes [[11, 10, 28], [9, 10, 12]] (n = 80) that every Phase 6 check uses; the microglia cohort (n = 76) and the other cohorts' tables were not run, and size at their cell sizes is inferred, not measured.
    • Additive-linear covariate effects. z enters the features additively along one fixed direction and the trajectory model linearly; non-linear or interaction effects of real covariates on the features are outside the construction.
    • One confounded covariate beside five independent ones. The confounding rests on z alone; the five noise covariates are i.i.d. normals, uncorrelated with one another and with the design, unlike age, education, PMI, RIN and library sizes. The configuration matched is the column count, the floor and the nuisance regime, not the covariates' joint distribution (design D2 records the alternatives considered).
    • Structural-analogue blocks. The blocks are InterSIM-calibrated methylation and expression analogues (498 measured features), not the case study's TMM log2-CPM blocks of 5,000 features each; the per-omic δ is a generator strength, and the regime is matched on the realized AUROC and |ρ|, not on the feature space.

    Scope:

    • No power is claimed at this configuration (the design-guidance pilot's δ arm measured none at δ ≤ 0.1), and the group_stage conditioning, a floor-1 arm and the microglia sizes were not run.
    • No change under src/motco/: every record field the check reads exists in 0.12.0, so the release and the case study's pin are untouched (design D9).
    • The two strengths are independent runs with their own decision files; the conjunction rule and the number of runs were fixed with the user after calibration and before submission (design D3 record). The δ = 0.05 run's shards were moved twice between nodes (PROVENANCE.txt); shards are resumable and a unit recorded in any shard file is never re-run, so the move changes which node computed a unit and nothing else.
    • The replicate-level tables (case_study_type_i_replicates.csv, case_study_type_i_pvalues.csv) are committed for both runs; merged.jsonl and records/ stay on ing under the worktree named in each PROVENANCE.txt, and case_study_type_i_check.py merge regenerates every table from records/.
