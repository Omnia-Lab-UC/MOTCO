    Unbalance at equal dispersion — report (2026-11-25)

    Change: unbalance-equal-dispersion-diagnosis (roadmap open item 16; no package change, MOTCO 0.26.0). Reads: results/unbalance-diagnosis-2026-11-25/ (unbalance_diagnosis_decision.json, settings.json, rate_table.csv, cell_table.csv, within_group_table.csv, corrected_table.csv, accounting.json, reproduction.json, PROVENANCE.txt, sacct.txt). Parent: the group-dispersion run's columns profile and decision (its settings, rate, cell and within-group tables and group_dispersion_decision.json, bound by sha256). Concept page: measurement visibility.

    The group-dispersion run read unbalance_excess on one reading of 24. At equal dispersion, delta at n = 80 with a first-group share of 0.8 rejected 44 times of 500 against a ceiling of 40. That design leaves 5 samples per stage in the smaller group, so it is both the smallest and the most unbalanced design read, and the run could not say which of the two mattered. By the predeclared order its three dispersion families were reported and not interpreted. This run asks whether the reading replicates and whether it follows small cells, unbalance or both. Every unit is an equal-dispersion null unit.

    • The designs. The committed n80_r0.8 design, rerun at its name and seeds as a gate, and seven new designs at 1,000 replicates that cross the smaller group's cell size (5, 7, 9, 13 per stage) with balance and total sample size.
    • The decision, fixed before any unit ran, judges delta, angle and shape in three families and takes the first that fails in a fixed order: balanced small cells, then a small group in an unbalanced design, then n = 80 at 9 or 13 per stage. It also fixes how the group-dispersion families are re-read under each verdict.

    In one paragraph.

    • The verdict is small_cells, on one reading. balanced_small fails: angle at n = 42 with 7 samples per stage in both groups rejects 76 times of 1,000, against a ceiling of 65. delta (54) and shape (47) hold there.
    • Unbalance is not needed, and the committed reading did not recur.
      • Every reading with a small group in an unbalanced design holds (unbalanced_small, 12 of 12), at 5 samples per stage as at 7.
      • So does every reading at n = 80 with 9 or 13 per stage (reference, 6 of 6).
      • A fresh replicate of the failing design reads delta at 45 of 1,000, and so does a 5-per-stage group inside n = 200 (44). Pooled with the rerun, delta at n80_r0.8 reads 89 of 1,500 (0.059).
    • The re-read gives dispersion_excess. The five group-dispersion designs with a 5-per-stage group are set aside. Unbalance at equal dispersion then holds (21 readings, none above the ceiling). The balanced dispersion family fails as it did (20 of 42 above the ceiling).
    • What that permits. Within readings at 13 samples per stage or more in every cell, a difference in noise dispersion alone takes the trajectory statistics above their size, with balanced groups too. A rejection there is evidence of a difference in trajectory or in dispersion.
    • The recorded arms. The omnibus holds in every family, and so do the df and leverage corrections and the per-feature tests. At n = 42 the angle excess is shared by PCA at the same rank (77), the within-group draws (70) and the split test's span (68). The df correction reads 62.
    • The gate passed. The rerun reproduces all 22 committed rows exactly. 7,500 of 7,500 units completed, with no inconsistent refit, in 35.4 recorded core-hours against the approved cap of 80.
    • What it holds for. Simulated Gaussian noise at equal dispersion, 658 features on three blocks, δ = 2.0, three stages and no covariates. No balanced design below 7 samples per stage was read. The re-read rule was written after the group-dispersion families' rates were known. No verdict changes what motco analyze runs.

    3. The decision

    • The readings. delta, angle and shape, each at p < 0.05, on the anchor of every new design. The rerun design enters no family.
    • The rule. judge_rates of the stage-span paper grade, imported unchanged. A family holds when its readings above the per-cell bound (0.0638 at 1,000 replicates, so 64 or more) stay within the count a run with exact size stays within, and none is above the family's Bonferroni ceiling. A reading with fewer than 900 available units would be unavailable and fail its family; none was.
    • The verdict is the first that applies: reproduction_failed, small_cells (balanced_small fails), small_unbalanced_cells (unbalanced_small fails), moderate_excess (reference fails), not_replicated. Every family is recorded whatever the verdict.

    • balanced_small is the easiest family to fail. It has three readings, so its ceiling is the Bonferroni one for m = 3; one reading above it fails the family. The count is 76 against 65.

    • Its scope. Readings at 13 samples per stage or more in every cell: n = 200, 320 and 500 at every share read, and n = 80 with balanced groups.
    • What it was foreseen to give. The design noted that balanced_unequal fails at 33 or more samples per stage, so under small_cells or small_unbalanced_cells the re-read was bound to give dispersion_excess. What was not known in advance was this run's verdict.

    • What it does not change. The group-dispersion files are not written to; their verdict stays unbalance_excess as committed, and this decision carries the re-read.

    10. Limitations

    • Simulated Gaussian noise at one stage effect. Every reading holds at 658 simulated features on three blocks, equal group dispersion, δ = 2.0 (held-out AUROC 1.000), three stages and no covariates. A weaker stage effect, four stages and covariates were not read. A cohort's own table is read at its own design: SEA-AD's held size at its own design under the df correction (roadmap items 7 (c) and 8). What carries over from this run is only the cell size at which a simulated design at δ = 2.0 loses size.
    • No balanced design below 7 per stage. A balanced design at 5 per stage (n = 30) cannot run under the production cross-validation. Whether balanced groups would hold at 5 per stage is unread.
    • The verdict rests on one reading. balanced_small fails on one of its three readings, by 76 against a ceiling of 65. The rule was fixed before any unit ran and is applied as written.
    • The re-read rule was written after the group-dispersion rates were known, and its outcome under this verdict was foreseeable (§8).
    • The design set was chosen after the committed rates were seen. The rule is the standing size rule, with no threshold taken from them, and not_replicated and moderate_excess were both outcomes it could return.
    • Size only. Every unit is a null unit; power at these designs is not read.
    • The recorded arms are readings on refits at the recorded rank. Their observed statistics equal production's on every unit, and their labels decide nothing.
