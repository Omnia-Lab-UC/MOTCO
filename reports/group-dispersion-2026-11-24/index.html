    Group-specific dispersion — report (2026-11-24)

    Change: group-specific-dispersion (MOTCO 0.26.0: the opt-in generator setting group_dispersion_scale; roadmap open item 8). Reads: results/group-dispersion-2026-11-24/ (group_dispersion_decision.json; per profile settings.json, rate_table.csv, cell_table.csv, within_group_table.csv, accounting.json; core/reproduction.json; PROVENANCE.txt, sacct.txt). Parents: the univariate baseline's stage_span profile and the stage-effect scaling run's band profile (their settings, rate tables and cell tables, bound by sha256). Concept page: measurement visibility.

    RRPP permutes the reduced model's residuals over all samples, so each draw gives both groups the pooled noise. The null therefore assumes that the two groups share one noise dispersion. This run asks whether the trajectory test holds its size when they do not. Every unit is a null unit: the groups share their trajectory and differ only in the spread of their noise and in their size.

    • The setting. s multiplies the second group's noise standard deviation in every feature, block and stage, about unchanged means: s ∈ {1.25, 1.5, 2}, covariance multipliers 1.56, 2.25 and 4.
    • The shares. The first group's share is 0.5 (balanced), 0.65 or 0.8 (the wider group is the smaller), or 0.35 or 0.2 (the wider group is the larger). Equal dispersion at shares 0.65 and 0.8 reads unbalance alone.
    • The decision, fixed before any unit ran, judges delta, angle and shape in four families and takes the first that fails in a fixed order: unbalance at equal dispersion, then balanced designs with a scale, then unbalanced designs with a scale.

    In one paragraph.

    • The verdict is unbalance_excess, on one reading. At equal dispersion with unequal groups, 23 of the 24 readings stay within their ceiling. The exception is delta at n = 80 with share 0.8, where the smaller group has 5 samples per stage: 44 rejections of 500, against a ceiling of 40 (the committed balanced design reads 30). Production's omnibus holds in this family, and so do the per-feature arms.
    • What the verdict sets. By the predeclared order, unbalance at equal dispersion is diagnosed first, as its own change. The three dispersion families are reported and not interpreted.
    • The dispersion families all fail, and far from the bound (reported, §5).
      • Balanced: 20 of 42 readings are above the ceiling. delta is above it from s = 1.25 at n = 200 (86 and 93 of 500) and from s = 1.5 at every other column (124–444). angle and shape stay within it at δ ≥ 0.5, apart from one reading each at s = 2, and fail in the weak-stage band.
      • Wider group the smaller: 48 of 60 are above. All three statistics fail by s = 1.5 at every column; at n = 200, share 0.8 and s = 2, delta / angle / shape read 412 / 94 / 118.
      • Wider group the larger: 19 of 60 are above: fifteen delta readings and four angle and shape readings in the band. At δ ≥ 0.5 angle and shape turn conservative (25 readings flagged).
    • The within-group draws (recorded) bring angle and shape back where the wider group is the smaller. They leave balanced delta where it was (332 → 319 of 500 at n = 200, s = 2), and raise delta where the wider group is the larger (109 → 223).
    • The per-feature tests (recorded) hold where the groups are balanced or the wider group is the larger. They fail where the wider group is the smaller: fdr reads 134 of 500 at n = 200, share 0.8 and s = 1.25, and 498 at s = 2.
    • The gate passed. The rerun of n200 reproduces all 36 committed rows exactly; 32,000 of 32,000 units completed; 186.8 recorded core-hours against the approved cap of 400.
    • What it holds for. Simulated Gaussian noise at 658 features, one scale on every feature of one group, three stages and no covariates. A difference in correlation structure was not simulated, and no real cohort's dispersion was read. No verdict changes what motco analyze runs.

    10. What follows

    • Unbalance at equal dispersion is diagnosed first, as its own change (design, Migration Plan). The roadmap opens it (open item 16). It starts from one reading: delta at n = 80 with 5 samples per stage in the smaller group, 0.088 against a ceiling of 0.080, beside a committed balanced 0.060 at the same n. It needs to say whether that reading is a property of small cells, of unbalance or of both, and whether it carries to a case study's table. SEA-AD's smallest cell holds 9 donors (F 11 / 10 / 28 against M 9 / 10 / 12). It read size at its own design under the df correction (roadmap items 7 (c) and 8).
    • The dispersion families are reported, not interpreted. The documentation states their rates and map with that status (measurement visibility). The interpretability statement gains no dispersion clause. It records that the null assumes one dispersion for both groups and that the reading waits on the diagnosis. Once the diagnosis reads, the families of this run can be re-read under the next verdict in the order, without new units.
    • The recorded arms say how far the per-feature tests share the reading. They hold where the groups are balanced and where the wider group is the larger. They fail where the wider group is the smaller.
    • The roadmap's "Not yet established" entries on unbalance and on noise now cite this run.
    • What does not change. motco analyze runs what it ran; no default moves; group_dispersion_scale stays an opt-in generator setting.
    • Still open. A dispersion that differs by feature, by stage or in correlation structure, and the same reading on a real cohort (roadmap open items 2 and 15).

    11. Limitations

    • Simulated Gaussian noise, one scale. Every reading holds at 658 simulated features with one scale applied to every feature of one group, three stages and no covariates. A difference in correlation structure, which acts in the scores as a dispersion scale but not a uniform one (design D3), was not simulated. No real cohort's dispersion was read, and neither case study rejects.
    • The verdict rests on one reading. unbalanced_equal fails on a single reading above its ceiling, at the smallest cells read. The rule was fixed before any unit ran and is applied as written.
    • The rule followed the probes. The probes saw rejection rates at a scale of 2. They chose the scales, the columns and the recorded arm, not the rule's thresholds (§9).
    • The columns read s ≥ 1.5. Below that only n = 200 at δ = 2.0 is read, so a column's first failing scale of 1.5 is an upper bound.
    • Size only. No unit carries a trajectory difference, so neither the power a conservative reading costs nor power under unequal dispersion is read.
    • The within-group arm is a reading on a refit at the recorded rank. Its observed statistics equal production's on every unit, but its draws assume equal dispersion across the stages of a group, the mirror of the within-stage null's assumption.
