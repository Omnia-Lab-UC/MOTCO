    Rank-floor check — 2026-10-23

    Paper-grade size and power check of the PLS latent-space rank floor (min_components=2, MOTCO 0.10.0) against the unfloored 0.9.0 rule at the Phase 6 SEA-AD design (openspec/changes/archive/2026-10-23-minimum-latent-rank/, design D7, spec rank-floor-validation). It is the gate behind the default flip: motco analyze and the simulation harness select the PLS rank from candidates at or above 2 only once this check names the floor-2 arm admissible. Every number below is read from results/rank-floor-2026-10-23/ (delta0.05/ and delta0.1/: rank_floor_decision.json, rank_floor_rates.csv, rank_floor_replicates.csv, rank_floor_pvalues.csv, merged.jsonl, the last three kept on ing as PROVENANCE.txt records; calibration/; smoke-delta0.05/, smoke-delta0.1/). Rates are rejection rates at p ≤ 0.05 over the available replicates of 500, at 999 permutations.

    Run: two SLURM arrays of 100 shards on ing (partition 512x1024): job 925691 for δ = 0.05 and job 925791 for δ = 0.1, 7,000 units each, 100 / 100 tasks completed, 0 arm failures, 53.0 + 45.8 recorded core-hours (29 and 25 s per unit, two analyses at 999 permutations). The code is local main at 0a15d44 plus this change's working tree, rsynced over the cluster checkout and verified by tree hash; file hashes are in each PROVENANCE.txt.

    Decision: adopt_floor at δ = 0.05 and adopt_floor at δ = 0.1, hence adopt_floor under the predeclared conjunction. The floor-2 arm holds the bound 0.0695 for every statistic at both controls in both runs.

    In one paragraph. At the Phase 6 SEA-AD design with the stage signal weakened until the unfloored rank rule selects rank 1 in 58 % (δ = 0.05) and 42 % (δ = 0.1) of the null replicates (held-out CV AUROC 0.51 and 0.57), each of 7,000 units per strength was analysed twice on the same data and permutation draws, with the 0.9.0 rule and with the rank floor of 2. In the floored arm the zero-effect anchor rejects delta 0.042 / 0.044, angle 0.054 / 0.060 and shape 0.036 / 0.038 (δ = 0.05 / 0.1), and the translation control 0.036 / 0.036, 0.046 / 0.042 and 0.026 / 0.030, all at or below 0.0695 at R = 500. The floor lifted 4,257 and 3,228 rank-1 selections, mostly to rank 2; in the unfloored arm every rank-1 unit measured angle as exactly 0° or 180° with a two-point null that never rejected. Power is at the null in both arms at these strengths, so the check establishes size, not a gain: the floor defines angle and shape where the 0.9.0 rule left them degenerate, and the record's spectrum and null summary say how resolvable they are.

    1. Construction and calibration

    • Why a new construction. The 0.9.0 latent-adjustment power comparison recorded the CV-selected rank per unit at the Phase 6 construction: no unit in any arm selected rank 1 (roadmap, Next changes item 4), while both SEA-AD cohorts did (held-out AUROC 0.63 / 0.66). The floor changes the measurement only where the unfloored rule selects rank 1, so it is validated where that happens.
    • Data. generate_semisynthetic_trajectory at the Phase 6 small-n design: three stages, group_stage_sizes = [[11, 10, 28], [9, 10, 12]] (n = 80), methylation (M-values) and expression measured, 498 features, joint magnitude, p_dmp = 0.1, baseline_continuity = 0, with the stage signal weakened through the per-omic effect sizes delta_methyl = delta_expr = delta_protein = δ, at the Phase 6 value 2.0 for every committed study. Two strengths were run, δ = 0.05 and δ = 0.1 (below). p_dmp stays at 0.1 so every pool-limited surgery keeps its Phase 6 headroom (translation 98 sites available, orientation and shape 242; every cell fits).
    • Cells. The zero-effect anchor; the translation negative control at e = 1.0; magnitude at e = 0.02, 0.05, 0.1, 0.25 (the Phase 6 magnitude profile below its saturation); orientation and shape at e = 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0 (the Phase 6 study profile). 14 cells × 500 replicates = 7,000 units per strength. The generator and RRPP seeds depend on the replicate index only (base seed 2026), so every cell and both arms of replicate r share the baseline draw and the permutation draws.
    • Arms. Each unit is analysed with analyze_trajectories twice, with the harness CV defaults (cv2_splits = 4, cv1_splits = 3, n_repeats = 5, max_components = 20, random_state = 1203), 999 permutations, the same RRPP seed, no covariates and the net-displacement estimator:
    • floor_1: min_components = 1, the 0.9.0 rule (measured, not judged);
    • floor_2: min_components = 2, the candidate default.
    • Rule (predeclared in design D7, pure function rank_floor_decision). For the anchor and the translation control, the floor_2 arm's rate over its R available replicates must be at most α + k·√(α(1 − α)/R) with α = 0.05 and k = 2 (0.0695 at R = 500) for delta, angle and shape. Every statistic holding at both controls gives adopt_floor, otherwise diagnose_floor_excess. With two strengths, adopt_floor requires both runs to hold (settled with the user before submission). The floor_1 arm's rates are reported and never enter the decision; power rows are descriptive.

    Calibration

    The calibration criterion is that the unfloored rule selects rank 1 in at least half of the null (anchor) replicates on a recorded smoke run before any paper-grade run (scripts/rank_floor_check.py calibrate; every iteration is in results/rank-floor-2026-10-23/calibration/calibration.json). Weakening δ alone first raises the selected rank: at δ = 1.0 and 0.5 the held-out AUROC stays at 1.0 and the CV picks 3–6 components, at δ = 0.25 it is 0.92 with 4–11 components, because the stage signal thins across more directions before it fades. It reaches rank 1 only once the signal is near chance.

    Smoke profiles

    R = 20 at 99 permutations, 14 cells × 2 arms = 560 analyses per strength (smoke-delta0.05/, smoke-delta0.1/). No arm failed; the floor_2 arm selected rank ≥ 2 in every unit while floor_1 measured angle in one dimension in 183 / 280 (δ = 0.05) and 147 / 280 (δ = 0.1) units. Each smoke decision reads diagnose_floor_excess on one statistic at 3 of 20 rejections (0.150 against the R = 20 bound 0.147): anchor angle at δ = 0.05, translation delta at δ = 0.1. At R = 20 one rejection moves a rate by 0.05, so the smoke decisions are calibration and cost evidence, not verdicts. Cost: 26–53 s per unit at 99 permutations locally, 58 s at 999 permutations.

    3. What the floor changes

    • Calibration realized. The unfloored anchor arm selected rank 1 in 289 / 500 replicates at δ = 0.05 (0.578; criterion met) and 210 / 500 at δ = 0.1 (0.420; the smoke replicates' 0.55 was the high side of the R = 50 estimate 0.44, so the paper-grade fraction sits just under one half, as reported before submission). The anchor rank distributions are 1: 289, 2: 120, 3: 59, 4: 18, 5: 10, ≥6: 4 (floor 1) against 2: 346, 3: 91, 4: 27, 5: 23, 6: 7, ≥7: 6 (floor 2) at δ = 0.05, and 1: 210, 2: 131, 3: 66, 4: 27, 5: 25, ≥6: 41 against 2: 275, 3: 104, 4: 37, 5: 39, 6: 22, ≥7: 23 at δ = 0.1.
    • The lift. Over all 7,000 units the unfloored rule selected rank 1 in 4,257 (δ = 0.05) and 3,228 (δ = 0.1); the floor moved those to rank 2 in 3,289 / 2,187, rank 3 in 558 / 499, rank 4 in 184 / 180 and higher in the rest. The two arms select the same rank in 2,635 / 3,633 units; in 108 / 139 units the unfloored modal rank was already ≥ 2 but moved, because outer folds that had chosen rank 1 chose another candidate under the floor. The floor-2 arm selected rank 2 in 4,795 / 3,909 units and rank ≥ 3 in the remainder; it never fell back to a lower rank.
    • angle at rank 1. In every unfloored anchor unit that selected rank 1 the observed angle is exactly 0° (246 and 182 units) or 180° (43 and 28), its null's 95 % quantile is 180° (a two-point distribution) and its smallest p-value is 0.068 / 0.054: the rank-1 angle test never rejects. The unfloored arm's anchor angle rates (0.030 / 0.038) therefore come entirely from its rank ≥ 2 units, where they are 15 / 211 = 0.071 and 19 / 290 = 0.066. Under the 0.9.0 rule a real-data angle p-value of 1.0 at rank 1 says nothing about orientation; this is the degeneracy the floor removes.
    • Null width. The median over anchor replicates of the angle null's 95 % quantile is 84.0° / 61.2° in the unfloored arm (180° in its rank-1 units, 55.9° / 52.4° in its others) against 61.2° / 57.0° with the floor; the null's median SD is 34.0° / 21.0° against 17.8° / 16.3°. In a two-dimensional space with no stage signal the angle null is wide (two near-random directions), which is what a resolvable-orientation reader should see in null_summary.angle.
    • The signal. Mean held-out CV AUROC at the anchor is 0.513 / 0.571 (floor 1) and 0.516 / 0.580 (floor 2): the floor does not change what the CV can find, only the smallest rank it may report.

    At these strengths the surgeries scale a stage effect that is itself near the noise (magnitude multiplies δ by 1 + e; orientation, shape and translation relocate differential sites whose shift is δ), so every rate in both arms sits at the null, 0.010–0.062. The construction therefore measures size, not power: nothing here says the floor makes angle or shape powerful. The angle differences of +0.016 to +0.036, several above two pooled SEs, are the unfloored arm's under-rejection at rank 1 (its angle rates of 0.010–0.042 are below α, as at the anchor), not detection; delta and shape differ by at most ±0.014 between the arms.

    5. Decision, limitation and scope

    Decision, read verbatim from results/rank-floor-2026-10-23/delta0.05/rank_floor_decision.json and delta0.1/rank_floor_decision.json: "decision": "adopt_floor" in both, with "exceeding": [] and every floor_2 verdict "holds" at the anchor and the translation control. Under the predeclared conjunction the check adopts the floor: MOTCO 0.10.0 keeps min_components=2 as the default of fit_pls_latent_space, SimulationEvaluationParams and TrajectoryAnalysisParams / motco analyze.

    Limitation: the floor guarantees two dimensions, not a second axis with signal. When the stage signal supports one component, or none, the second component is the best of the remaining candidates and carries whatever the inner CV preferred among them; angle and shape are then defined but their nulls are wide, and at these strengths their power is at α. A rank-2 selection under the floor is a statement about the measurement space, not evidence of a second biological axis. Read the record's config_spectrum relative eigengap and null_summary.angle together: they say how resolvable orientation is in each analysis, and the selected rank stays the covariate reported beside any angle result. The 0.9.0 rule remains reachable as min_components=1 for comparison.

    Scope.

    • The construction weakens the per-omic effect sizes uniformly; p_dmp, the cell sizes, the block set and the joint magnitude are the Phase 6 study's. It reproduces the SEA-AD cohorts' held-out AUROC regime, not their mechanism (a dominant technical axis aligned with stage), which the covariate-aware latent construction addresses.
    • Both arms used the harness CV defaults and no covariates; the floor applies inside the same cross-validation with covariates and latent adjustment, but their interaction was not measured here.
    • Every committed study config, fixture and dated report pins min_components: 1 and is unchanged by the default (tests/test_rank_floor_default_pins.py); the sead_sexdiff cohorts are to be rerun at the floor in their own proposal, pinning MOTCO 0.10.0.
    • Replicates are matched across cells and arms by seed; the two strengths are independent runs with their own decision files, and the report's conjunction rule was fixed with the user before submission.
