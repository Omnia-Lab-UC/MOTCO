    Covariate-adjustment Type I check — 2026-10-16

    Paper-grade size check of covariate-adjusted motco analyze at the Phase 6 SEA-AD design (openspec/changes/archive/2026-10-16-covariate-adjusted-trajectories/, spec covariate-adjustment-type-i). It is the gate the case study must pass before it reports covariate-adjusted trajectory results. Every number below is read from results/covariate-type-i-2026-10-16/ (covariate_type_i_decision.json, covariate_type_i_rates.csv, covariate_type_i_pvalues.csv, merged.jsonl). Rates are rejection rates at p ≤ 0.05 over 500 replicates × 999 permutations (Monte Carlo SE about 0.0097 at 0.05).

    Run: 180 replicates on the workstation, then the remaining 320 on ing as SLURM array 921167 (60 shards, all COMPLETED). The code is c3ee13a plus this change's working tree, and the script and analysis.py hashes in PROVENANCE.txt were verified identical on both machines. Decision: covariates_validated.

    In one paragraph. On null datasets at the SEA-AD MTG design, a numeric and a categorical covariate were made to depend on group, stage and their interaction, and their effect was injected into the features. Without adjustment the analysis rejects too often: shape at 0.364, delta at 0.098 and angle at 0.096. With both covariates declared, all three statistics hold the predeclared bound 0.05 + 2·√(0.05·0.95/500) = 0.0695: delta 0.040, angle 0.062 and shape 0.034. The two arms share every latent space (asserted per replicate), so the difference comes entirely from the trajectory model. The case study may report covariate-adjusted results under the conditions in §5.

    1. Construction

    • Data. generate_semisynthetic_trajectory at the phase6_small_n settings: three stages, group_stage_sizes = [[11, 10, 28], [9, 10, 12]], p_dmp = 0.1, ρ = 0, joint magnitude, group_effect_size = 0 (null). Only the methylation (367 CpGs, converted to M-values) and expression (131 genes) blocks are measured; both are analysed with the identity transform. Base seed 2026. Generator, covariate and RRPP seeds are derived per replicate.
    • Covariates. With g the group indicator and s the stage index (0–2):
    • numeric z = a_g·g + a_s·s + a_gs·g·s + ε, with ε ~ N(0, 1);
    • categorical: three levels from a multinomial logit with level logits k·c·η (k = 0, 1, 2) on the same linear predictor η.
    • Values: a_g = 0.5, a_s = 1, a_gs = 2 and c = 0.5.
    • Across replicates, the numeric covariate's group × stage interaction contrast (z̄_B,2 − z̄_B,0) − (z̄_A,2 − z̄_A,0) has mean 3.98 (SD 0.58). The covariates therefore bend the groups' trajectories differently, not only shift them.
    • Injection. X_f += s_z·sd_f·w_f·z + s_c·sd_f·Σ_k u_{k,f}·1[cat = k].
    • sd_f is the feature's pooled SD on the analysed scale.
    • w and the u_k are unit directions, each on a random 20 % of the 498 joint features, drawn once from the base seed.
    • Strengths: s_z = 3 and s_c = 2.
    • Arms. Each replicate is analysed twice with analyze_trajectories, using its defaults: net displacement, CV rank with the harness knobs, and the same RRPP seed.
    • adjusted: covariate_cols = ["z", "category"], category categorical. The design columns are z, category[c1] and category[c2].
    • unadjusted: no covariates.
    • The latent scores and selected rank were identical in both arms for all 500 replicates. CV selected rank 2 in 264 replicates and rank 3 in 236.
    • Rule. Per statistic, the adjusted rate over the R available replicates must be ≤ α + k·√(α(1 − α)/R), with α = 0.05 and k = 2. covariates_validated requires all three statistics to hold. The unadjusted arm is reported only and does not enter the decision.

    Calibration

    The design's first strengths (a_s = 0.5, a_gs = 0.75, s_z = 0.5, s_c = 0.3) did not confound: in the R = 20 smoke run, the unadjusted arm rejected at about α, which would have made the gate vacuous.

    Raising s_z alone, even to 30, did not help. The injection carries z's within-cell noise along w in the same proportion as its cell-mean pattern, so the RRPP null widens together with the signal. The confounding signal-to-noise is a_gs relative to the covariate's noise SD.

    The calibrated values above were chosen on probe replicates and confirmed by the smoke profile (R = 20, 99 permutations): unadjusted shape 0.45. The categorical logit scale keeps the factor mixed within cells, so complete-cell alignment cannot trip the full-rank guard. All values are recorded in the decision file.

    2. Rates against the bound

    From covariate_type_i_rates.csv. Rate (MC SE); R = 500 for every statistic, no failed replicate; bound 0.0695.

    statistic adjusted verdict unadjusted (descriptive)
    delta 0.040 (0.0088) holds 0.098 (0.0133)
    angle 0.062 (0.0108) holds 0.096 (0.0132)
    shape 0.034 (0.0081) holds 0.364 (0.0215)

    Stability. Split into five consecutive blocks of 100 replicates, the adjusted rates are:

    • delta: 0.07, 0.04, 0.05, 0.00, 0.04;
    • angle: 0.05, 0.05, 0.05, 0.11, 0.05;
    • shape: 0.06, 0.02, 0.03, 0.02, 0.04.

    The angle rate of 0.062 comes from one block at 0.11; the other four blocks sit at 0.05. At p < 0.05 instead of p ≤ 0.05, the convention of the earlier Type I reports, the rates are 0.040, 0.060 and 0.034. The decision is the same.

    Against the no-covariate anchor. The parent Phase 6 study's pls_cv × net_displacement anchor (no covariates, independent seeds; net-displacement Type I report) had delta 0.026, angle 0.050 and shape 0.020. The adjusted arm here is 0.01–0.015 higher on each statistic but within the bound. That is plausible for a model with three more columns at n = 80, where RRPP is exact only asymptotically. It is recorded here and not judged by the rule.

    3. What the unadjusted arm shows

    The construction confounds, mostly through shape: an interaction between the covariate and stage bends one group's path relative to the other's. With 36 % false shape rejections, an unadjusted SEA-AD shape result under comparable confounding could not be told apart from a donor-composition difference. delta and angle inflate about two-fold. The adjustment removes all three inflations, which is the property the gate tests.

    4. Scope and limits

    • The check covers additive, linear covariate effects that are correctly specified in the model. A covariate whose true effect is non-linear, or interacts with group or stage, is outside it.
    • The latent space is covariate-blind. In this construction the covariate effect is correlated with stage, so PLS may spend capacity on it; that case is measured here rather than assumed away. A covariate effect much larger than the stage signal is not.
    • This is one design: n = 80, two blocks, 498 features, three covariate design columns. More covariate columns at the same n use more residual degrees of freedom; this check does not bound that cost.
    • There is no power claim. Adjustment costs degrees of freedom and, in the presence of confounding, also removes confounded signal; the operating studies' power numbers were measured without covariates.

    5. Consequence for the case study

    The decision is covariates_validated. The SEA-AD case study may report covariate-adjusted motco analyze results (MOTCO ≥ 0.8.0) under these conditions:

    • keep the covariate set parsimonious (each factor level is a column);
    • report the record's covariates block (design columns, missing policy and dropped donors) beside the results;
    • carry the additive-linear and covariate-blind-basis limitations above;
    • keep the earlier binding statements unchanged: the CV-selected rank is reported beside angle, and non-rejections are uninformative.
