    Predictive-subspace size excess — diagnosis report (2026-11-13)

    Change: predictive-size-excess-diagnosis (no package change; MOTCO 0.24.0). Reads: results/predictive-size-excess-2026-11-13/ (predictive_size_excess_decision.json; the rate, paired and cell tables; accounting.json, reproduction.json, preflight.json, settings.json; PROVENANCE.txt, sacct.txt). Parent: the predictive-subspace pilot. Concept page: measurement visibility.

    The predictive-subspace pilot read keep_production on one finding. On the SEA-AD plasmode anchor (500 permuted-label replicates of the astrocyte export) all three predictive coordinate systems rejected delta in 65–68 units and shape in 38–39, where production rejected 21 and 13. No simulated design reproduced the excess.

    Roadmap open item 2 named a guess. The two predictive directions of the rank-5 fit hold its in-sample stage fit (stage R² 0.84 against a held-out AUROC of 0.636), and the reduced model's residuals in them are not exchangeable across stages, which RRPP's draws assume. Two checks test it on the pilot's own units, labels and RRPP seeds, under a decision fixed before any unit ran (design.md D8):

    • Check W keeps the coordinates and changes the null. RRPP's draws are restricted within stage: the reduced model's residuals are permuted among the donors of each stage, never across stages.
    • Check O keeps the null and changes the coordinates. The fitted stage indicators are computed out of fold: each donor's row comes from a stage-PLS model fitted without it.

    In one paragraph. The verdict is both_restore. Each repair alone brings the predictive coordinates' anchor rates within the size rule the pilot applied, so the excess needs both the unrestricted draws and coordinates fitted on the donors they measure.

    • Within-stage draws remove the delta excess entirely. 37–38 units are rejected only under the unrestricted draws, and none only under the within-stage ones; delta falls from 0.130–0.136 to 0.056–0.062. They remove little of the shape excess. shape falls from 0.076–0.078 to 0.064–0.066 (5–6 discordant units, all one way), so it holds the rule by one or two counts and stays at about 2.5 times production's 0.026.
    • The out-of-fold coordinates read 0.016 / 0.014 / 0.034, but at the anchor they carry almost no stage signal: the out-of-fold fit explains a median 0.022 of the stage indicators' variance, against 0.84 in sample.
    • On the real-noise plant the within-stage arms keep delta above production's at e = 0.5 (0.64–0.66 against 0.51), but production gains as much under the same draws (0.63).
    • Without covariates the predictive excess stays, so the six covariates and their df correction do not cause it.
    • The rule's consequence. A follow-up change may propose within-stage draws as an opt-in option and read their size and power before predictive_orthonormal is re-read. The pilot's keep_production stands.

    1. Configuration and provenance

    • The units. The pilot's 800 plasmode units (predictive_subspace_check.plasmode_settings / plasmode_units, imported unchanged): the anchor at the paper stream's replicates 0–499, and the production selection's real-noise span plant at e ∈ {0.25, 0.5, 1.0} at replicates 0–99. All run at 999 permutations with rejection at p ≤ 0.05. Each unit carries the pilot's label draw (within-stage, base seed 2034), plant and RRPP seed. The configuration is the case study's: six covariates, the df residual correction, net displacement and rank floor 2. The export is the SEA-AD MTG astrocyte cohort_30 (RNA and ATAC, 5,000 features each, 80 donors, cells F 11 / 10 / 28 and M 9 / 10 / 12), read in place through the plasmode's contract.
    • One fit per unit.
    • An anchor unit fits at the plasmode's committed fidelity rank of 5 without cross-validation. The anchor does not move the label-free features, so every anchor unit's CV selects that rank.
    • A plant unit runs the production CV once, and every other arm is forced at the rank it selects (4–14; medians 7, 7 and 5 at e = 0.25, 0.5 and 1.0).
    • The pre-flight required the cross-validated fit on anchor replicates 0 and 1 to select rank 5 at AUROC 0.635556, and to give the forced call's statistics and p-values exactly. It passed on the workstation and on ing.
    • The fourteen arms (design.md D7), each at the unit's RRPP seed with serial draws:
    arm coordinates draws covariates role
    pls_cv scores unrestricted six reference
    predictive, predictive_orthonormal, predictive_fitted in-sample predictive unrestricted six reference
    predictive__within_stage, predictive_orthonormal__within_stage, predictive_fitted__within_stage in-sample predictive within stage six check W, decisive
    fitted_out_of_fold out-of-fold fitted indicators unrestricted six check O, decisive
    pls_cv__within_stage scores within stage six descriptive
    fitted_out_of_fold__within_stage out-of-fold fitted indicators within stage six descriptive
    <reference arm>__no_covariates (four) as named unrestricted none descriptive
    • Within-stage draws. Per draw, each stage's residual rows are permuted among themselves, in declared stage order, from default_rng(the unit's RRPP seed). Everything else is RRPP's: the reduced fit, the df scale and estimate_difference per draw. The driver's loop equals RRPP exactly when every row is in one stratum (a test). One restricted sequence per unit is shared by every within-stage arm.
    • Out-of-fold coordinates. Four stage-stratified, shuffled folds per replicate, seeded from the driver's name, base seed 2037, the replicate and folds. For each fold, fit_plsda_model is fitted on the other three folds' rows of the analysis's PLS input features, at the unit's rank and scaling. The held-out rows then get transform(X_held) · y_loadingsᵀ, the fitted stage indicators in that model's response units. With every donor in training, the same computation returns predictive_fitted to 1e-8 (a test). Each training set holds 15 / 15 / 30 donors by stage.
    • Checks on every unit.
    • The pilot's five consistency checks.
    • same_fit_covariate_free: each covariate-free arm fitted the production model.
    • path_identity: the driver's inference path, under unrestricted draws, returns each reference arm's statistics to 1e-10 and its p-values exactly.
    • Code and host. Revision 7046672 on proposal/predictive-size-excess-diagnosis, run from a clean detached worktree on ing (partition 512x1024, nodes n1 and n3). Python 3.11.16, numpy 2.3.5, scipy 1.16.3, scikit-learn 1.8.0 and pandas 2.3.3, with BLAS pinned to one thread. Preparation job 938594, array 938598 (100 shards) and finishing job 938732; every allocation completed with exit 0:0. 10.92 recorded core-hours, against a projection of about 11.5 and a cap of 20 (PROVENANCE.txt).

    2. Accounting and reproduction

    • Units. 800 of 800 complete: no failed unit, no failed arm, no inconsistent unit and no duplicate record.
    • Checks. Every applicable check held on every unit. The largest deviations were path_identity 0, same_fit 0, same_fit_covariate_free 0, rank 0 and fitted_labels 5.3e-14. Every covariate-free arm's latent matrix equals its reference arm's exactly.
    • Reproduction: passed. Each of the four reference arms' available and rejected counts, in each of the four cells, for delta, angle and shape, equals the pilot's committed rate_table.csv: 48 of 48 rows. The forced anchor fit therefore reproduces the pilot's cross-validated anchor counts exactly (21 / 24 / 13 for pls_cv, 68 / 11 / 38 for predictive, 67 / 9 / 38 for predictive_orthonormal, 65 / 10 / 39 for predictive_fitted).

    3. The decision

    Rule version 1 grades each decisive arm on its three anchor readings:

    • restored: the arm holds the stage-span paper grade's count-plus-ceiling rule (judge_rates, m = 3). This is the rule and the family the pilot's S1 failed. At R = 500 a reading exceeds the bound α + 2·SE = 0.0695 above 34 rejections, the ceiling is 36, and one exceedance is allowed.
    • reduced: not restored, and the one-sided exact paired p-value for fewer rejections than the arm's counterpart is at most 0.025 on delta or shape.
    • unchanged: otherwise.

    Check W takes the weakest grade of its three arms, and check O the grade of its one arm.

    arm delta angle shape exceedances grade pilot's counterpart
    predictive__within_stage 31 (0.062) 17 (0.034) 33 (0.066) 0 restored 68 / 11 / 38
    predictive_orthonormal__within_stage 29 (0.058) 17 (0.034) 32 (0.064) 0 restored 67 / 9 / 38
    predictive_fitted__within_stage 28 (0.056) 17 (0.034) 33 (0.066) 0 restored 65 / 10 / 39
    fitted_out_of_fold 8 (0.016) 7 (0.014) 17 (0.034) 0 restored 65 / 10 / 39 (predictive_fitted)

    Check W is restored, and so is check O. The verdict is both_restore. The gate passed: the reproduction passed, no unit was inconsistent and the pre-flight passed.

    The consequence fixed with the rule: "The unrestricted draws are what fails in the predictive subspace on this export, and the out-of-fold reading confirms that the in-sample fit is where the excess lives. A follow-up change may propose within-stage draws as an opt-in option and must read their size and power on the pilot's simulated designs, the weak design and this anchor before predictive_orthonormal is re-read."

    4. Check W: what the within-stage draws repair

    Paired against each arm's counterpart (the same coordinates under unrestricted draws) on the 500 anchor units. b counts the units only the counterpart rejects, and c the units only the within-stage arm rejects:

    arm delta b / c delta p (fewer) shape b / c shape p (fewer)
    predictive__within_stage 37 / 0 7.3e-12 5 / 0 0.031
    predictive_orthonormal__within_stage 38 / 0 3.6e-12 6 / 0 0.016
    predictive_fitted__within_stage 37 / 0 7.3e-12 6 / 0 0.016
    • delta. The within-stage null removes the whole excess, and only that. Every unit the restricted draws stop rejecting was one the unrestricted draws rejected, and none goes the other way. Against production's unrestricted rates the within-stage arms still read slightly more (17–19 units against 9–10; two-sided p 0.09–0.25).
    • shape. It barely moves: 5–6 units, all one way. The arms hold the rule at 32–33 rejections against the exceedance threshold of 34. Against production shape stays higher: 26 units against 6–7, two-sided p 0.0005–0.0013. Production itself reads 19 under the same within-stage draws (§7).
    • angle rises from 9–11 to 17 and stays well inside the bound.

    So restored is firm for delta and marginal for shape. The rule grades both alike; the follow-up has to read shape again.

    5. Check O: what the out-of-fold coordinates show

    fitted_out_of_fold reads 8 / 7 / 17 on the anchor. Against predictive_fitted that is 64 / 7 discordant units on delta and 38 / 16 on shape. Against production's unrestricted rates it rejects less delta (20 / 7) and less angle (21 / 4), with shape close (13 / 17).

    The reading is weaker evidence than its grade suggests. At the anchor the out-of-fold coordinates carry almost no stage signal. The out-of-fold fit explains a median 0.022 of the stage indicators' variance, against 0.84 in sample on every anchor unit. Its angle null has a median 95th percentile of 94.9°, so the net displacement it measures is close to noise.

    What the arm shows is that the excess needs coordinates fitted on the donors they measure. It does not show that a cross-fitted measurement is usable at this design. Under within-stage draws the same coordinates read 23 / 18 / 23 (descriptive), also inside the rule.

    • The within-stage arms keep part of the real-noise gain. At e = 0.5 they read 0.64–0.66, above production's 0.51 (the decision's statement for each restored arm), and below their unrestricted counterparts' 0.79–0.82 (14–16 units, all one way).
    • Production gains under the same draws. It reads 0.63 at e = 0.5 (12 units, all one way). The within-stage draws keep the pooled stage means at their fitted values, which narrows the delta null against a magnitude edit of one group's configuration. Within-stage draws against within-stage draws, the predictive coordinates' advantage is therefore small: 0.64–0.66 against 0.63 at e = 0.5, and 0.28–0.29 against 0.24 at e = 0.25. No paired test of that pair was predeclared, so these are rates only.
    • The out-of-fold arm reads 0.81 and 0.99, above every in-sample arm. The design expected the opposite, a lower bound. The plant is defined by the full sample's stage means. A plausible reading, not tested here: each fold model is fitted on 60 of the same 80 donors, so it recovers the planted direction, while a held-out donor's own noise is not fitted into its coordinates. On that reading the out-of-fold plant rate reflects how the plant is built, not real-noise power. The arm also leaks shape there (0.16 at e = 0.5).

    7. Production under within-stage draws (descriptive)

    pls_cv__within_stage reads 19 / 27 / 19 on the anchor, inside the rule. Against pls_cv the paired counts are 4 / 2 on delta, 0 / 3 on angle and 0 / 6 on shape (two-sided p 0.69, 0.25 and 0.031). Production holds size under either null on this anchor. On the plant it gains delta power under the within-stage draws (§6) and reads more off-target shape (0.11 at e = 0.25 and e = 0.5, against 0.09 and 0.04).

    • The size of the inequality does not separate the predictive coordinates from the scores (1.39–1.59 against 1.41).

    • What the unrestricted draws do with it. They move residuals between stages of unequal dispersion, and the within-stage draws do not. The measured delta excess is consistent with that. The table describes the pattern; it does not test the mechanism.
    • The stage fit. In-sample stage R² is 0.840 on every anchor unit, against a median out-of-fold share of 0.022. On the plant cells the two readings are 0.95 / 0.27, 0.95 / 0.40 and 0.87 / 0.49 at e = 0.25, 0.5 and 1.0. The plant adds stage signal that survives out of fold.

    11. What follows

    • The verdict's consequence. A separate change may propose within-stage draws as an opt-in option of RRPP, the harness and motco analyze, byte-identical when unset, and must read their size and power on the pilot's simulated designs, the weak design and this anchor. Only its verdict could reopen predictive_orthonormal, under the admissibility rule the roadmap requires.
    • What that change should carry from this reading.
    • shape stays near the bound under within-stage draws on this anchor (0.064–0.066), so its size reading cannot be assumed.
    • Production gains as well. Production gains plant power under within-stage draws (0.51 → 0.63 at e = 0.5), so any comparison of measurement spaces must pair them under the same draws. The predictive coordinates' advantage over production then shrinks to a few points on the plant.
    • The null is a design question, not only a predictive-subspace one. Within-stage draws assume exchangeability within stage only, which is weaker than RRPP's assumption, and they keep the pooled stage means at their fitted values.
    • The pilot's keep_production stands. The predictive values stay opt-in diagnostics. No verdict here validates a test or changes a default.
    • Open item 2 keeps its prediction for ADNI. The guess was confirmed on this export, so the predicted excess should shrink as donors per feature grow. Reading it needs ADNI's export contract.
    • A covariate-free lead. Production without covariates exceeds on delta at this anchor (§8). Whether that holds beyond one fit is unread.

    12. Limitations

    • One export and one fit. The 500 anchor units share one latent matrix and differ only in the group labels, so every anchor rate is conditional on that fit (as the pilot's were). The out-of-fold arm averages over fold splits, not over donors.
    • The within-stage null mirrors how the plasmode draws its labels. The plasmode permutes the labels within stage, so the restricted draws match the anchor's null by construction. Whether they hold size where residuals are exchangeable across stages, and what they cost in power, is the follow-up's simulation reading.
    • shape holds by one or two counts. At R = 500 the within-stage arms sit at 32–33 against a threshold of 34. A rerun on new label draws could put one of them over it.
    • The out-of-fold arm's restored comes with almost no stage signal (§5), and its plant reading reflects the plant's construction (§6).
    • The plant cells are R = 100 and descriptive. No paired test between the within-stage predictive arms and production under the same draws was predeclared.
    • The rank is chosen on all donors. It is one integer, selected by held-out AUROC and pinned at 5 on the anchor.
