    The ADNI latent plasmode, diagnosed — report (2026-11-18)

    Change: adni-latent-plasmode-diagnosis (MOTCO 0.25.0, no package change). Reads: results/adni-latent-plasmode-diagnosis-2026-11-18/ (adni_latent_plasmode_diagnosis.json, component_table.csv, spread_table.csv, adni_table.csv, reproduction.json, settings.json, PROVENANCE.txt). Parent: the ADNI latent plasmode (its committed settings, tables and decision, bound by sha256). Concept page: measurement visibility.

    The latent plasmode estimated the share of ADNI's covariate-adjusted stage program inside production's fitted span from two independent held-out halves. It read gate_failed: in its calibration on synthetic cohorts, the bias of pls_cv's total capture exceeded ±0.05 at five of eight strength × point cells. Its rule names the consequence: a diagnosis of the estimator comes before any reading. This diagnosis re-reads the run's 9,200 records (no new unit) and asks where the bias comes from, where the spread lives, and whether any interval for ADNI's capture is bounded.

    In one paragraph. The reading is information_limited.

    • The estimator is sound. A unit's numerator and denominator are unbiased for their truths, and pooled over a cell's 24 cohorts the ratio sits on its truth. The gate's bias is the nonlinearity of a ratio with a noisy denominator, within one cohort.
    • The spread belongs to the cohort. 89 to 99 % of the variance of a cohort's mean denominator is between cohorts, so more units or splits of one cohort do not narrow it.
    • ADNI's program does not clear that spread. Its mean denominator is 1.22 to 1.90 of its standard deviations from zero, under every stand-in for the cohort-level variance. So no two-sided 95 % interval for the capture is bounded at any point.
    • What is left: one-sided bounds (recorded, not judged). A capture lies in [0, 1], and within that range ADNI's sets give upper bounds: production's span holds at most 0.46 to 0.57 of the program at 80 participants and 0.52 to 0.65 at 200, depending on the stand-in.
    • The consequence. No capture is stated on ADNI, and the parent's gate_failed stands. A resolved capture waits for a larger cohort or several cohorts.

    How to read the verdict. The rule (version 1) was written after an exploratory pass on the same records (the change's openspec/changes/archive/2026-11-18-adni-latent-plasmode-diagnosis/notes.md). It is a reading with its thresholds stated, not a predeclared test. The range restriction was added after the first recorded run, and it is not judged.

    1. Configuration and provenance

    • Inputs. The run's merged records on ing, read in place: 7,200 calibration and 2,000 cohort records (sha256 in reproduction.json). Every record carries its profile's committed settings hash, and each expected unit is present once. No export is read.
    • Reproduction. The parent driver's own table builders, run on the records, write the committed calibration_table.csv and cohort/capture_table.csv byte for byte.
    • Code. Revision e9ec526 from a clean worktree on ing, srun job 945803, about four minutes on two cores. Two earlier recorded runs are in PROVENANCE.txt and the change's notes. They had the same tables; one dropped the strength-0 stand-ins (a bug, fixed with a test), and neither carried the ranges.
    • The designated reading is pls_cv's total capture. The tables carry every space (the predictive subspace, a PCA at the CV rank, forced ranks 2, 8 and 19) and both stage contrasts.

    finding holds when (pls_cv, total) result

    The verdict order is reproduction_failed, estimator_biased (F1 or F2), resolvable (F4), units_limited (F3), then information_limited. Across every space and reading at the gate strengths, the largest |z| of either part or of the aggregate is 1.75.

    • Pooled over cohorts, the ratio is on its truth. The gate judged the mean over cohorts of each cohort's own ratio. With 25 units of one cohort that ratio's denominator stays noisy, and a ratio's error does not average out when its denominator is noisy.
    • At strength 1 the delta method accounts for the size. Its first-order bias at 25 units per cohort is −0.026 to −0.050, against the observed −0.035 to −0.077. At strength 0.67 the delta method does not apply: the cohort mean denominator's coefficient of variation is 0.59 to 0.66.
    • At strength 0.67 one or two cohorts dominate the gate's numbers. One or two of the 24 cohorts have a mean denominator below a quarter of the cell's. They inflate the per-cohort SD to 0.57 to 1.79, while the robust SD (IQR / 1.349) is 0.20 to 0.31 and the median error −0.026 to −0.053.

    5. ADNI's own interval (F4)

    • ADNI's inputs. For each point, ADNI's mean numerator and denominator over its 500 units.
    • Their covariance. V = f·B + W_ADNI / 500. ADNI's between-cohort covariance cannot be observed from one cohort, so B comes from the calibration at strength 0 or 0.67. It is used raw (f = 1) or scaled by ADNI's within-cohort variance of the denominator over the calibration's (f 0.52 to 0.82).
    • When the interval is bounded. Fieller's 95 % interval for the ratio is bounded only when t = D̄ / √V_DD exceeds 1.96.

    • What the sets do say. Every set is the line outside two roots, or the whole line. Within the capture's range [0, 1], all but two are an interval from 0 to the bound in the last column. At 750 participants under the strength-0 stand-ins the set covers the whole range.

For comparison at 80 participants, the label-free PCA is bounded at 0.59 to 0.77 and the predictive subspace at 0.30 to 0.37 (adni_table.csv).

    6. What follows

    • No capture is stated on ADNI. gate_failed stands, and so does the simulated 0.10 as the only capture of a weak stage program. A rule version 2 on ADNI that resolves the capture at ±0.15 would fail for the same reason: the cohort's own spread, not the units or the gate.
    • The estimator stands. Its parts and aggregate are unbiased, so it is fit for a cohort whose program energy clears its cohort-level spread. ROSMAP is the next candidate once an export exists; several cohorts pooled would also shrink the spread.
    • One-sided bounds are the most ADNI can give. A rule that states them (for example, "production's span holds at most about half of the program at 80 participants") would be its own change, written before it reads, with the stand-in fixed in advance.
    • Item 22's held-out readings stay mixtures, and open item 4's candidates have no real-noise capture pre-check on ADNI.

    7. Limitations

    • The reading was written after the numbers were seen, and the range restriction after the first recorded run. Both are recorded as such. The parent's verdict is the only predeclared one, and it is unchanged.
    • The cohort-level covariance is a stand-in from Gaussian synthetic cohorts. Its 24 cohorts estimate a variance with about 30 % relative error, and ADNI's within-cohort variance is 31 to 48 % below the calibration's at strength 0.67 (within_ratio_D). The four stand-ins bracket that range and agree on F4. The bounds in §5 move with them.
    • Fieller's interval assumes the cohort means are close to normal. The strength-0.67 calibration shows heavy tails near a zero denominator.
    • One cohort. ADNI's between-cohort spread cannot be observed, only modelled.
