    Plasmode attribution demonstration on SEA-AD astrocytes — 2026-11-02

    This report runs the plasmode demonstration (openspec change plasmode-attribution-demonstration, spec plasmode-attribution-demonstration; roadmap Next changes item 12). Phase 6 closed with its attribution leg withheld (exit review §1 G1, §6): neither case study had a global trajectory claim to trace. Item 11 made attribution available with declared covariates and validated it on semi-synthetic data (report). This check plants a known group × stage orientation difference in SEA-AD's real exported blocks, covariates and design, analyses each unit as the case study does, and asks whether the difference is detected and traced back to the planted features. A rule fixed before any unit either lifts the leg or reports it failed.

    Every number below is read from results/plasmode-2026-11-02/: - plasmode_decision.json, plasmode_summary.csv, merge.json, settings.json, sacct.txt, PROVENANCE.txt; - fidelity.json, calibration.json (every calibration iteration) and calibration_sacct.txt; - diagnostic/latent_capture.json (the label-free diagnostic in §4) and the indicative smoke in smoke/ (R = 20 × 99 permutations × B = 50).

    The per-unit records, merged.jsonl, plasmode_units.csv and plasmode_metrics.csv stay on ing; PROVENANCE.txt names them with their sha256. No file in the repository holds a donor identifier, a label vector or a donor-level value. Readings are over R = 500 units per cell at 999 permutations and B = 200 bootstrap replicates, on the observed component of the principal-orientation block of the covariate-adjusted (primary) arm, unless stated otherwise.

The decision file's settings hash 734bb538… equals settings.json's.

    Verdict: attribution_leg_failed, read verbatim from plasmode_decision.json ("indicative": false, 500 replicates per cell, adopted calibration iteration 4, cap_reached). The failing criteria are (ii) detection and two parts of (iii): planted-set precision and top-k frequency.

    In one paragraph. - The construction. SEA-AD MTG astrocytes cohort_30 (n = 80, RNA and ATAC, 10,000 features), analysed exactly as the case-study primary: six covariates, rank floor 2, net displacement, the df correction. In each unit the sex labels are permuted within stage, which removes any real sex × stage structure. Then 20 random features (10 per block) drift with stage in the donors labelled male, up to e within-stage SDs at the last stage. - Size holds on the real data. At the anchor (no plant) delta, angle and shape reject at 0.042, 0.048 and 0.026, under the bound of 0.0695. - The test does not see the plant. Calibration found no ladder step at which angle rejected: 0 of 40 units at every e from 0.5 to 3.0. At e* = 3.0, the cap, angle rejects in 0.036 of 500 units, the null rate. delta (0.026) and shape (0.016) are flat too. - Why. The measurement lives in a five-dimensional stage-PLS space that holds 99.9 % of the pooled stage displacement, but only 2.2 % of a planted direction: what any random direction in 10,000 dimensions gets (§4). The PLS-captured component recovers none of the plant (planted-set precision 0.001). - Attribution sees part of it, unstably. Over all 500 units at e*, about a third of the observed contrast's top-20 is planted (0.344, against 0.0023 at the anchor), with stable signs (0.957), but each planted feature enters the top-20 in only 6 % of bootstrap resamples. - Consequence. The Phase 6 attribution leg is reported failed, on detection first. The case study's non-rejection says nothing about sex differences carried by a few features; Phase 6 is not reopened and no case-study reading changes.

    1. Construction

    scripts/plasmode_check.py (fidelity | calibrate | run | merge | decide) reads the export in place and commits aggregates only.

    Input contract. The export directory is sead_sexdiff's results/motco_input/cohort_30 (export manifest from ab3423a, read with sead_sexdiff at 2d427b0). The check requires its manifest.json to declare exactly the blocks rna and atac (5,000 TMM log2-CPM features each, identity transform), the groups Female/Male and the stage order 0/1/2, and it reads metadata.csv and covariates.csv. A violation (donor mismatch, unknown stage, not exactly two groups, missing covariate, and others) raises a named error before any analysis. The sha256 of the five files read are part of the settings, so a resume on another export is refused.

    Covariates. The primary's six, in its model order: age_at_death, pmi, rin, years_education, log10_n_rna and log10_n_atac. The last two are the base-10 logarithms of n_rna and n_atac, derived in MOTCO as sead_sexdiff's ANALYSIS_LOG10_COLUMNS derives them (src/sead_sexdiff/data.py at 2d427b0); a unit test pins the derivation.

    Fidelity gate. The stage-PLS latent space is stage-supervised and covariate-blind, so it does not depend on the sex labels. One analysis on a within-stage permutation, with no permutations and no attribution, had to reproduce the primary's CV-selected rank 5 and mean held-out stage AUROC 0.635556. It did on the workstation and on ing (fidelity.json: rank 5, AUROC 0.6355555…). Every anchor unit was held to the same check, and all 500 passed (rank 5.0, AUROC 0.635556).

    Null-making step. Each replicate permutes the sex column within each stage, so the cell table [[11, 10, 28], [9, 10, 12]] is kept and every donor's blocks and covariates stay on its own row. A draw equal to the real labels is refused and redrawn; none was. The analysis under the real labels is never run. Size at the anchor is therefore size conditional on this export: the 500 units are label draws on one data matrix.

    Plant. In the donors labelled Male under the permuted labels (31 donors), each feature f of a planted set K is shifted by e · c(stage) · σ_f · s_f: - K is 10 RNA and 10 ATAC features drawn uniformly per replicate, s_f a uniform sign; - σ_f is the export's pooled within-stage SD (ddof = 3), so e is in within-stage SD units at the last stage; - c is 0, 0.5 and 1 at stages 0, 1 and 2, so the ramp adds no offset at baseline.

    At e* = 3.0 a planted feature in a male donor moves by 1.5 within-stage SDs at stage 1 and 3 SDs at stage 2. The plant is nearly orthogonal to the shared trajectory: its cosine with the pooled stage displacement averages 0.0001 (SE 0.0005). At e = 0 the blocks are the export, byte for byte.

    Seeds. Every draw derives from base seed 2034 (no committed check uses it), the stream (paper, calibration or fidelity), the replicate and the purpose (labels, plant, RRPP, bootstrap), never from the strength. The three cells of a replicate share the labels, K, the signs, the RRPP seed and the bootstrap seed.

    Arms, on one analysis per unit. - adjusted (primary). analyze_trajectories with the six covariates, covariate-adjusted attribution (B = 200, top-k 20), min_components 2, net displacement, residual_correction="df", the harness CV knobs (random_state 1203) and 999 permutations. - unadjusted (descriptive). The attribution primitive on the analysis's latent_artifacts without covariates, with the same bootstrap seed. It cannot lift the leg. - Each unit first checked that the exposed estimator reproduces the latent scores and that the adjusted means project to the analysis's stage means (to 1e-8 relative); no unit failed.

    Metrics per arm, block and component: the planted-set precision |top-20 ∩ K| / 20, the mean bootstrap sign stability and top-k frequency over all 20 planted features, and the share of recovered planted features whose observed sign is the planted one.

    3. Verdict and consequence

    Verdict: attribution_leg_failed. Size holds; detection fails at the cap, and so do planted-set precision and top-k frequency. The decision file's consequence, verbatim: "the Phase 6 G1 attribution leg is reported failed, naming the failing criteria; Phase 6 is not reopened and no case-study reading changes".

    • The Phase 6 attribution leg moves from withheld to failed. At the case study's design, the shipped analysis could not be shown to trace a planted orientation difference to its drivers. It fails at detection: a difference the test does not detect is never handed to attribution in the workflow the leg describes.
    • The case-study reading is unchanged and sharpened. The SEA-AD primary's non-rejection was already bracketed as uninformative. It is now also measured to be blind to sex differences confined to a few features: a 3-SD ramp on 20 of 10,000 features is not detected.
    • Size at the case study's configuration holds on the real export, conditionally on it (§1), which complements the semi-synthetic Type I and corrected-permutation reports.

    4. Why the test does not see the plant

    The trajectory statistics are measured in the stage-PLS latent space, five dimensions fitted to the stage label across all 10,000 features. diagnostic/latent_capture.py fits that space at the primary's rank (forced 5) on the unplanted export and measures, in analysed coordinates, the share of a direction's norm that falls inside it:

    • the pooled stage displacement: 99.94 %;
    • 200 planted directions drawn as the check draws them: median 2.16 % (q05–q95 1.11–3.20 %), against 2.24 % for any random direction (√(5/10,000)).

    A planted direction is therefore almost entirely outside the measured subspace. At e = 3 its norm in analysed units is about 13.4 (4.47 per unit of e), and it applies to 31 of 80 donors. Only about 0.3 of those units (2 % of 13.4) can enter the measured subspace, against a pooled stage displacement of 39.7 that lies almost entirely inside it.

    Refitting the space on the planted data does not rescue it. The planted cells' CV-selected rank barely moves (mean 4.85 at e = 3, 4.88 at e = 2, against 5.0 at the anchor), the held-out stage AUROC rises only from 0.6356 to 0.6442, and the PLS-captured component of the attribution recovers no planted feature (planted-set precision 0.001 at e = 3, against 0.432 for the residual component). The plant lives in the part of feature space the measurement discards.

    5. Descriptive readings

    None of these enters the verdict.

    • Unconditional readings at e* (all 500 units): planted-set precision 0.344 (SE 0.009), sign stability over K 0.957 (SE 0.001), top-k frequency over K 0.060 (SE 0.001). They agree with the 18 rejecting units, so the conditioning selected no favourable subset. Where planted features are recovered, their observed sign is the planted one in every case (sign agreement 1.0 over the 478 units with at least one hit).
    • False-recovery reference. At the anchor the planted-set precision is 0.0023 (SE 0.0005) and the sign stability over K 0.699: chance for 20 random features among 10,000. Against it, the observed contrast at e* is about 150 times enriched for the planted set, without being stable.
    • The descriptive cell (e = 2.0). delta / angle / shape reject at 0.030 / 0.034 / 0.020. Unconditionally, planted-set precision is 0.168, sign stability 0.918 and top-k frequency 0.033: the same picture at a lower dose.
    • The unadjusted arm. At e* it recovers more than the adjusted arm: planted-set precision 0.453 over all units (0.375 on the rejecting units), sign stability 0.971, top-k frequency 0.092. Plausibly, refitting six covariate coefficients on n = 80 adds variance to the adjusted contrast, while there is no confounding for it to remove: the within-stage permutation balances the covariates across the permuted groups in expectation. The check does not test that explanation.
    • Components. The PLS-captured component recovers nothing (planted-set precision 0.001 over all units at e*, sign stability 0.691, the anchor's level); the residual component carries the recovery (0.432, 0.955, 0.072).
    • Transitions. The per-transition contrasts recover little (planted-set precision 0.018 for 0->1 and 0.030 for 1->2 over all units at e*), although their signs are stable (0.958 and 0.965). Each transition carries half the ramp, and a single step between two cell means is plausibly noisier than the configuration's principal axis.
    • Degeneracy. The principal axis is flagged near-isotropic in 10.6 % of anchor units and 9.4 % at e = 3 and e = 2.

    6. Limitations and scope

    • One kind of plant. The plant is sparse (20 random features), linear in stage and confined to one group. That is the kind the stage-PLS measurement is blind to by construction (§4). A dense difference aligned with the stage signal, which rotates the shared trajectory inside the measured subspace, was not tested. Testing it would be a new check with its own predeclared rule, not a rescue of this one.
    • A stylized strength. e* = 3.0 within-stage SDs is the predeclared cap, large for 20 features; the design chose not to extend the ladder after calibration. The result is a statement about traceability of a known signal on real noise, covariates and design, not about power at a realistic effect.
    • Conditional size. Size at the anchor is conditional on this export (§1); the unconditional size at the case study's configuration is in the case-study Type I and corrected-permutation reports.
    • Recovery read on rejecting units. Criterion (iii) reads 18 units, most of them chance rejections. The unconditional readings over 500 units agree with it.
    • One cohort. Only astrocyte cohort_30 is measured. Microglia, cohort_50 and ADNI are not.
