    ADNI gain and specificity — report (2026-11-17)

    Change: adni-gain-and-specificity (MOTCO 0.25.0, no package change). Reads: results/adni-gain-specificity-2026-11-17/ (adni_gain_specificity_decision.json; per profile settings.json, preflight.json, rate_table.csv, paired_table.csv, cell_table.csv, accounting.json; plants/reproduction.json; PROVENANCE.txt). Parents: the ADNI real-noise anchor (its rate table and decision, bound by sha256) and the operating characteristics at the ADNI design (its settings and per-unit p-values). Concept page: measurement visibility.

    The ADNI anchor (item 21) found that predictive_orthonormal holds size on ADNI's blocks. Its decision fixed what else reopening the option at a design needs: a gain over production there in simulation, and specificity there, each under a decision declared before its reading. This change reads both, adds protected power, and reads what production itself detects on ADNI's noise. It plants stretches, rotations and bends of the stage program on ADNI's own blocks, under within-stage-permuted labels, and in the simulation of ADNI's design. One decision, fixed before any unit ran, reads both.

    In one paragraph. The verdict is power_loss.

    • The candidate loses target power on one plant. On a 90° rotation of the analysed sample's own stage configuration, production's angle rejects in 0.285 of units at the full cohort and predictive_orthonormal in 0.05. Production alone rejects 47 units and the candidate alone none, below S2's ceiling. At 750 participants the rates are 0.235 and 0.185.
    • It also fails specificity in simulation. A doubled stretch is rejected by shape in 0.295 of units against production's 0.06, and two more off-target readings are below 0.025. On ADNI's in-sample plants it adds no off-target rejection, and it removes most of production's own rotation-into-shape leak.
    • Size holds and the gain is real. Both arms hold size on every added control (S1). In simulation the candidate gains at the Bonferroni threshold in 5 of 8 target cells (G): delta 0.12 → 0.485 at a stretch of 0.5, angle 0.075 → 0.325 at a rotation of 0.5.
    • The consequence fixed with the rule. The reopening condition is not met for ADNI. predictive_orthonormal stays an opt-in diagnostic, and open item 1 closes for ADNI.
    • Production's statement (on its own rates, whatever the verdict):
    • delta reaches the 0.80 floor from a stretch of e = 1 at 750 participants (0.995 in sample, 0.855 held out) and from e = 0.5 in sample at the full cohort (0.825).
    • angle and shape stay below the floor at every effect read; their largest rate is 0.40.
    • Its off-target family leaks: 10 of 32 readings are above the bound and 7 above the ceiling, mostly in-sample rotations read as shape.

The workstation and ing each hold a byte-identical copy. The five contract files' hashes are in plants/settings.json and were unchanged after the run.
    • Profile plants: ADNI's blocks with a planted difference. Each unit permutes the sex labels within stage, leaves blocks and covariates on their rows, and adds a known shift to the second group's rows, one vector per stage. No analysis ran under the real labels.

    Units are the anchor driver's (its subsample, label and RRPP seeds, base seed 2039). The cohort anchor's 500 units are therefore item 21's, and every cell of a point at one replicate shares its sample, labels and permutation seed. - The plant. A configuration is the three stage coefficients of features ~ stage indicators + covariates, in the analysis sample's pooled-standardized coordinates, with the covariates coded as analyze_trajectories codes them. edit_stage_configuration edits it: - a stretch about the centroid by 1 + e, whose intended difference is delta; - a rigid rotation by 90°·e, intended angle; - a bend of the middle stage by e mean steps at fixed path length, intended shape; - a translation by e mean steps along the net displacement, a group main effect with no interaction.

    The shift is the edited configuration minus the original, mapped back through the pooled SD. - Two references for a plant. sample edits the analysis sample's own configuration. Under the label permutation both groups' expected configuration is the sample's, so the off-target truth is exact; the fitted span holds the plant by construction. heldout edits the configuration of the 751 participants outside the sample, a direction no fit was built on, available at n750 only. Its off-target truth is not exact (§9.2). - Cells (<reference>_<mode>_e<effect>; 500 replicates on a control, 200 on a plant, 999 permutations; 4,200 units at n750 and 3,000 at the cohort):

    2. Accounting, checks, pre-flights and the reproduction

    • Units. Plants 7,200 of 7,200 and simulation 2,600 of 2,600 complete: no failed unit, no failed arm, no inconsistent unit, no duplicate. On ADNI's blocks no subsample and no label draw needed a redraw.
    • Checks on every unit. Each forced arm reported its rank, every arm at one rank had the same model, each predictive arm measured two dimensions, and each within-stage arm observed its counterpart's statistics. The realized cell table was the point's. On ADNI's blocks only the second group's stage means moved, by the declared shift (plant). In simulation the truth recorded the stage-span construction at the cell's mode and effect (construction).
    • Plants pre-flight: passed. On cohort replicates 0 and 1 the cross-validated fit selected rank 8 at a held-out stage AUROC of 0.599514, and the call forced at rank 8 gave identical statistics and p-values.
    • Simulation pre-flight: passed. Anchor replicates 0 and 1 of the ADNI-design run, through its own evaluator under its committed settings, gave its committed p-values exactly: 0.29 / 0.153 / 0.195 and 0.187 / 0.746 / 0.387.
    • Anchor reproduction: passed. The cohort anchor's counts for all four arms equal item 21's committed table, 12 of 12 readings (pls_cv 25 / 32 / 28, predictive_orthonormal 29 / 20 / 18, and the two within-stage arms 35 / 38 / 28 and 27 / 28 / 21 of 500).

    3. The decision

    Rule version 1 (design.md D12–D14). Rejection is p ≤ 0.05.

    • S1 (size). Judged for both arms on the added controls: the n750 anchor and the translation at both points (9 readings), and the simulated anchor and translation (6). A family holds with at most one count above 34 of 500 and none above the ceiling (38 and 37).
    • S2, S3 and G. One-sided exact paired tests of predictive_orthonormal against pls_cv on the units' discordant pairs.
    • S2 (protected power): the target statistic of all 30 plants. It holds with at most two loss p-values below 0.025 and none below 0.05 / 30.
    • S3 (specificity): the off-target statistics of the 8 simulated plants (16 readings) and of the 16 in-sample plants (32). Each family holds with at most two excess p-values below 0.025 and none below 0.05 / m.
    • G (gain): at least one of the 8 simulated target readings gains at p ≤ 0.05 / 8.
    • Verdict. gate_failed, then production_excess (production fails S1), size_excess, power_loss (S2), specificity_excess (S3), no_gain (G), else reopened. All failing criteria are listed.
    criterion family readings observed holds
    S1, pls_cv plants; simulation 9; 6 no count above the bound in either yes
    S1, predictive_orthonormal plants; simulation 9; 6 no count above the bound in either yes
    S2 all plants 30 2 p-values below 0.025 (2 allowed), 1 below the ceiling 0.00167 no
    S3 simulated plants 16 3 below 0.025 (2 allowed), 1 below the ceiling 0.003125 no
    S3 in-sample plants 32 none below 0.025 yes
    G simulated targets 8 5 gains at p ≤ 0.00625 (one needed) yes

    The verdict is power_loss, with S2 and S3 failing. The gate passed and the decision is at paper grade (not indicative). The consequence fixed with the rule: "predictive_orthonormal loses target power against production at the named plants. The condition is not met for ADNI, and it stays an opt-in diagnostic. The measurement-visibility page records which differences its two measured dimensions give up at ADNI's design. In every verdict the SEA-AD anchor's reading stands for SEA-AD's design, keep_production stands, and the out-of-span cells are reported beside the verdict and were not judged."

    What the verdict licenses (written into the decision beside item 21's scoping rule S1–S5):

    • L1. "The reading speaks for ADNI's export, the two design points and the simulated table. Nothing is extended to 80, 200 or 500 participants, where only size was read."
    • L2. "At the full cohort, power and specificity were read on in-sample plants and in simulation. No held-out plant exists there. The held-out readings are those of 750 participants."
    • L3. "reopened permits a proposal. It changes no default and adds no option. The later change carries the rank rule open item 1 asks for." (Not reached under this verdict.)
    • L4. "Cohorts do not vouch for one another. The SEA-AD veto stands for SEA-AD's design, and ROSMAP has no reading."
    • L5. "The out-of-span cells license nothing. Outside the stage span the split test's complement is the detector, and its reading on ADNI's noise is not this change's."

    7. Gain (G) and the recorded gains

    G holds. Five of the eight simulated target readings gain at p ≤ 0.00625 (§5): the three stretches (delta 0.12 → 0.485, 0.535 → 0.99, 0.96 → 1.0) and the rotations of 0.5 and 1.0 (angle 0.075 → 0.325 and 0.065 → 0.40). The rotation of 0.25 and both bends do not. Pooled over the eight, the candidate alone rejects 366 units and production alone 45, a mean rate difference of +0.20.

    Recorded beside G (same rule, no criterion):

    • Held-out plants (6 readings): holds. Three gain, the stretch of 1.0 and both rotations (p 0.0035, 5.6e-5, 0.0018). Pooled, c = 127 against b = 58, +0.0575.
    • In-sample plants (16 readings): holds. Eight gain, all on stretches, rotations up to 45° and the cohort's bend of 1.0. Pooled, c = 414 against b = 158, +0.08.

    The gain the simulation shows is also on ADNI's noise. The verdict turns on what is lost, not on what is gained.

    • angle and shape. Neither reaches the floor on either reference at either point up to e = 1. On ADNI's noise a non-rejection of angle or shape stays uninformative, as item 9 found in simulation.

    Off-target: leaks. Production's 32 off-target readings on the in-sample plants, as one family under the count-plus-ceiling rule at 200 units (at most two above 16 of 200, none above 20), have 10 above the bound and 7 above the ceiling:

    On ADNI's noise, a production rejection names that the trajectories differ, not how. A rotation of the stage program reaches shape more often than angle (0.395 against 0.235 at n750, e = 1).

    9.2 Held-out off-target rates and the reference's reliability

    The held-out configuration's split-half correlation over the 500 n750 anchor units has a median of 0.166, and its Spearman–Brown step-up to the full reference a median of 0.285. About 0.3 of a held-out plant's energy is the cohort's stage program and the rest is estimation noise, as design D5 stated. The second eigenvalue of the halves' cross-product has a median ratio to the first of 0.000. Off-target rejections of 200, production / candidate:

    A held-out rotation is read by delta in up to 176 of 200 units. This is the plant's own noise: an edit of a noisy configuration carries an unintended size change. None of these rates was judged.

    10. What follows

    • The reopening condition is not met for ADNI. predictive_orthonormal stays an opt-in diagnostic, and open item 1 closes for ADNI. Open item 2 continues with ROSMAP for production alone.
    • What its two measured dimensions give up at ADNI's design (recorded on the measurement-visibility page):
    • a rotation of the sample's own stage program by 90°, where the pooled stage signal cancels: production reads its angle in units whose CV rank is above 2, and the candidate barely (§9.5);
    • in simulation, specificity on stretches, which it reads as shape more often as they grow.
    • The gains are real on both kinds of noise. They are recorded, and they do not outweigh a loss under the rule.
    • Production's statement is the case study's real-noise power statement. adni_sexdiff may cite it: on ADNI's noise delta detects a stretch of the adjusted stage program from e = 1 at 750 participants (from e = 0.5 in sample at the cohort); angle and shape reach the floor nowhere; and a rejection names a difference, not its kind.
    • The out-of-span cells are the starting point of the split test's reading on ADNI's noise.
    • Standing. The SEA-AD anchor's reading stands for SEA-AD's design, and keep_production stands.

    11. Limitations

    • Subsamples of one cohort are not independent cohorts. Two n750 subsamples share about half their participants, and every rate is conditional on ADNI's 1,501. Independent real noise needs ROSMAP.
    • The two references bracket; neither is the truth. An in-sample plant lies where the stage-supervised fit already looks, and a held-out one is about a third signal (§9.2). The cohort has no held-out plant (L2).
    • The simulation is a structural analogue. Its CV selects rank 19 against ADNI's 8, its blocks are InterSIM's, and its stage program is weak in pooled-SD units. Every failing specificity reading is simulated; on ADNI's in-sample plants the candidate holds.
    • A rotation and a bend are exact to the order of the scale drift. That is under 1.6 % of a pooled SD at effects up to 1.0 (median unit), and both arms read the same data.
    • The plant moves the fit. A cell's rank and AUROC are not the anchor's (§9.6). The failing rotation is the cell where the pooled stage signal cancels and the CV falls to rank 2 in most units.
    • The family rules have false-alarm rates. At exact size a correctly sized arm fails the plants size family with probability at most 0.07 and the simulated one 0.06. The verdict does not rest on a reading near a boundary: both failing families have a reading far below their ceiling (7.1e-15 against 0.00167; 2.2e-10 against 0.003125).
    • One omic type. ADNI's blocks are metabolites at 0.2 to 0.4 features per participant.
