    Covariate-adjusted attribution at the case study's configuration — 2026-11-02

    This report validates covariate-adjusted orientation attribution as motco analyze runs it with declared covariates (openspec change covariate-adjusted-attribution, spec adjusted-attribution-validation; roadmap Next changes item 11). Phase 6 closed with its attribution leg withheld because attribution could not run on a covariate-adjusted contrast (exit review §4 D6). MOTCO 0.14.0 adds it. This check asks whether it removes a confounded covariate from the drivers, and whether it recovers and stabilizes planted drivers as well as an oracle that knows the injection.

    Every number below is read from results/adjusted-attribution-2026-11-02/: - run/adjusted_attribution_decision.json, run/adjusted_attribution_summary.csv, run/merge.json, run/decide.json, run/settings.json, run/sacct.txt, run/PROVENANCE.txt; - calibration/calibration.json (every calibration iteration) and calibration/probe/ (the diagnostic behind the gate amendment); - the smoke smoke/ (R = 20 × 99 permutations × B = 50, indicative) and timing/timing.json.

    run/records/, run/merged.jsonl and the 5.4 MB run/adjusted_attribution_metrics.csv stay on ing; run/PROVENANCE.txt names them with their sha256. Readings are means over R = 500 replicates per cell at 999 permutations and B = 200 bootstrap replicates, on the observed component of the principal-orientation block unless stated otherwise.

    Verdict: adjusted_attribution_validated, read verbatim from adjusted_attribution_decision.json ("indicative": false, "failing": [], 500 replicates per cell, adopted calibration iteration 9).

    In one paragraph. - The construction. The case study's configuration: the astrocyte cohort_30 table (n = 80), methylation + expression, a covariate z confounded with group × stage and injected into 20 % of the features at s_z = 10, five noise covariates (six design columns), rank floor 2, net displacement and the df correction. The stage effect is the Phase 6 one (δ = 2.0), so there is an orientation to plant. Each unit ran the shipped analysis once, then attributed its frozen model three ways: adjusted (the analysis's own attribution), an oracle with the injection removed exactly, and unadjusted. - The confounding is real. Without adjustment the covariate takes 0.43 of the top-20 at the anchor beyond the oracle, and tilts the contrast toward the injection direction by about 0.51 in every cell. - Adjustment removes it. The adjusted arm's contamination is within 0.005 of the oracle's, and its residual projection is −0.003 to −0.002, under 1 % of the unadjusted arm's 0.51–0.52. - At no cost to recovery or stability. Against the oracle, truth precision is +0.038 / +0.005, sign stability +0.034 / +0.017 and top-k frequency −0.011 / −0.009 at e = 0.5 / 1.0, all inside their margins. - The gates were amended before any unit. Calibration under the declared rule could not be satisfied: at the orientation cells the planted drivers crowd every injected feature out of the unadjusted top-20 at any injection strength. With the user's approval the contamination gate moved to the anchor and a projection gate was added at every cell (§1); every criterion and margin is as declared. - Consequence. Item 12, the plasmode demonstration on SEA-AD's real astrocyte blocks, may start on 0.14.0.

    1. Construction

    scripts/adjusted_attribution_check.py composes the case-study construction by import (case_study_type_i_check, built on the D10 construction of covariate_type_i_check):

    • Generator. The semi-synthetic generator at the astrocyte table [[11, 10, 28], [9, 10, 12]], three stages, methylation (367 CpGs) and expression (131 genes), joint magnitude, p_dmp 0.1 and the Phase 6 per-omic stage effect δ = 2.0. The cells are the zero-effect anchor and the orientation surgery at e = 0.5 and 1.0.
    • Covariates, in model order (six numeric design columns): z = a_g·g + a_s·s + a_gs·g·s + ε (D10's coefficients 0.5 / 1.0 / 2.0, noise SD 1), injected as X_f += s_z·sd_f·w_f·z on a fixed 20 % feature subset (100 of 498 features); and x1 … x5, i.i.d. N(0, 1), never injected.
    • Seeds. Every seed derives from base seed 2033 (no committed check uses it) and the replicate index, so the three cells of a replicate share the baseline draw, the covariates, the injection, the RRPP seed and the bootstrap seed.
    • Truth. The generator's driver set: features whose group-stage differential mean change differs between groups (the union over transitions for the principal block). It holds 156 features on average at e = 0.5 and 291 at e = 1.0, none at the anchor; 32 and 59 of the 100 injected features are drivers there.

    Arms, on one measurement per unit. - adjusted. analyze_trajectories with the six covariates, attribution (B = 200, top-k 20), min_components 2, net displacement, residual_correction="df", the harness CV knobs and 999 permutations. - oracle. The attribution primitive on X̃ − z ⊗ (v / σ): the analysed features minus the exact raw-unit injection v = s_z·sd·w scaled by the pooled analysed scale σ, so its cell means are the uninjected ones up to a constant vector. Same frozen estimator, bootstrap seed, B and top-k. - unadjusted. The primitive on the analysed features as they are (descriptive). - All three draw the same within-cell bootstrap resamples. Each unit first checks that the exposed estimator reproduces the latent scores (to 1e-10 of the largest) and that the adjusted means project to the analysis's stage means (to 1e-8 relative); no unit failed either check.

    The measured analyses. The held-out CV stage AUROC is 1.000 in every cell. The CV-selected rank is 2–3 at the anchor (292 / 208 units), 2–4 at e = 0.5 (median 3) and 2–6 at e = 1.0 (median 3). The adjusted analysis's angle rejects at 0.046 at the anchor, 0.256 at e = 0.5 and 0.666 at e = 1.0.

    Calibration and the gate amendment

    Calibration ran R = 20 × 99 permutations × B = 50 at the anchor and orientation e = 1.0, starting at s_z = 10 and stepping by 5. Every iteration is in calibration/calibration.json.

    Under the declared rule (contamination gap, unadjusted − oracle, at least 0.25 in both cells), iterations 0–8 at s_z 10–50 ended stop_exhausted:

    15 0.4225 0.000 0.975

    Raising s_z lowered the anchor's gap and never opened one at e = 1.0: in standardized units an injected feature converges to z's own cell pattern as s_z grows. The diagnostic in calibration/probe/ (six replicates per row, no permutations, indicative) showed why. At e = 1.0 only about 39 injected features are not drivers, and their best rank in the unadjusted contrast was 30th to 90th. Weakening the stage effect to δ = 0.25 did not change that (0.0–0.07). The unadjusted − oracle projection on the injection direction was about 0.5 in every cell nonetheless. The confounding reaches the contrast; it does not reach the top-20 when a strong orientation is planted.

    The amendment (2026-11-02, approved by the user before any smoke or paper-grade unit; design.md D10). The contamination gate reads the anchor only, and a projection gate, |unadjusted − oracle projection| − 2·SE ≥ 0.2, reads every cell. Every criterion and margin is unchanged. Under this rule (version 2), iteration 9 at s_z = 10 met every criterion and was adopted: contamination gap 0.485 at the anchor, projection gaps 0.563 and 0.547, oracle precision 0.975. Its readings equal iteration 0's.

    The construction is informative. The covariate fills the unadjusted top-20 where nothing else competes (0.475 against the oracle's 0.050 at the anchor), and it bends the unadjusted contrast in every cell. The planted drivers are recoverable: the oracle's top-20 at e = 1.0 is 98.5 % drivers.

    The residual-confounding bound is 0.10 × the unadjusted arm's |mean projection difference| in the same cell (0.5091, 0.5207, 0.5113), so adjustment removes over 99 % of the confounding the unadjusted contrast carries. The adjusted arm's truth precision and sign stability sit slightly above the oracle's, plausibly because the adjustment also removes the features' chance within-cell association with the five noise covariates, which the oracle keeps; the check does not test that explanation. Top-k frequency is 0.009–0.011 below the oracle's, a tenth of its margin: the six refitted coefficients per replicate add some spread to the per-feature ranks.

    The adjusted contrast is not the oracle's contrast. Their cosine is 0.55 at the anchor and 0.74 / 0.79 at e = 0.5 / 1.0, and their top-20 overlap 0.28 / 0.50 / 0.50; the unadjusted arm's are lower (0.49 / 0.69 / 0.74 and 0.17 / 0.43 / 0.44). Estimating the coefficients on n = 80 moves the per-feature contrast; the criteria ask whether that movement reintroduces the confounder or loses drivers, and it does neither.

    4. Verdict and consequence

    Verdict: adjusted_attribution_validated. Both confounding gates and the recoverability gate hold, and every criterion holds at every cell. The decision file's consequence, verbatim: "covariate-adjusted attribution is validated at the case study's configuration; item 12 (the plasmode demonstration on SEA-AD astrocyte cohort_30) may start on the released version (0.14.0)".

    • Item 12 is proposed next, pinned to MOTCO 0.14.0. Its proposal fixes the detection rate, the recovery thresholds and the null-making step on the real blocks. The Phase 6 attribution leg stays withheld until it reports.
    • motco analyze. With declared covariates, --attribution reports covariate-adjusted drivers; the record's attribution.adjustment block names the means it used. The validation status in docs/api/analysis.md now cites this report.

    5. Descriptive readings

    None of these enters the verdict.

    • The unadjusted arm. Read against the same bounds, it fails contamination at the anchor (0.4485 against 0.05) and residual confounding everywhere (0.52–0.53 against about 0.05). Its truth precision is higher than the oracle's (+0.084 / +0.013): injected features that are also drivers get a boost and are counted as correct, which is why the contamination metric counts only injected non-drivers.
    • The PLS-captured component. Contamination holds (≤ 0.019). Residual confounding reads 0.045 / 0.037 / 0.023 against 0.019 / 0.019 / 0.031. On the means, adjustment removes 85 / 89 / 97 % of the captured component's confounding, short of 90 % at the anchor and e = 0.5. The captured view projects the adjusted means through a basis fitted without the covariates, so a share of the injection direction that lies in that basis is reconstructed with the signal. The driver report uses the observed component (design D9).
    • The residual component. Contamination holds. On the means, adjustment removes 90 / 93 / 94 % of its confounding, but the 2-SE readings, 0.049 / 0.040 / 0.025, exceed the bounds 0.032 / 0.033 / 0.020; truth precision at e = 0.5 reads −0.052 against −0.05. The residual is observed minus captured, so it inherits the captured view's remainder with the opposite sign.
    • The transitions. Both transition blocks hold every criterion at every cell, with margins wider than the principal block's (residual confounding 0.003 against about 0.028).
    • The angle-rejected units. Restricted to units whose adjusted analysis rejects angle at 0.05 (23 / 128 / 333 units), every criterion holds (largest contamination reading 0.0137; truth precision at e = 0.5 −0.0131).
    • Rank-deficient replicates. None in any unit. All six covariates are numeric, so no resample can drop a factor level.

    6. Limitations and scope

    • Structural analogue. The blocks are InterSIM omics (367 methylation and 131 expression features, 498), not SEA-AD's 10,000 RNA and ATAC features. Item 12 runs on the real blocks.
    • One table, one stage effect. Only the astrocyte table (item 12's cohort) at δ = 2.0 is measured. At SEA-AD's own stage signal (held-out AUROC 0.63–0.70) the stage transitions are near zero and every arm's contrast is noise; how attribution behaves there is item 12's question.
    • Numeric covariates only. The rank-deficient path for a rare factor level is unit-tested, not measured here.
    • The covariate model. Covariates enter additively and linearly, as in the trajectory models; the interpretation boundary in every adjusted result says so. Adjustment removes only declared covariates.
    • The amended gates. The contamination gate is read at the anchor only. At the orientation cells, removal is judged by the projection, which does not depend on the top-k, while contamination there is a criterion without a gate.
    • The observed principal block. The verdict reads one component and one block. The PLS-captured and residual views hold contamination but not the 90 % residual-confounding bound at every cell (§5).
