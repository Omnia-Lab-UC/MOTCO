    Phase 6 exit review — 2026-11-02

    Decision: Phase 6 is CLOSED on a size-controlled null; the attribution leg withheld, lifted by a named demonstration or reported as failed on its own terms. The exit gate is recorded as not met as written. Its reproducibility criterion is met. Its driver-tracing criterion is unexercised: neither case study has a global trajectory claim to trace, which is different from failed. The phase's purpose is demonstrated up to inference: an end-to-end, reproducible, size-controlled analysis of two real cohorts whose non-rejections are measured (ADNI) or bracketed (SEA-AD) to be uninformative. The attribution leg is withheld, not waived. Two consequent changes, each with its own proposal, lift it or report it failed (§6), as the Phase 5 review withheld its magnitude specificity claim and the 2026-10-08 addendum lifted it. Phase 7 stays deferred.

    1. The exit-gate criteria

    Phase 6's purpose is "demonstrate that the validated method answers a meaningful biological question end to end". Its exit gate has two parts. Each is recorded with one of three values: met, not met or unexercised.

    criterion status evidence
    G1 — collaborators can trace each global trajectory claim to stable feature/module drivers unexercised; attribution unavailable at the case-study configuration No claim exists. SEA-AD rejects no statistic in any of its 44 analyses (SEA-AD report, Result and §11), and ADNI's primary rejects none (ADNI report, Summary). Had a claim existed, attribution could not have run on it: analyze_trajectories refuses attribution whenever covariates are declared (src/motco/analysis.py:519–523), and both primaries declare six covariate columns (§4, D6).
    G2 — collaborators can reproduce the result from documented inputs and configuration met SEA-AD: the re-issue's gates and reproducibility check are byte-identical on the re-issue machine, and every output that reads no p-value reproduces the superseded outputs made on another machine at rounding level, with every rank and CV AUROC equal (SEA-AD report §12). ADNI: adni-sexdiff report --check regenerates the report from the committed outputs and compares it cell by cell (ADNI report, Reproduction). Both run every analysis through one entry point, motco.analysis.analyze_trajectories, and record its motco analyze CLI equivalent.
    the gate as a whole not met as written G2 is met; G1 is unexercised, not met.
    the purpose demonstrated up to inference Two real designs are analysed end to end with size measured at both primaries (§2), and their non-rejections are measured or bracketed to be uninformative (§2, §5). The interpretive half, the drivers, has no real-data demonstration (§6).

    The vacuous pass is refused. Read literally, "each global trajectory claim" ranges over an empty set, and the gate would pass with nothing traced. This review does not take that reading. The purpose sentence and the conditional Work bullet ("For significant orientation, …") both presuppose a detected difference, and a gate that passes because nothing was found would mean nothing. Nor is G1 recorded as failed: failure would say that attribution was tried on a claim and produced no stable drivers, and nothing of the kind happened.

    2. The evidence ledger

    Every size and power statement carries one of three provenance classes:

    • measured: a committed decision file at that design and configuration;
    • bracketed: measured at neighbouring designs whose stage signal or n encloses the cohort's;
    • inferred: from a mechanism, such as κ = √(n / (n − r)) depending on n and the reduced rank r only.

    The paper may state a measured or a bracketed statement, naming its class. It may not turn a bracket into a number for the cohort. Rates are rejection rates at p ≤ 0.05; delta / angle / shape in that order.

    analysis group design result size power at its stage signal

    Why SEA-AD's power is a bracket and not a measurement. The SEA-AD cohorts' held-out AUROCs (0.6356–0.6965) lie inside the pilot's δ-arm range (0.553–0.729), where no statistic has power at n = 200. They lie above the rank-floor check's 0.516 / 0.580, where every rate sits at the null at the astrocyte cohort's own table, without covariates. No run was made at n = 80 and AUROC 0.6356–0.6965 with the case study's covariates. The non-rejections are uninformative on both sides of the bracket, but the bracket gives no number for the cohort.

    The binding readings, as the case studies predeclared them:

    • SEA-AD primaries (SEA-AD report, reading rules and §11). The angle and shape non-rejections are uninformative at this n, and the angle null is too wide to inform a non-rejection. The delta non-rejections are weak evidence against a size difference, since delta is conservative under df (0.031 / 0.029 pooled). The CV-selected rank, eigengap and null summary are reported beside angle.
    • SEA-AD arms. Each is read beside the primary under its own rules. No rank, preprocessing, latent-adjustment or exclusion dependence was found. The latent_adjusted arm's angle and shape p-values are reported, not read.
    • ADNI primary (ADNI report, READING_RULES). delta is not rejected; the angle and shape non-rejections are uninformative by rule 3, which states that no power statement exists at this design. The ADNI-design check turns that absence into a measurement and extends it to delta: "a non-rejection is uninformative up to the construction maximum" for all three statistics (ADNI-design report §4). adni_sexdiff confirms rule 3 in its own change (§8).
    • ADNI sensitivities. A rejection status that differs from the primary's is dependence on the arm's setting, never a replacement of the primary (READING_RULES 1), and the rank sweep's rules count no ranks that reject (sweep rules 2, 5 and 6; §4, D5).

    3. What Phase 6 establishes, withholds, and does not allow to be claimed

    Claimable now:

    • An end-to-end, reproducible real-data analysis of two designs through one entry point. SEA-AD (n = 80, RNA + ATAC pseudobulk, 44 analyses) and ADNI (n = 1,501, serum metabolomics, 15 analyses) each ran every analysis through analyze_trajectories. Each had predeclared reading rules, sensitivity to rank and preprocessing, and a reproduction record (§1, G2).
    • Size at both primaries, measured. SEA-AD cohort_30 under df: pooled 0.029–0.058 per cohort table and statistic against 0.0597 (corrected-permutation report §2). ADNI: 0.034–0.068 against 0.0695 (ADNI-design report §2).
    • The null result, stated with its uninformativeness. Neither primary rejects any statistic: SEA-AD astrocyte and microglia cohort_30, and ADNI. The non-rejections are uninformative. At ADNI this is measured: no statistic reaches the 0.80 floor at any construction effect. At SEA-AD it is bracketed, and stated as a bracket (§2).
    • The design finding (§5): at these designs the held-out stage signal, not n, limits power.
    • The configuration lessons. Each is a measured MOTCO result that a real analysis ran into first:
    • where the stage signal is weak, the CV rank needs a floor of 2 (rank-floor report, adopt_floor; MOTCO 0.10.0);
    • with six covariate columns at n = 80, the Freedman–Lane null shrinks with the reduced model's residual df, and the df correction restores size (case-study diagnosis, residual_df_shrinkage; corrected-permutation report, adopt_df; MOTCO 0.13.0);
    • the covariate-blind basis follows technical axes where they dominate the variance (SEA-AD library composition, ADNI assay phase). The axis diagnostics report them, and no statistic is re-read for them. The stage latent adjustment is inadmissible for angle and shape at n = 80 (residualized_basis);
    • the held-out CV stage AUROC is the observable that places a cohort on the power axis (§5).

    Withheld, with a named path to lift it:

    • Driver tracing on real data: G1, the Phase 6 Work bullet for attribution, and the roadmap's objective 4. It is withheld because it was never exercised. It has not failed, and it is not waived. Items 11 and 12 lift it, or report it failed on its own terms (§6). The paper's real-data section states it as withheld and names the gap.

    Not claimable:

    • Any agreement reading. A non-rejection of delta, angle or shape in either cohort is not evidence that the sexes' trajectories agree in size, direction or shape. Both case studies' rules say so, and the measured or bracketed power says why.
    • The sensitivity-arm rejections as results. ADNI's delta rejections at forced rank 6 (p 0.0471) and under nmr_all_columns (p 0.0447) are setting dependence under the case study's predeclared rules (§4, D5).
    • A numeric power for SEA-AD. The bracket says that the non-rejections are uninformative. It gives no power number at n = 80 and AUROC 0.6356 (§2).
    • Metabolite- or feature-level claims from the structural analogue. Both operating-characteristic constructions use InterSIM blocks (498 features), and the ADNI one differs from ADNI on its latent geometry (§4, D7). They support statements about what the analysis can detect at the design. They support nothing about metabolite-, gene- or peak-level behaviour.

    4. Resolution of each deviation

    D1 — No global trajectory claim to trace. Claim revision. Neither primary rejects: SEA-AD astrocyte cohort_30 reads p 0.501 / 0.449 / 0.331 and ADNI p 0.1116 / 0.6879 / 0.7105. SEA-AD rejects nothing in any of its 44 analyses. The mechanism is power, not size: size is measured at both primaries, and power is absent at both designs (§2, §5). G1 is recorded as unexercised and the phase closes on the null. No further analysis of either cohort is run to produce a claim. The case studies' predeclared rules forbid that. SEA-AD's reading rules state that "nothing is re-tuned", and ADNI's READING_RULES 9 reads "The analysis is run once at these settings; a second run at other settings is a new proposal". Sources: SEA-AD report, reading rules and §11; ADNI report, Summary and Reading rules.

    D2 — The rank-1 angle degeneracy. Method revision (MOTCO 0.10.0). Under the 0.9.0 rule both SEA-AD cohorts selected rank 1. There angle is 0° or 180° by construction, and its RRPP null is a two-point distribution. minimum-latent-rank floors the CV candidates at 2. The rank-floor check returned adopt_floor at δ = 0.05 and 0.1: the floor-2 arm holds 0.0695 at the anchor (delta 0.042 / 0.044, angle 0.054 / 0.060, shape 0.036 / 0.038). The floor defines angle and shape in at least two dimensions; it does not make them powerful. Source: rank-floor report §1–§4; results/rank-floor-2026-10-23/delta0.05/rank_floor_decision.json, results/rank-floor-2026-10-23/delta0.1/rank_floor_decision.json.

    D3 — Residual-df shrinkage of the Freedman–Lane null. Method revision (MOTCO 0.13.0); the case study was re-issued with no status change. At the case study's configuration (n = 80, six covariate columns) the covariate-adjusted primary exceeded the bound on translation delta at δ = 0.1: 0.072 against 0.0695 (case-study Type I report, diagnose_configuration_excess). The diagnosis found residual_df_shrinkage. The reduced model's residuals have mean square RSS / n rather than RSS / (n − p_red), with p_red = 10. Pooled delta rises 0.0545 → 0.0595 → 0.066 with one, three and six covariate columns. The opt-in df correction multiplies the residuals by κ = √(n / (n − r)). The corrected-permutation check returned adopt_df, with pooled rates of 0.029–0.058. SEA-AD then re-issued every MOTCO-dependent output under df: every p-value moved up or stayed, and no rejection status changed in any of the 44 analyses (SEA-AD report, Result and §12). ADNI keeps the uncorrected null, because κ ≈ 1.003 at n = 1,501 is immaterial (ADNI-design report §1) and delta is conservative there (0.034, §2).

    D4 — The covariate-blind basis follows technical axes, and the stage latent adjustment is inadmissible for angle and shape. Claim revision. SEA-AD: the first latent component of the astrocyte cohort_30 primary tracks ribosomal_mean (|ρ| 0.840858) more closely than stage (ρ 0.491212). In microglia it tracks mitochondrial_pseudobulk (0.843828, against stage 0.688175) (SEA-AD report §5). The positive control, microglia against astrocytes, is a control for the stage signal. It does not hold on the primary basis in cohort_30, through E3 alone (0.6882 against mitochondrial_pseudobulk 0.8438), and it holds on the latent_adjusted basis (E3 0.5279 against tmm_rna 0.4153) (SEA-AD report §10). ADNI: the primary's first component tracks the assay phase (|ρ| 0.542; ADNI report, Axis diagnostics of the primary). The stage latent adjustment that would remove such axes is residualized_basis at n = 80: at a forced rank of 2 it still exceeds the bound on anchor angle (0.084 / 0.082; case-study diagnosis). The claim is revised, not the method: - an axis correlated with a technical variable is a measurement-space finding, reported beside the statistics and never a reinterpretation of them; - the latent_adjusted arm's angle and shape p-values are reported, not read; - the covariate-blind analysis stays the primary in both case studies.

    D5 — ADNI's two sensitivity-arm delta rejections. Claim revision: none; the predeclared reading stands. delta rejects at forced rank 6 (p 0.0471), one of six forced ranks. It also rejects under the nmr_all_columns export variant (p 0.0447), one of six export variants (ADNI report, Rank sensitivity and Export sensitivity). Under adni_sexdiff's predeclared sweep and membership rules both are setting dependence. The primary's non-rejection stays the result, and the sweep's rules count no ranks that reject (sweep rules 2, 5 and 6). - A liberal null does not explain them. Size holds at the design, and delta is conservative there (0.034; ADNI-design report §2). - They are not a claim. No multiplicity-controlled rule makes one. - They are not a G1 candidate. Running attribution on them would be the forking path this review closes, and it would meet the gap of D6.

    D6 — Attribution is unavailable with covariates. Capability gap → item 11. Two guards block it: - analyze_trajectories raises "Attribution is not available with covariates: it works from unadjusted cell means" whenever covariates are declared (src/motco/analysis.py:519–523, pinned by tests/test_analysis.py:579). - The lower-level analyze_orientation_attribution accepts covariate-adjusted means only as a precomputed mean_table, and then requires bootstrap_replicates = 0, because "row-level bootstrap semantics are unavailable" (src/motco/stats/attribution.py:368–372).

    Both case-study primaries declare six covariate columns. So a claim from either primary could not have been traced to stable drivers as the analysis is built today. Unadjusted attribution is no substitute: under a confounded covariate the unadjusted contrast is a different estimand, and the covariate Type I check's unadjusted arm rejected shape at 0.364 on null data (covariate Type I report §2).

    D7 — The structural analogue is wider at ADNI. Claim scope. The ADNI-design construction matches ADNI on the calibrated quantities: held-out AUROC 0.606 against 0.600, and first-component |ρ| with phase 0.578 against 0.542. It does not match ADNI's latent geometry: - CV rank 19 in 494 of 500 anchor units, against 8; - eigengap 0.330 against 0.744; - angle null q95 51.0° against 35.4°.

    Its blocks are InterSIM omics (498 features), not ADNI's 298 metabolites (ADNI-design report §1, §5). Its measured rates say what the analysis can detect at the design; they say nothing about metabolite-level behaviour. The SEA-AD statements carry the same limitation (the same 498 InterSIM features). The ADNI-design report records it as wider at ADNI, because there the construction's rank sits at the cap and its eigengap is less than half of ADNI's.

    5. The cross-study finding and the binding reading

    At these designs the held-out stage signal, not n, limits power.

    run n held-out stage AUROC best power source
    ADNI-design check 1,501 0.606 (ADNI 0.600) delta at most 0.530 (SE 0.022), at a doubled stage effect in one group (e = 1.0); angle and shape at the null rate ADNI-design report §1, §3
    design-guidance slice (three stages, two blocks) 80 1.000 (mean ≥ 0.9998) delta 0.620 (SE 0.022) at e = 0.1 slice report §3.1, §4
    design-guidance pilot, δ arm (δ = 0.05 / 0.1) 200 0.553 / 0.729 no statistic above 0.09 at any effect (magnitude ≤ 0.03, orientation ≤ 0.07, shape ≤ 0.09; R = 100) pilot report §3.3, §3.4, §4

    A cohort almost nineteen times larger, at AUROC 0.60, detects a doubled stage effect less often than n = 80 at a saturated stage signal detects a 10 % size change. The SEA-AD cohorts (AUROC 0.6356–0.6965) sit inside the δ arm's bracket, at a smaller n than it measured.

    Binding reading, for the paper and for any later real-data use of motco analyze: a non-rejection is informative only where a measured operating characteristic at the cohort's own design and stage signal says so; the held-out CV stage AUROC is reported beside the CV-selected rank with every result. - It extends the Phase 6 statement that already requires the rank beside any angle result, and replaces nothing in it. - It is a reading condition, not a power model. Three regimes do not fit a power curve in AUROC, and none is offered.

    6. The withheld leg and its lifting criterion

    The gap. The lifting demonstration must run at the configuration in which a real claim would be read: covariates declared, the rank floor, net displacement and, at small n, df. Attribution as built cannot run there (D6: the guards at src/motco/analysis.py:519–523 and src/motco/stats/attribution.py:368–372). Two consequent changes follow, in order, each with its own proposal.

    Item 11 — covariate-adjusted attribution in motco analyze (MOTCO). It has three parts: - attribution from covariate-adjusted cell means, evaluated at the record's reference row; - bootstrap stability that refits the adjustment within each resample on the frozen basis; - its own validation that the attribution of an adjusted contrast is stable and unbiased under a confounded covariate.

    It is byte-identical without covariates. It would modify real-data-trajectory-analysis and orientation-driver-attribution. Its proposal decides, after a smoke timing, whether the validation needs cluster compute.

    Item 12 — the plasmode demonstration (after item 11). It lifts the leg or reports it failed. - Data. The real exported blocks, covariates and design of SEA-AD astrocyte cohort_30, the primary cohort. SEA-AD is open data, while ADNI's are under a data-use agreement. This configuration's size under df is measured. And ADNI's analogue sits at the CV cap, so its latent geometry is the less understood of the two. - Construction. A planted group × stage difference of a declared kind, with a known feature set, is added to the real data at declared strengths. The orientation kind comes first, because the Work bullet is conditioned on orientation. Each plasmode unit is analysed by motco analyze at the case-study configuration with attribution. - Lift criteria. The leg is lifted when all three of these hold: - (i) the unplanted construction holds size at the one-sided bound α + 2·√(α(1 − α)/R); - (ii) the planted difference is detected at a rate fixed in item 12's proposal; - (iii) the planted features are recovered among the attribution's top drivers, with bootstrap sign stability and top-k frequency above thresholds fixed in that proposal before any run.

    Otherwise the leg is reported failed on its own terms, with the failing criterion named. - What this review leaves to item 12's proposal. Three things are left open. The first is the detection rate in (ii). The second is the thresholds in (iii), which are fixed there before any run. The third is the null-making step for (i): real sex labels may carry a real difference, so "unplanted" data need one, such as a within-stage label permutation that preserves the covariates. The proposal may also add an unadjusted arm as a descriptive comparison. That arm cannot lift the leg (D6). - Ownership. MOTCO owns the planting construction and the decision rule, as it does for its other validation checks. It reads sead_sexdiff's export through a documented input contract and commits aggregates only. sead_sexdiff supplies the export, and no donor-level data are committed to MOTCO. Item 12's proposal confirms the split.

    If item 11 proves harder than expected, the leg stays withheld and the paper reports it so, with the gap named. The closure does not depend on it.

    7. Phase 7 stays deferred — unchanged

    SNF-native trajectory statistics stay deferred on the Phase 5 exit review's §5 grounds. Nothing in either case study bears on SNF: both measured the PLS latent space throughout.
