    Case-study configuration diagnosis — 2026-10-30

    This report diagnoses the size excess that the case-study configuration Type I check found at the SEA-AD case study's configuration (openspec change case-study-configuration-diagnosis, spec case-study-configuration-diagnosis). It covers two findings: the covariate-adjusted model's delta, and the stage latent adjustment's anchor angle and shape.

    Every number below is read from results/case-study-diagnosis-2026-10-30/: - case_study_diagnosis_decision.json, diagnosis_contrasts.csv, decide.json; - per run in delta0.1/ and delta0.05/: diagnosis_rates.csv, diagnosis_rank_table.csv, diagnosis_reproduction.json, merge.json, settings.json, PROVENANCE.txt; - the smoke records smoke-delta0.1/, smoke-delta0.05/ and smoke-decision/.

    diagnosis_pvalues.csv, merged.jsonl and records/ stay on ing; each PROVENANCE.txt names them with their sha256. Rates are rejection rates at p ≤ 0.05 over R = 500 replicates at 999 permutations. The bound is the parent check's one-sided α + 2·√(α(1 − α)/R) = 0.0695, i.e. at most 34 rejections in 500.

    Run. - Two 50-shard SLURM arrays on ing (partition 512x1024, n11 and the heavily loaded n3 excluded), from the clean worktree of feat/case-study-configuration-diagnosis at 5c30c51. The git tree is 4fe2050d…, identical to the workstation, and the diagnosis tests passed there. - Job 930358 ran the δ = 0.1 parent and job 930408 the δ = 0.05 parent: 50 / 50 tasks COMPLETED 0:0 in each, 10.7 + 14.4 = 25.1 recorded core-hours (34 planned). - 1,000 units per parent (the zero-effect anchor and the translation control at e = 1.0, × 500 replicates). Each unit ran four analyses and twelve RRPP-only arms, with 0 arm failures. - Every unit passed three hard checks: - the reconstruction of the fitted covariate-blind projection matched the analysis's latent scores (maximum error 2.7e-13 against the 1e-9 tolerance); - X − X0 and Y − Y0 were rank one in z (maximum residual 6.5e-16); - the script's z_noise5 equalled analyze_trajectories exactly.

    Reproduction. The diagnosis regenerated the parent's data, covariates, CV folds and RRPP seeds. z_noise5 reproduces the parent's adjusted arm, and latent_stage_cv its latent_stage arm, with 0 mismatches over 3,000 + 3,000 p-values per parent (diagnosis_reproduction.json, status holds in both runs). So every derived arm below sits on the parent's exact units, and the decision's gate is open.

    Verdicts. Both are read verbatim from case_study_diagnosis_decision.json ("decision": "diagnosed", "indicative": false): - Covariate-adjusted delta: residual_df_shrinkage. The column contrast C2 (z_noise5 vs z_noise0) is significant (one-sided p = 0.015). The residual-df corrected case-study model holds the bound for every statistic at both controls in both runs, and its own column contrast C4 is not significant (p = 0.156). - stage latent adjustment: residualized_basis. At a forced rank of 2, with no cross-validation, it still exceeds on anchor angle in both runs (0.084 / 0.082), although its shape now holds.

    In one paragraph. - The design. The diagnosis reran the parent check's exact units: - on the covariate-blind basis, it crossed the confounded covariate z (estimated in the model, or its injection removed exactly from the latent scores) with 0, 2 or 5 pure-noise covariates; - each of those six arms was rerun with the Freedman–Lane residuals rescaled by κ = √(n / (n − p_red)); - the stage latent adjustment ran at the CV floor of 2, at floor 1 and at a forced rank of 2. - delta rises with the column count and the correction removes it. Pooled over the four control readings (2,000 units), delta rises 0.0545 → 0.0595 → 0.066 with z in the model and 0.0525 → 0.0545 → 0.060 under the oracle. The corrected arms read 0.0475–0.0545, with no arm above the bound in any reading. - Estimating z costs no delta size. C3 (z_noise0 vs oracle_noise0) has p = 0.39. - What this means. The case study's delta excess is the Freedman–Lane null's residual-df shrinkage at ten reduced-model columns and n = 80. It is not the covariate's confounding. - The stage arm. Its anchor angle excess sits at rank 2 itself. Its shape excess sits in the CV rank's tail, and a fixed rank of 2 cures shape but not angle. - Consequences. The case study's primary waits for a MOTCO change offering the corrected permutation, validated by its own Type I and power check. The stage sensitivity arm stays inadmissible at this configuration, at a fixed rank too.

    1. Construction

    scripts/case_study_diagnosis.py (design D1) binds each run to one committed run directory of the parent check (--parent).

    Parent binding. - It refuses a parent whose settings.json fails its recorded hash. - It also refuses one whose settings case_study_type_i_check.build_settings does not rebuild from the recorded arguments to the same hash. - The diagnosis's own settings.json carries the parent's settings, path and hash: ca57cc38… against parent 899ce29a… at δ = 0.1, and 6c5d447a… against 97eb6d90… at δ = 0.05.

    The parent's construction is unchanged: - the Phase 6 SEA-AD design, [[11, 10, 28], [9, 10, 12]] with n = 80, methylation + expression; - the per-omic stage effect weakened to δ = 0.1 or 0.05; - z = 0.5·g + 1·s + 2·g·s + ε, injected at s_z = 10 along a fixed direction; - five i.i.d. N(0, 1) noise covariates x1–x5; - the rank floor of 2, the harness CV knobs and the net-displacement estimator.

    • Y and the factorial. Y is the latent score matrix of one analyze_trajectories call with the six covariates on the covariate-blind basis. The six factorial arms are inference-only on that basis: the analysis's design, _covariate_adjusted_design, estimate_difference and serial RRPP.
    • The oracle (design D3).
    • X0 is D10's build at s_z = 0 with the same seeds.
    • The fitted covariate-blind map is reconstructed from the block scalers, the PLS centring and ddof = 1 scaling, and the PLS rotations, and checked against the analysis's latent scores.
    • Y0 is X0 through that map. It equals Y minus z times a fixed latent vector, which the tests check against the analytic injection.
    • The correction (design D5). Each corrected arm runs RRPP on ŷ_red + κ·e_red with the arm's models and seed: the same permutation indices, the uncorrected observed statistics, and p-values by the analysis's rule.

    Contrasts (design D7) are paired on the same units: - For a contrast of arm B against arm A, replicate r contributes d_r: the readings (2 runs × 2 controls) that B rejects and A does not, minus the reverse. - The one-sided p-value is P(Σ ε_r d_r ≥ Σ d_r) over independent equiprobable signs, computed exactly by convolution. Each replicate is one cluster because its four readings share the covariates, noise columns and RRPP seed. - The predeclared delta contrasts are: - C1: oracle_noise5 vs oracle_noise0 - C2: z_noise5 vs z_noise0 - C3: z_noise0 vs oracle_noise0 - C4: z_noise5_corrected vs z_noise0_corrected

    Smoke (design D10; smoke-*, R = 20 × 99 permutations; the design.md "Smoke and shard plan" record): - 40 / 40 units per parent with 0 failures, and indicative verdicts unresolved / residualized_basis; - a separate R = 4 × 999 run reproduced 24 + 24 p-values per parent with 0 mismatches; - one unit took 20.5 s on a workstation core (1.11× a parent unit). On ing the mean unit took 31 s (δ = 0.1, n2 and n4) and 42 s (δ = 0.05, n2).

    5. What the arms show

    The case study's delta excess is residual-df shrinkage. The mechanism the proposal named predicts three things, and the run shows all three: - The uncorrected rate rises with the reduced model's column count in both families. - With z: 0.0545, 0.0595, 0.066 at p_red 5, 7, 10. - Under the oracle: 0.0525, 0.0545, 0.060 at p_red 4, 6, 9. - The proposal's normal-tail estimate was 0.067 at six columns and 0.056 at none. - The corrected rate is flat and at or below α. - With z: 0.049, 0.048, 0.0545. - Under the oracle: 0.048, 0.0475, 0.0505. - No corrected arm exceeds the bound on delta in any reading; z_noise5_corrected's largest is 30 / 500. - angle is not inflated. The z arms read 0.042–0.050.

    C2 is significant at 0.015. C1 points the same way but stops at 0.065 (net 15 against C2's 23). C4 keeps a residual net of 11 (p = 0.156), so the uniform κ removes most, though not visibly all, of the column trend. C3 is flat (p = 0.39, net 4), so estimating a covariate confounded with the group × stage cells costs no delta size here.

    The C2 discordance comes from the translation control: 21 readings against 0, while the anchor readings balance at 20 against 18. That fits a null narrowed by the reduced model's columns, which a constant location offset in group B leaves otherwise untouched. The parent's single excess (translation delta at δ = 0.1, 36 / 500) belongs to this pattern.

    The correction costs some angle and shape rejections. z_noise5_corrected reads angle 0.042 and shape 0.0465, against 0.0455 and 0.0615 uncorrected. A uniformly widened null is conservative for the two statistics that were not inflated. That is a power question for the follow-up change (design D5 deferred the leverage-adjusted alternative to it).

    The oracle is not a size-α reference for angle. - Removing z's injection exactly from the latent scores, while leaving z out of the model, gives angle 0.0725–0.083 in every reading, corrected or not. Every z arm holds angle. C3 on angle is 23 against 82 in the opposite direction. - The basis is fitted on features that contain z, and only a z column in both models absorbs whatever that basis selected along z. That is a candidate explanation, not a tested one: the smoke probes (design.md, "Smoke and shard plan") found no z-aligned content in Y0's within-cell or interaction pattern on 10–20 units. The mechanism stays open. - The smoke had flagged the oracle rows for delta (5–7 / 20). At R = 500 the oracle's delta is 0.0525–0.060, so that was noise at R = 20. The angle inflation is real. - The delta verdict reads only paired contrasts, and C1 is within the oracle family. Neither depends on the oracle being size-α. Only C3's absolute meaning does, and C3 is flat on delta.

    The stage latent adjustment fails on angle at rank 2, not through its rank tail. - With the rank forced to 2 and no cross-validation, which removes both the rank tail and the per-training-set nuisance coefficients, anchor angle still reads 0.084 / 0.082. - Under CV, its rank-2 units reject angle at 0.102 / 0.089. The parent's latent_stage angle excess is therefore a property of the residualized basis. - shape behaves differently: its excess came with the CV rank's tail, and at rank 2 it holds (0.068 / 0.064). - Floor 1 changes little: anchor angle 0.082 / 0.080 and shape 0.082 / 0.068, with 11–12 % of units at rank 1.

    7. Limitations and scope

    The parent check's four limitations carry over unchanged:

    • Astrocyte primary cohort sizes only. [[11, 10, 28], [9, 10, 12]] with n = 80. The microglia cohort (n = 76) and the other cohorts' tables were not run. The shrinkage scales with p_red / n, so a cohort with fewer donors at the same covariate set is expected to be more liberal, not less.
    • Additive-linear covariate effects. z enters the features additively along one fixed direction and the trajectory model linearly.
    • One confounded covariate beside five independent ones. The five noise covariates are i.i.d. normals, uncorrelated with one another and with the design, unlike the case study's age, education, PMI, RIN and library sizes. The column-count mechanism does not depend on their joint distribution, but its size at the real covariates is inferred.
    • Structural-analogue blocks. These are InterSIM-calibrated methylation and expression analogues (498 measured features), not the case study's TMM log2-CPM blocks of 5,000 features each.

    And those of the diagnosis itself:

    • latent_stage_rank2 confounds two things. It removes the CV rank's tail and the per-training-set nuisance coefficients together. Under residualized_basis neither is the cause of the angle excess, but the arm cannot say which of them carried the parent's shape excess. The rank table places that excess in the tail.
    • The oracle's absolute rates. The oracle arms are a valid within-family reference for the column contrast C1, not a size-α baseline for angle (§5).
    • No new replicates. The diagnosis reuses the parent's 500 replicates per strength, so the parent's own excess (two rejections over the bound) is explained rather than re-measured. The R = 1,000 replication was not needed under this verdict and stays deferred.
    • The correction is minimal. The uniform κ restores the unbiased residual variance but not the leverage structure, and C4's residual net of 11 (p = 0.156) leaves room for that. The follow-up change measures both corrections' size and power.
    • No package change. Nothing under src/motco/ changed: the correction is computed in the script from the public RRPP, and MOTCO stays at 0.12.0. The case study's pin is untouched.
    • What stays on ing. The 45,000-row diagnosis_pvalues.csv (2.5 MB per run), merged.jsonl and records/ stay there, named with their sha256 in each PROVENANCE.txt. case_study_diagnosis.py merge regenerates every committed table from records/.
