    The ADNI latent plasmode — report (2026-11-18)

    Change: adni-latent-plasmode-capture (MOTCO 0.25.0, no package change). Reads: results/adni-latent-plasmode-2026-11-18/ (adni_latent_plasmode_decision.json; per profile settings.json, preflight.json, capture_table.csv, point_table.csv, accounting.json; cohort/reproduction.json; calibration/calibration_table.csv; PROVENANCE.txt). Parents: the ADNI real-noise anchor (its point table, bound by sha256) and ADNI gain and specificity (its plants cell table). Concept page: measurement visibility.

    How much of a real stage program production's fitted span sees is not established (roadmap open item 2). The only capture of a weak program is simulated, 0.10 at SEA-AD's stage signal (item 14). Item 22 read the share of a held-out configuration inside the span fitted on 750 ADNI participants, 0.536, but that configuration is mostly estimation noise. This change estimated the capture of the program itself from two independent held-out halves: with m1, m2 the halves' centred covariate-adjusted stage configurations and Q a space's basis fitted on a third, disjoint sample, tr(m1·Q·Qᵀ·m2ᵀ) and tr(m1·m2ᵀ) are unbiased for the program's energy inside the space and in total. A calibration on synthetic cohorts with the truth known gated the estimator before any reading.

    In one paragraph. The verdict is gate_failed, so nothing is read.

    • The run is clean. 2,000 cohort units and 7,200 calibration units completed with none failed or inconsistent. Both pre-flights passed, and both reproductions hold exactly.
    • The gate fails on bias alone. pls_cv's total capture misses the ±0.05 limit at five of eight strength × point cells. At strength 1 the bias is small and negative (−0.035 to −0.077). At 0.67 it reads −0.45 to +0.11, but there the 24-cohort mean has a standard error of 0.12 to 0.37, and an exactly unbiased estimator would have passed all four points with probability about 0.001.
    • It would not have resolved either. The program is not present at 80 participants under the rule (energy 0.779 against a threshold of 1.015), and no point's interval is narrower than ±1.13 against the limit ±0.15.
    • The consequence fixed with the rule. A diagnosis of the estimator comes before any reading. No real-noise capture is stated, the simulated 0.10 stays the only capture of a weak program, and item 22's held-out readings stay mixtures of program and noise.

    1. Configuration and provenance

    • Export. adni_sexdiff/results/motco_input on ing, read in place through ADNI_CONTRACT and never copied: 1,501 participants, the p180 (133) and nmr (165) blocks, Female 237 / 310 / 125 and Male 223 / 448 / 158 at CN / MCI / AD. Its five file hashes are in both settings and were unchanged after the run.
    • Profile cohort. The anchor driver's units, unchanged: 500 per point at 80, 200, 500 and 750 participants subsampled within sex × stage cells. The units at 80, 200 and 500 are the anchor run's, those at 750 the gain run's. Each unit fits the spaces on its sample and splits the remaining participants, within stage, into two halves 20 times (base seed 2041). The halves hold about 710, 650, 500 and 375 participants.
    • Spaces. pls_cv (designated: the case study's configuration, cross-validated on the unit, rank floor 2), the predictive subspace of the same model, the fit forced at ranks 2, 8 and 19, and a PCA of the sample at the unit's CV rank. Every fit is one analyze_trajectories call with permutations=0, so no RRPP draw ran.
    • Estimate. Per point, space and reading, the sum over units of the numerator over the sum of the denominator, never clipped (design D4). Readings: the total capture, the net-displacement and interior contrasts, the capture of the halves' difference (pure estimation noise), and item 22's two naive captures.
    • Profile calibration. 24 Gaussian cohorts per strength from the full-cohort fit features ~ stage + covariates and its residual covariance, carrying the cohort's own stage configuration at strengths 0, 0.67 and 1, with 25 units per cohort and point through the cohort profile's pipeline. Strength 0.67 extrapolates item 22's split-half reliability; the pre-flight measured the full cohort's own at 0.663.
    • Code and hosts. Revision 5cf3b20 from a clean detached worktree on ing, Python 3.11.16, numpy 2.3.5, scipy 1.16.3, scikit-learn 1.8.0, pandas 2.3.3, BLAS pinned to one thread. Cohort 32 workers on n3, calibration 64 on n2, decide after both. Launch commands and job times are in PROVENANCE.txt.

    3. The decision

    Rule version 1, fixed before any unit ran (design D8); not indicative (500 units per cohort point, 24 cohorts and 25 units in the calibration).

    step condition result
    gate: units failed + inconsistent ≤ 1 % per point holds (0 everywhere)
    gate: reproductions both hold holds
    gate: bias |bias| of pls_cv total ≤ 0.05 at strengths 0.67 and 1, every point fails at 5 of 8 cells
    program present n80 energy > 2 × its SD at strength 0 not present: 0.779 against 1.015
    resolved pls_cv total half-width ≤ 0.15 no point: 2.79 / 3.59 / 2.22 / 1.13

    Verdict: gate_failed. Under it nothing is read: no capture is stated, no contrast is graded and no follow-up with plants is licensed. The steps after the gate are recorded in the decision file and quoted here only to show that the gate was not the only obstacle.

    4. Why the gate failed

    pls_cv's total capture in the calibration, mean over 24 cohorts. The standard error is the cohort SD over √24; the last column is the chance that an exactly unbiased estimator with that standard error lands within ±0.05.

    • At strength 1 the bias is real and small. It is negative at every point, about 1.2 to 2.5 standard errors, and the PCA and the forced ranks share the sign at every point (−0.067 to −0.110 and −0.030 to −0.086); the predictive subspace does not (−0.034 to +0.030). Numerator and denominator are unbiased given the fit, so a ratio of sums whose denominator is noisy is the expected source (design, Risks); that is a reading for the diagnosis, not a finding of this run.
    • At strength 0.67 the bias cannot be measured to ±0.05. The cohort-level SD is 0.57 to 1.79, so the 24-cohort mean has a standard error of 0.12 to 0.37, and the readings there lie within 1.3 standard errors of zero. Under exact unbiasedness the four points would all pass with probability 0.14 × 0.11 × 0.18 × 0.34 ≈ 0.001 (normal approximation). The rule fixed the limit before the spread was known, and the spread made the limit unattainable at this calibration size.
    • ADNI's program is weaker than strength 0.67. The cohort's own cross-product energy is 0.70 to 0.78 per point, against 0.92 to 0.98 in the calibration at 0.67 and 1.90 to 2.01 at 1: about strength 0.6 in energy. The cohort sits where the estimator is noisiest.

    5. Why it would not have resolved

    • The spread is a cohort's, and one cohort is all ADNI has. At 80 participants every unit's halves come from a remainder of 1,421 of the 1,501 participants. Averaging 500 units and 20 splits removes the noise of the fit and of the split, but not that of the remainder's own stage means, which is one draw per cohort. That is the spread the calibration measures (design D7), and the interval uses it.
    • The intervals. pls_cv's total capture reads 0.119, 0.221, 0.366 and 0.477 at the four points, each ± 2.79, 3.59, 2.22 and 1.13. Every space is unresolved at every point: the narrowest interval is the predictive subspace's at 500 participants, ±0.52.
    • Even at strength 1 twice the cohort SD is 0.23 to 0.33, above the limit 0.15.
    • Program presence. At strength 0 the total energy's SD across cohorts is 0.47 to 0.53, so presence needs about 1.0. The cohort reads 0.78 at 80 participants; at strength 0.67 the calibration's own cohorts exceed 1.015 about half the time (normal approximation). Neither contrast is present at any point: the net displacement's energy is 0.38 to 0.45 against a null SD of 0.47 to 0.50, the interior's 0.32 to 0.33 against 0.23 to 0.27.

    7. What follows

    • A diagnosis of the estimator comes before any reading (the rule's consequence). It needs no new unit: the calibration's per-unit records are kept on ing.
      • Split the calibration's spread at strength 0.67 into its between-cohort and within-cohort parts. The interval used the SD of a 25-unit estimate; if much of it is within cohorts, the 500-unit cohort estimate is more precise than its interval says. If almost all of it is between cohorts, ADNI's one cohort cannot resolve the capture at ±0.15 with this estimator, and the number waits for a larger cohort.
      • Locate the bias at strength 1, for example by a delta-method correction of the ratio of sums on the calibration's records, and check it against the truth.
      • Any rule version 2 is a new change, written before its units run. A bias limit must be one its calibration can measure: at the observed SDs, a standard error of 0.025 at strength 0.67 would need 3,000 to 5,000 cohorts at 80 and 200 participants.
    • Until then. The latent plasmode stays open on ADNI. Item 22's held-out readings stay mixtures, the simulated 0.10 stays the only capture of a weak stage program, and open item 4's candidates cannot use this estimator as a pre-check. ROSMAP has no export.

    8. Limitations

    • The calibration is Gaussian and its program is the cohort's estimate, about half of it noise in direction; its true captures are not ADNI's (design, Risks). The bias it measured belongs to the estimator at ADNI's design, not to ADNI's program.
    • Strength 0.67 was a setting. The cohort's own energy puts it nearer 0.6, below the calibration's interval strength.
    • One cohort. Every descriptive reading in §6 is one cohort's, with dependent units.
    • Section 4's probabilities use a normal approximation of the 24-cohort mean with the observed SDs; they describe the rule's attainability and are not part of the decision.
