    Net-displacement Type I study — 2026-10-13

    Paper-grade, null-only re-measurement of the net-displacement orientation estimator (openspec/changes/archive/2026-10-13-net-displacement-type-i-study/). It settles the Type I question that the orientation measurement probe left open. It runs no power cell, makes no power claim, and changes no production default. Every rate below is read from results/net-displacement-type-i-2026-10-13/{phase6,phase5}/report/ (anchor_decision.csv/.json, design_point_operating.csv, type_i_table.csv, acceptance_report.csv) and results/net-displacement-type-i-2026-10-13/follow_up_decision.json. Rates are rejection rates at p < 0.05 over 500 replicates × 999 permutations (Monte Carlo SE about 0.0097 at 0.05).

    Runs: results/net-displacement-type-i-2026-10-13/phase6/ (SLURM array 917290) and …/phase5/ (array 917291) on ing · Code: 9089729 · Anchor decision: size_holds at every column at both designs · Follow-up: paper_grade_power_comparison.

    In one paragraph. The probe found the net-displacement anchor angle rate at 0.08–0.10 in every space at both designs, against 0.01–0.05 under PC1, over 100 replicates. At 500 replicates × 999 permutations that excess does not reproduce. Every net-displacement column holds the predeclared bound 0.05 + 2·√(0.05·0.95/500) = 0.0695 on delta, angle and shape, at both designs. The lead arm, pls_cv × net_displacement, has anchor angle 0.050 at Phase 6 and 0.050 at Phase 5. Net displacement does move the anchor angle rate from conservative toward nominal at Phase 6 (PC1 0.024, net displacement 0.048–0.058); it is a size change within the bound, not an excess. The predeclared rule therefore selects the paper-grade power comparison of pls_cv × net_displacement against pls_cv × pc1. One caveat carries forward: under net displacement the translation control's angle rate sits near or above the anchor bound in some columns (Phase 6 concat 0.078), so the comparison should hold that control to the same bound (§5).

    The fixed-basis pls_within_group arm is excluded (the probe identified why its anchor inflates). - Designs: - Phase 6 (examples/trajectory_power_study/net_displacement_type_i_phase6.json): three stages, group_stage_sizes = [[11, 10, 28], [9, 10, 12]], methylation + expression, base seed 800, family phase6-small-n. - Phase 5 (net_displacement_type_i_phase5.json): n = 1200, four stages, group_ratio 0.5, all three blocks, base seed 600, family phase5-primary. - Both use p_dmp = 0.1, ρ = 0, joint magnitude and surgery_censoring = error. - Cells (12 per design): per column, the shared zero-effect anchor (none, e = 0) and the translation negative control at e = 1.0, plus the engine's two type_i_baseline cells (independently seeded, measured in the baseline column). - Seeds: every column evaluates the same datasets at each replicate index. evaluation.seed is the constant 3 and is also the RRPP seed, so every column draws the same permutation indices, and the baseline column re-computes the parent studies' records (§4). - Rule: acceptance.anchor_decision (rule version 1): per column and statistic, anchor rate ≤ α + 2·√(α(1 − α)/R) with α = 0.05 and R the available replicates (0.0695 at R = 500). A column is size_holds when all three statistics hold. Advisory; it feeds neither the gate (off) nor the acceptance targets.

    delta and shape do not depend on the estimator: in the two pls_cv columns they are the same records' statistics and their rates are identical, as they must be. The fixed-rank and label-free spaces are conservative on delta and shape at both designs; that is a property of those spaces, not of the estimator, and no rule here reads it.

    Against the probe. The probe's lead-arm anchor angle rates were 0.100 (Phase 6) and 0.080 (Phase 5) over 100 replicates × 199 permutations, on its own seed root (base seed 900), so its datasets are independent of these. Here the rate is 0.050 at both designs. Split into five consecutive blocks of 100 replicates, the lead arm's Phase 6 anchor angle rate ranges from 0.01 to 0.09 (0.06, 0.09, 0.01, 0.04, 0.05), and at Phase 5 from 0.02 to 0.07 (0.07, 0.04, 0.02, 0.05, 0.07); a 100-replicate rate of 0.08–0.10 is within that sampling spread. The probe's excess was Monte Carlo noise at R = 100, which is what the probe report said it could not exclude.

    Net displacement narrows the angle null by a factor of 7–8 in the pls_cv space (Phase 6 84.0° → 12.1°, Phase 5 5.5° → 0.7°) and removes most of its across-replicate dispersion (Phase 6 IQR 115° → 27°), which is the PC1 sign/flip tail the probe described. A narrower null is consistent with size holding: the test calibrates against its own permutation distribution.

    Reproduction. The baseline column shares the parent study's generator seeds and evaluation parameters, including the RRPP seed, so it re-computes the parent's records:

    • Phase 6: all 1,000 baseline records (anchor and translation, replicates 0–499) equal results/phase6-small-n-2026-10-09/merged.jsonl in generator seed, p-values and observed statistics. The two type_i_baseline cells carry the parent's cell ids and reproduce its 1,000 records' p-values exactly.
    • Phase 5: all 1,000 baseline records equal results/phase5-2026-09-10/merged.jsonl in generator seed, selected rank and p-values. Observed statistics are bit-identical in 317 records and differ in the rest by at most 2.8 × 10⁻¹⁰ relative (floating point; the parent ran at b8f7daa), never enough to change a p-value. The type_i_baseline cells are new (magnitude_kind enters their ids).

    The baseline anchor rates therefore equal the parent studies' recorded rates (Phase 6 0.026 / 0.024 / 0.020; Phase 5 0.036 / 0.046 / 0.048), and the five columns are paired measurements of the same 500 datasets per design.

    5. Decision and the next change

    anchor_decision.json, both designs: size_holds at all five columns. follow_up_decision.json (rule version 1, inputs phase6/report/anchor_decision.json sha256 a0af9d20…, phase5/report/anchor_decision.json sha256 89e8e449…):

    • verdict paper_grade_power_comparison: the lead arm pls_cv × net_displacement is size_holds at both designs;
    • localization none at both designs (no net-displacement column exceeds).

    The verdict selects the next change: a paper-grade power comparison of pls_cv × net_displacement against pls_cv × pc1 at both designs, through the study engine (the estimator is now a contracted design axis, so the comparison is one explicit-points config per design over the Phase 5 / Phase 6 power grids). The anchor-excess diagnosis is not needed. Two points to carry into that change:

    1. Hold the translation control to the anchor bound. Under net displacement the translation angle rate is 0.068 for the lead arm at Phase 6 (bound 0.0695) and 0.058–0.078 in the other spaces; Phase 6 concat (0.078) would exceed the bound if it were judged by it. The lead arm passes at both designs, but by one rejection at Phase 6, so the comparison should declare translation angle as a mandatory control rather than leave it descriptive.
    2. concat stays the ceiling reference, not a candidate, and its p-padding question remains open; this study's null-only design cannot answer it.
